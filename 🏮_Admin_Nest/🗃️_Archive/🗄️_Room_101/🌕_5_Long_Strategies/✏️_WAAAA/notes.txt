WAAAA

The official baseline:

Writers Against Autocrats and Assholes

The respectable grant-application expansion:

Writers Against Authoritarianism, Abuse and Autocracy

The broader coalition expansion:

Writers, Artists, Academics and Archivists

And the internal explanation:

The number of As depends upon how badly everyone is behaving.

That last feature is genuinely useful. You can have:

* WAA — local irritation
* WAAA — organised authoritarian conduct
* WAAAA — international writers’ coalition
* WAAAAAAAAA — emergency constitutional conditions

It is visually memorable, culturally adaptable, and inherently refuses the polished NGO habit of pretending the people destroying civic life are merely “stakeholders presenting challenges.”
