## 🌑 BLACK SHUCK CAVALRY

- Chapter 3 chronology audit, because apparently we are now doing forensic Hobbit sleep logistics.
- Frodo, Sam and Pippin have **one ordinary outdoor sleep first**.
- The next day, **post-lunch**, Frodo encounters the first Black Rider properly in the wild.
- In the later chat they realise the same sort of rider had already been sniffing around the Hobbiton/pub information network the night before — Sam's dad and Frodo have effectively both got pieces of the sighting chain.
- After twilight on that same day: **Black Rider again.** This is the proper **Black Shuck Incident™** to us.
- Then the Elves arrive singing.
- Then **Elf Night™** is sleep number two.
- Gandalf remains fucking absent throughout.

- The useful thing about staying at Hobbit-accessible knowledge is that this thing is not yet primarily **A NAZGÛL WITH LORE**.
- It is:
  - black-clad;
  - faceless / visually inaccessible;
  - on a horse;
  - stalking rather than simply travelling;
  - scenting and lowering itself like something hunting deer;
  - unable to quite place the Hobbits;
  - very fucking wrong.
- The horse behaves like a horse.
- **Why is the fucking man scenting us?**
- Hence: Black Shuck grammar, but somebody has apparently supplied Black Shuck with cavalry.
- Not “Nazgûl = Black Shuck”. More: **what does the Black Rider feel like before Tolkien tells us what a Black Rider means?**
- It is much more folk-horror / uncanny-road syntax than the very established cinematic Ringwraith image in my head.

## 💍 THE RING IS BEING A LITTLE BASTARD

- The Rider seems able to detect **something**, but cannot precisely localise Frodo.
- It stalks as though following scent or trace rather than ordinary visual identification.
- This makes it feel less like it is hunting **Frodo the visible Hobbit** and more like it is trying to place something about the **Ring-bearing condition / essence / signal**.
- Meanwhile the Ring is going:

  **PUT ME ON :)**

- Which is spectacularly unhelpful if putting it on makes Frodo more perceptible to whatever system the Rider belongs to.
- Current provisional mechanism:
  - Rider senses;
  - Rider cannot quite place;
  - Rider searches;
  - Frodo becomes frightened;
  - Ring becomes intensely salient;
  - Ring encourages the exact act that may resolve the Rider's localisation problem.
- **The Ring does not need to announce itself if it can get Frodo to do the announcing.**

- This feels less like a haunted object and much more like a **possessed object**.
- A haunting is often still entangled with mortal attachment: death, grievance, place, memory, unfinished business.
- The Ring does not feel sentimental about mortal life at all.
- It feels like:

  **Here is a living being. What can I make it do?**

- Frodo's fear is useful because it supplies leverage.
- His desire not to be seen is useful because it supplies the argument for using the Ring.
- His body is useful because it transports and wears it.
- His agency is useful because it can be recruited.
- **Possession does not necessarily require overriding Frodo's will. It can mean learning how to make Frodo's will useful.**

- Which makes “possess” wonderfully unstable:
  - Frodo possesses the Ring.
  - The Ring increasingly attempts to possess Frodo.
  - Frodo's safest position may currently be: **physically possessing it while refusing to enact possession through use.**

- The Ring is also weirdly resonant with powerful rhetoric / ideas:
  - it does not need to lie about the problem;
  - Frodo genuinely is in danger;
  - it merely makes one particular response feel inevitable;
  - **you have a real problem; I am already in your pocket; why aren't you using me?**
- But it is more than rhetoric because it is also an interface, material object and apparently metaphysical mediator.
- Hybrid bastard.

## 🕯️ ANTI-PRESENCE

- The phrase that keeps surviving contact with the text is **anti-presence**.
- Better to say **Presence** than narrowly “Eucharist” or “Blessed Sacrament”, because the interesting question is larger than a one-to-one sacramental allegory.
- Definitely **not**: Ring = evil communion wafer. Calm down.
- More like the thought experiment:

  **What would material mediation look like if its metaphysical orientation were the absolute inverse of divine Presence?**

- Catholic metaphysical grammar matters here because the claim of Presence is not merely decorative metaphor.
- The point of all Grandfather's Yap™ was precisely: **what do you mean when you say God is present?**
- Transubstantiation matters because Catholic traditions can range in intellectual emphasis, but the claim is substantially more literal than “this bread reminds us of Jesus”.
- A useful Jewish comparison for the *more diffuse / arising-presence* end of the conceptual range is something like divine presence associated with communal prayer/minyan — not because these traditions are identical, but because it helps distinguish **presence as actual metaphysical condition** from mere symbolism.

- The Ring looks like an inverse material mediation:
  - absent dominating power becomes locally operative;
  - the material object provides the point of contact;
  - participation changes the participant;
  - repeated use appears to deepen the relationship;
  - mortal agency becomes available to a power that is not bodily present.
- Presence does not need to annihilate plurality.
- The Ring's **One** increasingly looks like false unity: plurality subordinated to one dominating will.
- Communion versus possession.
- Participation versus instrumentalisation.
- Presence versus **anti-presence**.

- This does not mean Tolkien sat down with a theological worksheet labelled **INVERSE BLESSED SACRAMENT DESIGN SPEC**.
- It may be deliberate thought experiment, inherited metaphysical furniture, or simply what happens when somebody who has thought very fucking seriously about material theology invents a powerful magical object.
- Keep watching.

## 🔎 GHOST HUNTERS WITH CLIPBOARDS

- The historical timing is also interesting because Tolkien grows up after Victorian psychical research has become **A Thing**.
- Ghost hunts are increasingly not just “someone tells a ghost story” but **people trying to investigate paranormal claims systematically**.
- THE GHOST, but with paperwork.
- Questions become:
  - when does it appear?
  - who can perceive it?
  - does distance matter?
  - does an associated object matter?
  - can animals perceive it?
  - does contact change anything?
  - is the phenomenon intelligent?
  - what are the conditions of manifestation?
- Add photography, wireless/radio and the increasingly ordinary reality that invisible signals really can cross space and become perceptible to the right receiver, and the cultural environment gets deliciously weird.

- Chapter 3 can almost be written as a paranormal field report:
  - Rider detects but does not precisely localise.
  - Rider performs abnormal scent/search behaviour.
  - Ring-bearer experiences urge to activate associated object.
  - Object is already known to alter ordinary visibility.
  - Unknown whether activation changes perceptibility to pursuing entity.
  - Elvish singing approaches.
  - Rider withdraws.
  - Causation: **??????????**
  - Recommendation: **FIND THE FUCKING WIZARD.**

- Important evidence boundary: this is useful historical context to investigate, **not yet evidence Tolkien personally sat around doing ghost hunts**.

## ✨ THE ELVES: LUCKY STAR, HOBBIT

- These specific Elves remain approved.
- Not blanket Elf policy. **THESE SPECIFIC ELVES.**
- They are possibly the weirdest travel influencers ever written.

  Frodo: “Is the road dangerous?”

  Gildor: “Perhaps.”

  Frodo: “Should I leave?”

  Gildor: “Yes.”

  Frodo: “Where should I go?”

  Gildor: “I do not give advice.”

  Frodo: **FOR FUCK'S SAKE.**

- But materially they are helpful:
  - their arrival coincides with Black Shuck Cavalry fucking off;
  - they confer privately after hearing what Frodo has encountered;
  - they update their plans;
  - they take the Hobbits with them;
  - they feed them;
  - they provide protection and information-network effects.
- Their knowledge is opaque but their motivations are weirdly transparent.
- Modern-ear neurodiverse coding remains fun as an observation of **social protocol mismatch**, not “Tolkien wrote autistic Elves”.
- They know weird shit.
- They say auspicious star things.
- **LUCKY STAR, HOBBIT.**
- Cool. They're ours.

- The song itself feels **sea-shanty-ish in function/movement**, rather than literally needing to be a historical shanty form.
- These are travelling people singing collectively while moving through the landscape.
- Much less “ethereal soprano floating in cinematic reverb”.
- Much more:

  **WE ARE HERE. WE KNOW WHERE WE ARE. WE ARE TOGETHER. HERE IS A SONG ABOUT THE FUCKING STARS.**

- Rider: isolated, furtive, scenting, stopping, searching.
- Elves: collective, rhythmic, vocal, confidently moving.
- Provisional result: **anti-Black-Shuck properties observed.**
- Not “anti-Ringwraith” yet. We do not know what a Ringwraith fucking is.

## 🎖️ FRODO: OH FUCK, COMMAND IS REAL

- This is where Frodo starts feeling **inhabited**, rather than merely well-constructed.
- Not “Frodo = Tolkien”.
- More: Tolkien appears to know this social/emotional posture from somewhere close enough to write it without much explanatory distance.
- Frodo's ordinary class authority has previously meant things like household arrangements, gardeners, dinner, wine, etc.
- Then danger arrives and suddenly:

  **Oh. Fuck. This man recognises my authority when the decision might get him killed.**

- That is a completely different moral category from ordering which wine is at the table.
- Sam is not merely somebody Frodo can command.
- Sam is somebody who trusts Frodo enough to follow him when obedience may cost him enormously.
- Authority therefore turns into responsibility at exactly the moment it becomes dangerous.
- Very junior-officer-shaped horror:

  **OH SHIT. THEY REALLY WILL FOLLOW ME.**

- Sam makes it worse by not being reluctantly obedient.
- His assessment after two encounters with Unknown Hostile Black Shuck Cavalry is basically:

  **Situation deteriorating. Senior support absent. Very good, Mr Frodo. I remain attached to you.**

- Frodo:

  **…oh fuck. I have a soldier.**

- Useful ongoing FotR question:

  **Which characters feel observed, and which feel inhabited?**

- Current possibility: **Tolkien may inhabit Frodo while remembering Sam.**

## 🌍 WAIT, YOU LOT HAVE TOLKIEN TOO?

- One of the strangest pre-internet British experiences: knowing intellectually that Tolkien was translated and sold elsewhere is not the same as understanding that **other countries had enormous Tolkien fandoms**.
- From inside Britain it could feel like one of the Things We Have.
- Famous, yes.
- International planetary imaginative infrastructure? **……………………you WHAT.**
- The internet introduced British people to International Nerds and apparently we were not prepared.

  “I have devoted fifteen years to Tolkien scholarship.”

  British person: **……………………you WHAT.**

- This applies to everything.
- We experienced a lot of exported culture as **the thing that was already in the cupboard**.
- Then somebody from 5,000 miles away knows more about it than you do and the response is not necessarily pride so much as:

  **WHO TAUGHT YOU ABOUT THAT.**

- Rowling is useful as a comparison because *Harry Potter* also contains loads of British cultural furniture that does not necessarily feel like nationally specific content when you are inside it.
- *Prisoner of Azkaban*: enormous ominous black dog.
- British child: **yeah. bad fucking news. we have those.**
- International reader: possibly encountering the whole grammar as fictional-world lore.
- Cultural proximity can reveal unmarked assumptions.
- Cultural distance can reveal structures that proximity makes invisible.
- Neither gets the whole thing automatically.

- Useful reading method:

  **Before researching what Tolkien “meant”, record what the text activates in a culturally situated reader before the Tolkien explanatory apparatus tells us what everything is.**

## 🧝 ARE THE ELVES WELSH? JEWISH? BYZANTINE? YES? NO? CALM DOWN.

- Tolkien's cultural construction is interesting precisely because it does **not** currently collapse into:
  - Elves = Welsh;
  - Elves = Jews;
  - Elves = Byzantines;
  - Elves = Islamicate world;
  - Hobbits = simple one-to-one English stand-in;
  - etc.
- There are recognisable ingredients and resonances.
- Welsh/Celtic linguistic and aesthetic pieces are obvious enough to keep pinging.
- There are also Jewish resonances that, unusually in English literature, currently feel **weirdly Jewish but nice and non-threatening** rather than activating the standard antisemitic payload.
- From a British historical imagination there are also possible Constantinopolitan / Ottoman / wider Islamic-world echoes in the ancient, multilingual, learned, networked civilisation standing beyond the provincial little society.
- None of these has to become **THE KEY**.

- And this is interesting against a British imperial literary culture that could be absolutely fucking obsessive about categorising human beings.
- The racial/national filing cabinet:

  **THE IRISHMAN IS LIKE THIS. THE JEW DOES THIS. THE ORIENTAL IS THIS TYPE. THE FRENCHMAN NATURALLY DOES THAT.**

- Tolkien is a man who plainly does not lack the capacity to classify things.
- **Christ alive, can he classify things.**
- Which makes it notable that three chapters in we cannot yet confidently point at a fictional people and say **there is the uncomplicated contemporary racial stereotype doing narrative work**.
- That is not the same as declaring Tolkien free of Orientalism, imperial assumptions, philosemitic typing, racism, etc.
- We haven't read the fucking books yet.
- Keep the notebook open, especially when peoples from East/South enter more fully.
- But **absence of easy typing is still data**.

- Likewise the current threatening Other is weirdly anti-ethnographic.
- We cannot properly inspect the Black Riders.
- Faces unavailable.
- Identities unavailable.
- Bodies uncertain.
- Behaviour paranormal.
- They are a **phenomenon before they are a people**.

- Possible Tolkien construction technique:
  - take linguistic material from one place;
  - literary memory from another;
  - metaphysics from somewhere else;
  - lived English social structures;
  - folklore;
  - medieval texts;
  - personal invention;
  - give the resulting people enough internal history that they stop being reducible to the ingredients.
- **Overdetermination rather than stereotype.**
- Not necessarily “better”; not automatically innocent; but structurally different from fantasy ethnic caricature.

## ⛪ ENGLISH AS FUCK, BUT ENGLAND IS NOT THE WHOLE MORAL UNIVERSE

- There is something here about Tolkien's Catholic position that is not simply **Catholic = tolerant**, because lol no.
- More interesting: Catholicism places him thoroughly inside English society while also slightly askew from the Anglican establishment identity structure.
- Still officer.
- Still Oxford.
- Still British gentleman setting permanently stuck on **MAXIMUM ENGLISHMAN**.
- But Catholic belonging carries a built-in transnational frame:
  - local parish;
  - country;
  - wider Church;
  - humanity;
  - God.
- The nation is real and can be loved fiercely without necessarily being the highest possible category of belonging.
- Historically that transnational Catholic allegiance could itself be read in Britain as suspect or disloyal — including through anti-Catholic and anti-Irish prejudice and the old anxiety about Rome.
- Catholic practice can include prayers for monarch/country while making their authority explicitly secondary to God.
- There is a recognisable philosophical texture here:

  **home matters enormously; home is not the whole universe.**

- Tolkien could struggle to be more English.
- The Shire can be beloved without becoming **the criterion for who counts as a person**.
- Hold this as a lens, not a causal proof.

## 🧿 ANTI-FASCISM WITHOUT GIVING BRO A 1954 DEI CERTIFICATE

- The timing makes the refusal of easy human typing particularly interesting because British fascism was not just two cranks in a basement.
- It had moneyed, socially powerful constituencies and existed inside Tolkien's adult political environment.
- Fascism also turns the old racial-classification obsession into explicit political machinery.
- So there is a serious future research question here:

  **What did Tolkien explicitly reject about racial fascism, what imperial assumptions did he retain, and what literary techniques did he use to construct difference without simply reproducing contemporary racial types?**

- Do not jump from textual pattern to “Tolkien consciously designed anti-fascist fantasy ethnography”.
- Research the documentary Tolkien when we get there.
- Let contradictions remain contradictions.
- No sainthood after Chapter 3.

## 🎶 IMPULSIVE POETRY PROBLEM

- Tolkien himself apparently has the behavioural tendency we keep seeing in the Hobbits:

  **EVENT OCCURS → LANGUAGE STARTS RHYMING.**

- There is a wartime / journey-to-war verse thread to pin down properly later rather than handwave.
- The immediate comic observation remains:

  Army: **You are being transported towards one of the worst battles in human history.**

- Decades later his characters cannot walk through three hedgerows without producing verse.
- This may be another **inhabited behaviour** rather than Fantasy Author Adds Song Because Fantasy.
- Research exact poem / date / journey before making the historical claim precise.
