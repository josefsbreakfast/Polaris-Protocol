The “can I actually say that?” file. Fast verification of recurring claims, with something like:

Claim → status → precise formulation → source → date checked → caveat if materially necessary.

Perfect for things such as: what section 145 does; whether it is in force; what section 66 covers; what the Law Commission recommended; when Macdonald reported; what government promised; what the VAWG strategy says about online misogyny; petition signatures/dates.

This should be deliberately boring and excellent.
