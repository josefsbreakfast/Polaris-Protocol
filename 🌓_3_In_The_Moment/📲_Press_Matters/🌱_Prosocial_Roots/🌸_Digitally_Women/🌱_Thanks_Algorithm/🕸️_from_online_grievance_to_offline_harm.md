### `🕸️_from_online_grievance_to_offline_harm.md`

**The internet does not stop at the edge of the screen.** Examine what is actually known about relationships between digitally reinforced misogyny/grievance and harassment, stalking, coercive behaviour, sexual abuse and other forms of violence. Concentrate on mechanisms, escalation and behavioural pathways rather than asserting a simplistic content→crime causal line. Cross-link to `💔_Violence_IRL/`. This is also where the artificial separation between “online harms” and embodied safeguarding problems starts falling apart.
