`🏘️_the_camden_crew_and_vawg.md`

**Working subtitle:** *Four governments, fifteen years of strategies, and the recurring discovery that violence against women and girls is a problem.*

The node could run a **2010 → 2026 policy lineage**, but weight the second half heavily toward **post-2024 government**. The useful question isn't merely “what VAWG policy exists?” It's **what changed, what survived a change of government, what got renamed/reframed, what was actually implemented, and where does online misogyny now sit inside that machinery?**

That gives us a very useful spine:

* **2010–15:** Coalition VAWG strategy and the architecture inherited/created in that period.
* **2015–24:** successive Conservative strategies, Domestic Abuse Act era, Online Safety Act, safeguarding/radicalisation developments, policing reviews and the accumulating recommendations.
* **2024 onward:** Labour's stated ambition to halve VAWG in a decade; Home Office/education/policing/online-safety strands; what “prevention” actually means operationally.
* **2025–26:** how misogyny, manosphere content, young men's radicalisation and technology are being incorporated—or awkwardly translated—into established government vocabularies.
* **September 2026:** Petition 746640 as a useful pressure test: Parliament is being asked something broader than “create another offence”. It's being asked about recognition, prevention, online radicalisation, institutional capability, education and survivor support simultaneously.
* **The Camden Crew problem:** the political actors around this have institutional histories and policy histories of their own. Treating the current debate as though everybody materialised in 2026 would throw away useful context.

And then the killer crosslink is **♻️ Re: Recommendations**.

Because the VAWG chronology lets us ask:

> **Which things being proposed in 2026 are genuinely new, and which are the latest vocabulary for safeguards, prevention duties, information-sharing or institutional-learning problems government has already been told about?**

That also gives `🌸_Digitally_Women` a nice structural distinction: **📖 Previously On The Internet** explains how *women and the internet got here*; **🏘️ The Camden Crew and VAWG** explains how *British government policy got here*. Then **🧾 Petition GC** is where those histories collide in Parliament.
