Crucially, it establishes our principle:

Don’t force one vocabulary to do every job.

Government, criminal law, researchers, journalists, platforms, feminist movements and people experiencing harm may describe overlapping portions of the same ecology differently.

This should cross-link heavily to Guide to Girl Internet and Guide to Online Feminisms, because neither cultural literacy nor feminist political vocabulary should be forced into a government-policy glossary.
