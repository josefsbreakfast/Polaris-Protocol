Job: Restore feminist technological imagination to the history.

Long before today’s debates about recommender systems and Online Safety Act duties, feminist thinkers and digital communities were already asking what networked technologies might do to:

* sex;
* gender;
* bodies;
* identity;
* labour;
* hierarchy;
* reproduction;
* community;
* knowledge;
* power.

This is the intellectual/history node for cyberfeminism and its surrounding digital cultures.

It stops our history becoming:

technology happened → women were harmed → government regulation followed.

Women were also experimenting with what digital technologies might make possible.

Question answered: Before we started asking how to make platforms less harmful, what did feminists imagine technology could actually become?
