Job: Explain why this history exists inside 🌸 Digitally Women and route readers through it.

This should be short. Its central warning is that several histories are easily collapsed into one:

* women using and making digital culture;
* feminist organising through digital technologies;
* feminist technological thought;
* online misogyny and antifeminist countermobilisation;
* platformisation and algorithmic amplification.

They interact, but they are not one history.

It should also explain that we are deliberately not reproducing the full political history here. 👾_Debugging_The_Men/ already contains much of the stronger offline chronology of women’s movements, revelations, institutional responses and backlash. Cross-link it.
