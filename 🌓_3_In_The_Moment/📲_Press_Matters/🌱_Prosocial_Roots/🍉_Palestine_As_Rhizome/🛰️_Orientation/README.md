# 🛰️ Orientation  
**First created:** 2026-07-17 | **Last updated:** 2026-07-17  
*Conceptual foundations for tracing Palestine’s many routes through public life without allowing the model, its comparisons, or its domestic consequences to displace Palestine itself.*

---

## 🛰️ Purpose

This cluster establishes the conceptual foundations of **🍉 Palestine As Rhizome**.

The wider pack asks why Palestine remains a durable site of:

- protest;
- political identification;
- legal dispute;
- professional concern;
- institutional conflict;
- public grief;
- democratic scrutiny;
- and international solidarity.

It uses the rhizome as a model for tracing the many routes through which an unresolved Palestinian struggle enters public life.

But a connected model requires discipline.

Without it, the work may:

- mistake decentralisation for incoherence;
- mistake connection for equivalence;
- turn Palestinians into symbols for other people’s politics;
- allow domestic controversies to replace Palestinian conditions;
- reproduce contested labels as neutral fact;
- or treat every connected issue as though it were secretly the same issue.

This orientation cluster therefore establishes four principles:

1. the rhizome is useful because Palestinian solidarity has multiple roots, routes, and points of emergence;
2. Palestine and Palestinians remain central to every route being traced;
3. connection does not make distinct histories, harms, or political situations equivalent;
4. and the whole pack requires shared editorial guardrails governing evidence, language, representation, comparison, and harm.

These principles should be read before the wider branches are used for reporting, analysis, or casework.

---

## 🌱 The Model

The rhizome model helps explain political persistence without searching for:

- one central command;
- one representative organisation;
- one fixed ideology;
- one typical marcher;
- one route into solidarity;
- or one visible structure that can be removed to end the movement.

People may arrive at Palestinian solidarity through:

- Palestinian identity and family;
- humanitarian concern;
- international law;
- anti-war politics;
- religious commitment;
- Jewish dissent;
- anti-racism;
- professional ethics;
- trade unionism;
- student politics;
- arms exports;
- public procurement;
- policing;
- protest rights;
- media credibility;
- or democratic consistency.

These routes intersect.

They do not become identical.

A visible expression may be suppressed while the unresolved issue continues through another institutional, legal, professional, or relational route.

The rhizome model provides a way to map that persistence.

It does not suggest that everything is Palestine.

It asks where the material route to Palestine can actually be shown.

---

## 🇵🇸 The Centre

The routes are not the centre.

Palestine is.

Palestinian people are not an analytical device through which other societies examine:

- their own democratic health;
- institutional contradictions;
- communal identities;
- political morality;
- professional ethics;
- or media failures.

Those questions belong in this pack where they are materially connected to Palestinian conditions.

They must not displace:

- Palestinian life;
- land;
- history;
- testimony;
- material survival;
- political plurality;
- rights;
- and collective agency.

The rhizome describes how the issue travels.

It must not become a method for travelling away from Palestine.

---

## 🕸️ The Connections

Rhizomatic analysis requires connection.

The work may trace:

- institutional connections;
- financial connections;
- legal connections;
- political connections;
- historical connections;
- tactical connections;
- conceptual connections;
- and relationships of solidarity.

Each type of connection supports a different claim.

A university investment may create a financial and institutional route.

A court decision may create a legal route.

A shared campaign may create a relational route.

A historical comparison may reveal a transmitted institution or political technique.

None of these connections automatically establishes that the situations at either end are identical.

A route should therefore be:

- identified;
- evidenced;
- limited;
- and described with the differences preserved.

Connection makes the relationship visible.

Equivalence erases the ground on either side.

## 🌀 The Discipline

The pack should remain capable of naming:

- mass harm;
- displacement;
- state violence;
- attacks on civilians;
- antisemitism;
- anti-Palestinian racism;
- Islamophobia;
- movement failure;
- political manipulation;
- institutional inconsistency;
- protest misconduct;
- and abusive uses of security language

without turning any population into a permanent suspect class.

This requires editorial discipline.

Across the pack:

- Palestinian people and material conditions should remain visible;
- claims, evidence, inference, and findings should be distinguished;
- time-sensitive facts should be dated;
- contested language should be attributed;
- protest should be represented proportionately;
- populations, movements, networks, organisations, and events should not be conflated;
- antisemitism and anti-Palestinian racism should be assessed seriously and specifically;
- comparison should preserve historical difference;
- security claims should be examined rather than assumed;
- images should not consume suffering;
- and institutional responses should be studied as possible causes of further harm.

The aim is not neutral distance from consequence.

It is sufficient accuracy to describe consequence without reproducing it.

## 🧭 Core Distinctions

The orientation cluster maintains several distinctions throughout the pack.

### Route Is Not Origin

An issue may provide a route into Palestinian solidarity without being the origin of the Palestinian struggle.

### Connection Is Not Equivalence

Two cases may share an institution, mechanism, law, or political concern without becoming interchangeable.

### Solidarity Is Not Ownership

Recognising a relationship does not confer the right to redefine another population’s history or political demands.

### Plurality Is Not Incoherence

A movement may contain serious disagreement while retaining a recognisable minimum shared centre.

### Decentralisation Is Not Absence Of Structure

Networks may operate without one command centre while still containing leadership, influence, funding, hierarchy, and accountability.

### Humanitarian Recognition Is Not Political Resolution

Relief can be urgently necessary without settling questions of rights, land, accountability, representation, or political status.

### Scrutiny Is Not Collective Liability

Specific conduct should receive specific scrutiny.

It should not become permanent suspicion directed at an entire population.

### Removal Is Not Resolution

A visible action may be suppressed while the underlying grievance, institutional connection, or political route remains active.

## ❓ Orientation Questions

Before entering the wider pack, ask:

- What Palestinian condition or political claim is at the centre?
- Through which route did this wider issue become connected?
- Is that route material, institutional, legal, political, professional, historical, tactical, conceptual, or relational?
- What evidence establishes the connection?
- Which differences must remain visible?
- Are Palestinians present as people and political actors?
- Has a domestic institution become the main character?
- Are movement, organisation, network, population, and event being distinguished?
- Is a contested label being presented as neutral fact?
- Is one incident being made representative of a whole movement?
- Has analogy replaced evidence?
- Is any population being treated as a permanent suspect class?
- What new roots may have been generated by the institutional response?
- Does the analysis clarify Palestine, or merely use Palestine to explain something else?

## 🌿 Working Principle

Expand without displacement.

Connect without collapse.

Scrutinise without collective suspicion.

Report the routes without losing the people and conditions from which they grow.
