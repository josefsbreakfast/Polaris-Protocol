Working principle for all ten mega-nodes

Each should contain:

* a clear orientation;
* one central institutional or political-economy argument;
* factual material where relevant;
* explicit evidence boundaries;
* careful separation of:
  * documented fact;
  * reported claim;
  * participant interpretation;
  * analytical inference;
  * plausible but unresolved connection;
* clear distinction between capability, access, implementation, use, attribution and causation;
* internal headings from the old child-node inventory;
* a compact finding;
* cross-references;
* no branching until a section becomes independently useful, evidentially distinct or too large to navigate.
