Claim (Facebook), rec to diaspora accts:  

“India.com: After the resignation of former Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan over the NEET-UG paper leak row, the CJP-led protest has been hit by fresh controversy.
Influencer Meghnad alleged that CJP leaders abandoned hungry protesters at Delhi's Jantar Mantar and attended a party instead. The claims surfaced after a viral video of CJP leader Saurav Das celebrating sparked criticism online, adding to the political storm surrounding the protest movement.
#cjpprotest #cjpleaders #protest #party #starved #SauravDas #AshutoshRanka #CockroachJanataParty”

Verdict: partly accurate, but it collapses an allegation into a reported fact

The screenshot gets the broad chronology right:

* Dharmendra Pradhan resigned from the Union Council of Ministers on 25 July 2026. The President’s office formally confirmed the resignation and assigned the education portfolio to Pralhad Joshi.  
* CJP then ended its Jantar Mantar agitation after the resignation and further government assurances.  
* Videos do show CJP figures, including Saurav Das, dancing at a gathering labelled a “resignation party.” Das has defended the celebration publicly.  
* Meghnad S did publicly allege that he and others had spent two days helping “abandoned and starving” volunteers get home. Other reporting confirms that offers of money and transport assistance were still being made nearly 48 hours after the protest ended.  

But the post’s wording is too definite.

“CJP leaders abandoned hungry protesters … and attended a party instead”

That is Meghnad’s allegation, not an independently established finding. I have not found independent reporting proving:

* that the leadership deliberately abandoned the volunteers;
* that CJP remained formally responsible for feeding or transporting everyone after calling off the protest;
* that leaders went straight from abandoning protesters to the party;
* or that the party caused the volunteers to be hungry.

The party footage itself appears genuine enough that CJP figures are openly defending it, but some reports still describe the exact venue and context as unverified or purported.  

There is also a slightly misleading temporal implication. The gathering was presented as a victory/resignation celebration after the protest had been called off, not necessarily as leaders partying while the official sit-in remained active. The criticism is that they celebrated while some volunteers were apparently still stranded and needing food or fares—not simply that political organisers celebrated a major concession.  

A more defensible version would be:

After Dharmendra Pradhan’s resignation and CJP’s decision to end its Jantar Mantar protest, videos emerged of CJP leaders attending a “resignation party.” Political commentator Meghnad S alleged that some volunteers had meanwhile been left without adequate food or money to travel home. Those allegations have prompted criticism, but have not yet been independently substantiated in full.

One smaller correction: the screenshot calls Meghnad merely an “influencer.” That is not false, but “political commentator and YouTuber who had supported and attended the protest” gives readers materially better context.  

So: not fabricated, but framed for maximum scandal and inadequate about what is verified versus alleged.
