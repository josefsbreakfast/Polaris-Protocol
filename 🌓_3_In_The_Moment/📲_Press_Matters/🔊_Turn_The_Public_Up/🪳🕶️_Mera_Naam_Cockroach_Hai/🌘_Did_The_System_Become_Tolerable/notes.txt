Working principle for all ten mega-nodes

Each should contain:

* a clear orientation;
* one central assessment question;
* factual material where relevant;
* exact separation between announcement and implementation;
* explicit evidence boundaries;
* careful distinction between:
  * documented concession;
  * reported concession;
  * promised action;
  * implemented action;
  * independently verified outcome;
  * participant interpretation;
  * analytical inference;
* attention to the effects on different groups rather than only the movement’s visible leadership;
* a compact finding;
* cross-references;
* no branching until a section becomes independently useful, evidentially distinct or too large to navigate.

Key assessment test across the whole subsection:

```text
Did the response:

1. acknowledge the harm?
2. remove coercion?
3. redistribute material cost?
4. change institutional authority?
5. reduce recurrence?
6. preserve enforceability?
7. leave the public able to re-enter if promises failed?
```
