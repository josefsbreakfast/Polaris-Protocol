# 🍊 That Orange Plague

## 🛰️ Orientation

- Investigate the political phenomenon associated with Donald Trump and its effects on democratic institutions, public discourse and immigration enforcement.
- Examine the relationship between:
  - Personalised political authority.
  - Populist communication.
  - Nationalist identity politics.
  - Anti-immigration rhetoric.
  - Executive power.
  - Institutional accountability.
  - International political responses.
  - Political satire and visual protest.
- Investigate why American domestic political developments attract attention from democratic institutions abroad.
- Examine the European Parliament artwork as a potential case study in political symbolism and international criticism.
- Distinguish criticism of Donald Trump personally from analysis of the political institutions, organisations and incentives surrounding him.
- Avoid treating Trumpism as a sufficient explanation for every historical problem examined in this cluster.
- Central question: How does a political movement organised around a highly visible leader interact with institutional systems that existed before him and may continue after him?
