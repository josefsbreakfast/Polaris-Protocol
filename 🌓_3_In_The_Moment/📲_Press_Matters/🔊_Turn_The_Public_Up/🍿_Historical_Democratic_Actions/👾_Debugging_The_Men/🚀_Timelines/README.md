# 🚀 Timelines

**First created:** 2026-09-09 | **Last updated:** 2026-09-09

*The historical spine of Debugging The Men: how fragmented knowledge became comparable, how disclosure changed public and institutional conditions, how backlash developed, and what trajectories may follow.*

---

## 🛰️ Orientation

These nodes are not mutually exclusive date boxes.

They are different lenses on a changing information environment. Some periods overlap deliberately because the same event can be the end of one process and the beginning of another.

The progression is:

> **fragmented knowledge → public mobilisation and disclosure → structural interpretation → conditional legitimacy → branching futures**

The timelines do not assume one conspiracy, one public, one backlash, or one inevitable direction of travel.

---

## 🚀 The Sequence

### 🩸 The Long Fuse — 2010–2017

How survivor testimony, institutional failures, online conflict, lateral comparison, and changing information environments lowered the threshold for the 2017 disclosure cascade.

### 🔥 Pussyhat Backlash — 2017–2018

A close-up on the rupture: the Women's March, the pussyhat as a replicable political symbol, MeToo, collective disclosure, and the heterogeneous backlash that followed.

### 🩻 The Structural Revelation — 2018–2022

How the post-MeToo environment encouraged wider structural questions about institutions, facilitation, reputation, vulnerability, accountability, and repeated failure.

### 🌪️ The Legitimacy Crisis — 2023–2025

How accumulated scandals interacted with austerity, public-service strain, political instability, fragmented media, and declining confidence in institutional reassurance.

### 🔮 The Post-Legitimacy Landscape — 2026–2030

Scenario work rather than prophecy: plausible trajectories if credibility becomes increasingly conditional, publics build alternative information systems, and institutions respond through repair, adaptation, containment, or coercion.

---

## 🧭 Reading Rule

Later knowledge should not automatically be backdated into earlier periods.

A behaviour can exist before it is widely named. A pattern can be visible to targets before institutions recognise it. A later political actor can exploit a grievance without having created it.

The timeline therefore keeps separate:

> **emergence → recognition → aggregation → interpretation → response → adaptation**
