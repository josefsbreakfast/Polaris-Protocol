# 🤝_after_russia_stops.md

- Purpose:
  - address the strategic question that begins after territorial aggression is contained or ends;
  - distinguish resisting Russia from designing a workable future relationship with Russia.

- Core proposition:
  > Defeating a Russian policy is not the same task as designing a world in which Russia subsequently exists.

- Baseline:
  - Ukraine's sovereignty answers:
    - where Russia must stop.
  - it does not alone answer:
    - what Europe should do with Russia afterwards.

- Russia remains:
  - nuclear-armed;
  - geographically enormous;
  - economically relevant;
  - an Arctic power;
  - a European and Asian power;
  - a permanent UNSC member.

- Possible future Russias:
  - Putin remains;
  - leadership transition;
  - more authoritarian government;
  - less authoritarian government;
  - weaker state;
  - richer state;
  - hostile state;
  - gradually normalising state.

- Every version still requires:
  - diplomacy;
  - borders;
  - communications;
  - risk management;
  - strategic coexistence.

- Possible long-term architecture:
  - diplomatic representation;
  - incident hotlines;
  - nuclear-risk reduction;
  - arms control;
  - maritime deconfliction;
  - airspace protocols;
  - Black Sea arrangements;
  - Arctic governance;
  - intelligence liaison;
  - migration arrangements;
  - trade in permitted sectors;
  - eventual conditional economic normalisation.

- Key questions:
  - what minimum relationship is necessary even under continued hostility?
  - what forms of cooperation are possible without normalising aggression?
  - what sanctions architecture has a defined exit path?
  - what behaviour earns relaxation?
  - what behaviour triggers reimposition?

- Historical / status connection:
  - if Russian signalling contains:
    - recognition;
    - prestige;
    - great-power status,
  - could some of those needs be satisfied at low strategic cost while preserving Ukrainian sovereignty?

- Important:
  - recognition ≠ territorial concession.
  - diplomacy ≠ trust.
  - coexistence ≠ friendship.
  - negotiation ≠ capitulation.

- Strong working line:
  > "Fuck off back behind your borders" is a defensible war aim. It is not a complete post-war foreign policy.
