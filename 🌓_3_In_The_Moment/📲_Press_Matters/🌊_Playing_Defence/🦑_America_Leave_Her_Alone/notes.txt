# 🦑 America Leave Her Alone — basic cluster plan

- Use a relational metaphor where useful: two countries in a very long, unusually intimate relationship with shared assets, obligations, habits, resentments, dependencies and history.
- Treat the UK and US as sovereign actors with overlapping interests, not as one strategic unit.
- Ask what each side thinks it contributes, what it thinks it receives, and what it thinks the other side fails to appreciate.
- Separate ordinary disagreement from dependency, leverage, coercion and genuine strategic threat.
- Track emotional and institutional perceptions as well as strictly rational cost-benefit calculations.
- Allow for the possibility that American institutions sincerely feel that allies have under-contributed, even where the evidence produces a more complicated answer.
- Allow for the possibility that Britain has become accustomed to American protection, capability or access in ways it has not adequately priced.
- Treat irritation and exhaustion as strategically relevant even where Britain does not feel directly threatened.
- Ask whether repeated US interventions are producing the intended effect in Britain or merely making British actors want more distance.
- Do not assume a coordinated American plan where fragmented institutions, commercial actors, political signalling or habit explain the evidence better.
- Do not treat current arrangements as permanent.
- Do not treat allied access as ownership.
- Do not treat American interests as automatically overriding British ones.
- Do not treat British sovereignty over every current territory or arrangement as morally incontestable.
- Keep decolonisation, self-determination, local populations and third-country interests visible throughout.
- Use Russia and China as structural comparators where useful without pretending the three states are equivalent.
- Ask what both countries need this relationship to look like over the next 25–50 years.
- Keep the central preventive question visible: **is this the relationship we actually intend to be creating?**
