## 🧭 How To Read This Register

Sources are grouped by the function they perform rather than ranked solely by outlet prestige.

### Primary Or Institutional Source

An affected authority, government department, regulator, law-enforcement body, technical advisory issuer, company, operator, or research body speaking from its own institutional position.

Primary does not mean complete, neutral, or independently demonstrated.

### Technical Or Sector Source

A security researcher, sector publication, specialist newsroom, industry body, or technical synthesis capable of supplying detail not present in general reporting.

### Independent Reporting

A newsroom reporting events, official assessments, interviews, leaks, claims, political statements, or downstream effects.

Its independence must be assessed proposition by proposition. Several articles may still rely on one official, memo, advisory, vendor, or Telegram post.

### Actor Or Claim Route

A source that preserves what an actor said or claimed.

It establishes the existence and wording of the claim where the reporting route is reliable. It does not independently establish authorship, effect, customer, or state direction.

### Context Source

Polling, policy, war-map, legal, economic, political, alliance, or public-confidence evidence used to interpret consequences rather than prove a cyber incident.

### Lead Or Supporting Reference

A source retained because it may support later research, comparison, correction, or exclusion. It should not be promoted silently into a stronger evidentiary role.

## ✅ Verification And Maintenance Status

This register performs four kinds of preservation.

```text
CANONICAL URL
the cleaned public link retained for future use

SOURCE FUNCTION
what the source is capable of supporting

DEPENDENCY NOTE
whether another link repeats the same underlying source

REVIEW LIMIT
what has not been independently checked or cannot be inferred
```

At the 19 August review:

- 102 canonical URLs were preserved;
- 56 domains were represented;
- tracking parameters were removed where they did not identify distinct content;
- duplicate AP and republication routes were preserved where their circulation history matters;
- spreadsheet suffix artefacts such as `,Tier` were removed;
- named but unlinked sources were retained separately;
- source presence was not converted into evidentiary endorsement;
- new incident, attribution, mechanism and comparative sources were separated by function;
- and actor-derived scale or access claims were kept distinct from affected-authority findings.

Future updates should:

1. append genuinely new sources rather than replace preservation-sensitive evidence lists;
2. add the source beside the proposition it supports;
3. record when several links share one underlying source;
4. label actor statements as claims;
5. prefer affected authorities and primary technical material where available;
6. retain independent reporting where it establishes political, intelligence, local, or downstream facts unavailable in primary publications;
7. mark broken, archived, paywalled or inaccessible sources rather than silently deleting them;
8. add correction dates where a source materially changes its account;
9. propagate changed attribution across the timeline, CSV, XLSX and analytical nodes;
10. preserve the README's expanded `Follow the evidence` block during later drafting passes.
