# ⚖️ Law

**First created:** 2026-08-20 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-20  
*Legal factchecking routes for Palestine: identifying the applicable body of law, distinguishing argument from authority, preserving procedural posture, and refusing to turn headlines into judgments.*

---

## 🚨 Scope Note — This Is Not Legal Advice

This branch is for **legal factchecking and legal-source literacy**.

It is not a law firm.

It does not provide personalised legal advice, determine anyone's liability, or replace advice from a qualified lawyer working in the relevant jurisdiction.

The questions here are primarily:

> **What law is being invoked?**

> **What does the relevant legal source actually say?**

> **What has a court, tribunal, prosecutor, government or other body actually done?**

> **What remains allegation, argument, interpretation or unresolved law?**

Where a reader needs advice about what they personally should do, publish, disclose, challenge or litigate, they should obtain proper legal advice.

## 🛰️ Orientation

Israel and Palestine generate an enormous number of legal claims.

Many sound deceptively simple:

- Is the occupation legal?
- Are settlements illegal?
- Is Gaza occupied?
- Is this genocide?
- Is this apartheid?
- Was this strike a war crime?
- Are civilian casualties automatically unlawful?
- What does proportionality mean?
- What is collective punishment?
- Can civilians lose protection?
- Are hostages prisoners of war?
- What is the legal status of detainees?
- What did the ICJ actually rule?
- What does an ICC warrant mean?
- Does recognition create a state?
- What legal effect does a UN resolution have?
- Is BDS protected speech?
- What do anti-BDS laws actually prohibit?
- What obligations do third states have?

These questions do not all belong to the same body of law.

They do not all have binary answers.

And they cannot responsibly be answered by searching for whichever lawyer, government or NGO uses the strongest wording.

This folder exists to make the legal architecture visible.

## ⚖️ The First Question Is: Which Law?

Before answering a legal question, identify the relevant legal framework.

Possible bodies of law include:

- international humanitarian law;
- international criminal law;
- international human-rights law;
- the law of occupation;
- refugee law;
- treaty law;
- the law governing state responsibility;
- domestic constitutional law;
- criminal law;
- administrative law;
- public procurement law;
- sanctions law;
- anti-discrimination law;
- freedom-of-expression law;
- and the procedural law of particular courts and tribunals.

Several may apply at once.

Do not treat **international law** as one giant statute book.

---

## 🧭 Identify The Legal Actor

Legal propositions depend on who is acting.

Ask:

- a state?
- an occupying power?
- an armed group?
- an individual commander?
- a civilian?
- a corporation?
- a public authority?
- an international organisation?
- a third state?
- a court?
- a prosecutor?

Rules applying to states are not automatically rules of individual criminal liability.

Rules governing military operations are not automatically rules governing public procurement.

A finding concerning state responsibility does not automatically establish that a named individual committed a crime.

Keep the legal subject visible.

---

## 🏛️ Identify The Institution

“The court” is not enough.

Different institutions have different:

- jurisdictions;
- mandates;
- procedures;
- evidential standards;
- remedies;
- enforcement mechanisms;
- and legal effects.

At minimum, distinguish between bodies such as:

- the International Court of Justice;
- the International Criminal Court;
- domestic courts;
- regional human-rights courts;
- UN bodies;
- commissions of inquiry;
- prosecutors;
- treaty bodies;
- regulators;
- legislatures;
- and governments.

An ICJ case between states is not an ICC prosecution of individuals.

A UN General Assembly resolution is not an ICJ judgment.

A prosecutor's application is not a conviction.

---

## 🧬 Procedural Posture Is Part Of The Law

Legal reporting frequently strips away procedure.

This folder should preserve it.

Possible stages include:

- complaint;
- referral;
- investigation;
- application;
- request for provisional measures;
- jurisdiction;
- admissibility;
- arrest-warrant application;
- warrant issuance;
- charge;
- trial;
- merits judgment;
- advisory opinion;
- appeal;
- enforcement;
- compliance review.

Do not say:

> **the court found X**

when the court decided only that it had jurisdiction to hear arguments about X.

Do not say:

> **the court rejected X**

when it rejected a particular procedural request.

Do not say:

> **X was convicted**

because a prosecutor sought an arrest warrant.

The stage is not decorative detail.

It changes the proposition.

## 📚 Authority Before Commentary

For substantial legal claims, work outward from the strongest available legal authority.

A useful hierarchy may include:

1. treaty or statute;
2. judgment, order or advisory opinion;
3. rules of procedure or official legal instrument;
4. official explanatory material;
5. high-quality legal scholarship;
6. specialist legal analysis;
7. journalism;
8. advocacy summaries;
9. social-media commentary;
10. AI summaries.

This is not a universal ranking of truth.

It is a reminder to locate the legal proposition as close as possible to the legal authority that creates, interprets or applies it.

---

## 🧿 Legal Argument Is Not Legal Finding

Governments make legal arguments.

NGOs make legal arguments.

Academics make legal arguments.

Lawyers make legal arguments.

Prosecutors make legal arguments.

Defendants make legal arguments.

Some are excellent.

Some later prevail.

Some do not.

The archive should preserve formulations such as:

> **The government argues...**

> **The prosecutor alleges...**

> **The organisation's legal analysis concludes...**

> **Professor X interprets the rule as...**

when that is what the source establishes.

Do not upgrade advocacy into adjudication.

---

## 🔬 Holdings, Reasoning And Implications Are Different

When reading a judgment or opinion, distinguish:

### What the body formally decided

The operative legal result.

### How it reasoned

The legal reasoning supporting that result.

### What commentators infer from it

Potential implications for other cases, actors or circumstances.

Those layers can overlap.

They are not identical.

A good legal factcheck should make clear which one it is discussing.

---

## 🧵 Quote The Operative Language When It Matters

Legal disputes often turn on one missing qualifier.

Where a proposition is contentious, preserve the actual wording of the relevant legal source where practical.

Particular care is needed with words such as:

- plausible;
- reasonable;
- necessary;
- proportionate;
- intentional;
- knowingly;
- widespread;
- systematic;
- effective control;
- direct participation;
- protected;
- prohibited;
- obligation;
- jurisdiction;
- and provisional.

Do not make a judgment easier to explain by making it legally stronger than it is.

## ⚔️ War Crimes And Individual Criminal Responsibility

“War crime” is not simply a synonym for:

> **terrible thing that happened during war.**

Individual criminal responsibility can require proof concerning:

- conduct;
- context;
- knowledge;
- intent;
- status of persons or objects;
- nexus to armed conflict;
- and other offence-specific elements.

A civilian death does not automatically prove a war crime.

Nor does the existence of military activity automatically make civilian harm lawful.

The legal analysis depends on the rule and the facts.

Where criminal responsibility is alleged, preserve the distinction between:

- allegation;
- investigation;
- charge;
- warrant;
- trial;
- and conviction.

## 🧵 Apartheid

This term can operate as:

- an ordinary political description;
- a historical comparison;
- a human-rights framework;
- and a legal classification under international law.

Later nodes should identify which proposition is being checked.

Relevant questions include:

- What legal definitions exist?
- Which instruments contain them?
- What elements must be established?
- Which organisations or experts have applied the term?
- What evidence do they rely upon?
- What counterarguments exist?
- Has a competent court made the particular finding being claimed?

Do not resolve a legal question merely by counting how many organisations use the word.

Do not dismiss a legal argument merely because the word is politically contested.

## ⚖️ ICC

A later dedicated section should track:

- jurisdiction;
- territorial and temporal scope;
- investigations;
- prosecutorial applications;
- arrest warrants;
- challenges;
- cooperation obligations;
- and case status.

Do not confuse:

> **ICC jurisdiction over a situation**

with:

> **proof that every alleged offence within that situation occurred.**

Likewise, an arrest warrant is serious legal action.

It is not a conviction.

## 🇺🇳 UN Resolutions And Bodies

- Which body?
- Security Council?
- General Assembly?
- Human Rights Council?
- Secretariat?
- treaty body?
- commission of inquiry?
- special rapporteur?
- agency?
- expert panel?

Then ask:

- What kind of instrument is this?
- Is it binding?
- Recommendatory?
- Evidential?
- Investigative?
- Interpretive?
- Political?
- Administrative?

Different UN outputs have different legal significance.

## 📣 Protest, Boycott And Anti-BDS Law

Legal questions about Palestine do not stop at the conflict zone.

They can involve:

- political boycotts;
- public procurement;
- government contracting;
- pension or investment rules;
- campus protest;
- employment;
- discrimination;
- freedom of expression;
- protest restrictions;
- charity law;
- and anti-BDS measures.

These rules vary substantially by jurisdiction.

A Wikipedia overview or news article may be useful for finding the issue.

It is not the final authority.

For individual claims, follow through to:

- legislation;
- executive action;
- contracts;
- judgments;
- constitutional rulings;
- regulator guidance;
- and current procedural status.

## 📰 Legal Headlines Need Translation Too

Common failure modes include:

> **Court says genocide**

when the procedural decision was narrower.

> **Court clears Israel**

when no final merits finding has been made.

> **UN declares X illegal**

without identifying the UN body or legal effect.

> **Warrant proves guilt**

when guilt remains to be adjudicated.

> **International law says...**

without identifying the rule, authority or contested interpretation.

Legal factchecking often means translating headlines back into procedure.

## 🚧 Common Legal Failure Modes

Watch for:

- treating a lawyer's opinion as a court ruling;
- treating an NGO report as binding law;
- treating a government interpretation as uncontested law;
- confusing the ICJ and ICC;
- confusing state responsibility with individual criminal responsibility;
- confusing jurisdiction with guilt;
- confusing provisional measures with final judgment;
- confusing an arrest warrant with conviction;
- confusing political recognition with resolution of every territorial question;
- treating all UN resolutions as legally equivalent;
- stripping legal tests of their required mental elements;
- using ordinary-language definitions for specialist legal terms;
- quoting one paragraph of a judgment without the operative section;
- ignoring dissenting or separate opinions where they materially illuminate a contested issue;
- and applying present legal conclusions backwards without checking the law and facts applicable at the relevant date.
