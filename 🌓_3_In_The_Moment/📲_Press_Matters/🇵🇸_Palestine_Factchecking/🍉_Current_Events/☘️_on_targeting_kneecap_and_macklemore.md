## 🛰️ Orientation

There is a very easy way to spend this entire node talking about Kneecap, Macklemore, Ed Sheeran, Robert Kraft, Keir Starmer, Irishness, Jewishness, antisemitism, terrorism, free speech, the NFL, colonialism, whiteness, the Good Friday Agreement, Cable Street, and whether somebody has accidentally written extremely good band merch.

All of those things may matter.

But the complaint underneath the complaint is much more serious.

There have been sustained and serious concerns about the conduct of Israeli military operations in Gaza: civilian deaths, proportionality, targeting practices, intelligence systems, AI-assisted target generation, the meaning of “human review”, the responsibility of commanders, governments, technology suppliers and allies, and the legal disputes over war crimes, crimes against humanity and genocide.

In September 2026, *NAZA*, directed by Israeli filmmakers Yuval Abraham and Rachel Szor, placed testimony from 24 Israeli military and intelligence insiders about parts of that machinery before an international audience. The Israeli military and Israeli political figures have disputed or condemned important claims associated with the film. Those disagreements are not reasons to stop asking questions. They are reasons to identify the claims, evidence, denials and remaining unknowns carefully.

At almost exactly the same time, a different information storm developed around Macklemore’s Palestine advocacy during Ed Sheeran’s US tour and the removal of Macklemore from later dates.

And then everybody started talking about everybody else.

This node is interested in how that happens.

Not because antisemitism is imaginary.

Not because terrorism is imaginary.

Not because anti-Irish prejudice is imaginary.

Not because fear is illegitimate.

**PEOPLE ARE ALLOWED TO BE SCARED.**

But fear is also information. Information can be routed. Fear can be amplified, converted into institutional action, attached to other populations, and made useful to people or organisations with power.

The question is not merely whether fear is real.

The question is:

> **What is being done with it?**

And, while we are all arguing:

> **What the fuck is happening to the original conversation?**

## 4. 🎤 What Actually Happened To Macklemore?

The recent episode needs to remain a chronology rather than a morality play.

Reuters reported that Macklemore made “Free Palestine” remarks during his 4 September 2026 MetLife Stadium appearance on Ed Sheeran’s *Loop Tour*. On 14 September, Reuters reported that Macklemore had been removed from later US dates after the promoter said venue owners would not host concerts with him on the bill. Macklemore alleged that Robert Kraft had rallied venue owners against his inclusion; Kraft publicly framed his position around combating antisemitism.

**Reported:** Macklemore performed, spoke about Palestine, and was subsequently removed from later dates.

**Promoter’s position:** venues would not host the concerts with Macklemore included.

**Macklemore’s position:** Kraft played a central role in producing that outcome.

**Kraft’s position:** his intervention was connected to his campaign against antisemitism.

Those propositions should not be flattened into one sentence claiming a centrally coordinated censorship operation unless evidence establishes one.

[📰 Reuters: “Macklemore dropped from Ed Sheeran’s US tour after ‘Free Palestine’ remarks”](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/macklemore-dropped-ed-sheerans-us-tour-after-free-palestine-remarks-2026-09-14/)

## 5. 🏟️ Who Owns The Stage?

Robert Kraft matters here not merely because he has an opinion.

He owns infrastructure.

**capital → stadium → contractual leverage → promoter → tour → performer → audience**

That does not tell us Kraft’s motives beyond what he has himself stated. It does not establish that every venue owner acted for the same reason. It does not tell us whether any third party pressured Kraft.

Those are separate questions.

What precisely is being identified as antisemitic?

What does the decision-maker understand “Free Palestine” to mean?

Which forms of Palestine advocacy would remain permissible?

Why is exclusion the chosen remedy?

What threshold separates criticism of Israel, offensive speech, antisemitism, incitement and a credible security threat?

And who gets to operationalise the answer?

## 6. 🏈 Bodies Welcome; Politics Negotiable

There is another American political economy sitting underneath the stadium.

Professional and college American football have long raised questions about the distribution of money, power, bodily risk and political voice, particularly because Black men have supplied a large share of the athletic labour while ownership and executive power remain distributed very differently.

That does **not** make Macklemore’s experience an example of anti-Black racism. Macklemore is a white artist working in a Black-created musical form.

The research question is:

> **What kinds of Black male visibility, physicality and cultural production are highly valuable to American sports-entertainment institutions — and what happens when political speech travels through the same cultural and institutional channels?**

This needs its own evidence pass before any stronger claim is made.

## 8. 🎲 BAYES HAS ENTERED THE PUB

[Conditional probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conditional_probability)

**P(marker | dangerous actor) ≠ P(dangerous actor | marker)**

A simplified social mechanism:

**actual violence → security response → characteristics associated with some perpetrators → characteristics become danger markers → larger population inherits suspicion → dissociation becomes evidence of safety → failure to dissociate becomes evidence of suspicion**

The histories of Irish people, Muslims, Palestinians, Jews, Black people or any other population are not interchangeable.

**Different histories can instantiate similar classification errors without becoming the same history.**

## 11. 🚪 The Price Of Admission

> **Prove you are safe by showing us you are not one of them.**

Not one of *those* Irish. Not one of *those* Muslims. Not one of *those* Palestinians. Not one of *those* protesters. Not one of *those* Jews. Not one of *those* artists.

Sometimes dissociation is a perfectly ordinary statement of fact: a person does not share another person’s politics and says so.

The problem is when **categorical dissociation becomes the admission price for ordinary belonging**.

When safety becomes conditional on helping move suspicion onto somebody else, the person being offered safety has been given a bargain.

That bargain can be refused.

Not every actor participates in every step. Not every gain is intentional. A system does not need one mastermind.

## 18. 🎥 NAZA: Return To The Complaint

*NAZA* premiered at Venice in September 2026 and received the festival’s special jury prize.

The Guardian, which produced the documentary, reports that the film is based on testimony from **24 Israeli military and intelligence insiders** and examines surveillance, remote killing and AI-assisted systems used during the Gaza war.

The filmmakers describe their subject not as isolated misconduct by a handful of rogue soldiers, but as the policies, orders, language, practices and systems within which military personnel operated.

The Israeli military has rejected important allegations associated with the film and criticised reliance on anonymous testimony. Israeli political figures have also attacked the film and its directors.

The documentary should therefore be treated as a major evidentiary object generating questions that can be corroborated, challenged, compared with earlier investigations and tested against military doctrine, records, testimony and other evidence.

**Israeli political authority → military command → intelligence organisations → technical units → software / AI systems → cloud and compute infrastructure → contractors and vendors → weapons manufacturers → foreign military support → US and other allied policy → procurement → investors and financial capital**

But:

> **CONNECTION ≠ KNOWLEDGE ≠ CONTROL ≠ INTENT ≠ RESPONSIBILITY.**

Investment is not command. Providing general-purpose computing infrastructure is not automatically knowledge of a particular strike. Writing software is not necessarily approving a target. Political support is not identical to operational control. Being Israeli is not responsibility for Israeli government conduct. Being Jewish is obviously not responsibility for Israeli government conduct. Being American or British is not individual responsibility for the actions of those governments.

Mapping the machine is not a method for making everybody guilty.

It is a method for working out **who did what**.

---
## 21. ⚖️ “Complicit In Genocide” Means What, Exactly?

This question becomes useless if every form of connection is collapsed into one moral blob.

**Genocide** is a specific legal category with particular elements, including the required intent. Allegations and legal findings about genocide must therefore be distinguished from allegations or findings concerning war crimes, crimes against humanity, unlawful attacks, disproportionate attacks or other violations.

Likewise:

**state responsibility**  
**individual criminal responsibility**  
**command responsibility**  
**aiding, abetting or assistance**  
**corporate responsibility**  
**political responsibility**  
**procurement responsibility**  
**investment relationship**  
**moral complicity**

are not synonyms.

The useful question is:

> **If contemporary warfare is distributed across governments, militaries, algorithms, intelligence systems, contractors, infrastructure, weapons suppliers, investors and allies, how do we attribute responsibility without artificially stopping at the person who presses the button — or dissolving responsibility into “everyone is guilty”?**

## 22. 📢 What The Shouting Shouts Over

The identity questions are not automatically fake questions.

Antisemitism matters. Anti-Irish prejudice matters. Political violence matters. Incitement matters. Free expression matters.

The problem is substitution.

**“Why are the Irish like this?”** is not an answer to **what targeting systems were used in Gaza?**

**“Is Kneecap intolerable?”** is not an answer to **what constituted meaningful human review?**

**“Is Macklemore antisemitic?”** does not answer **what civilian-harm thresholds were authorised?**

And:

**“Are Jewish people frightened?”**

can be answered:

**Yes.**

Then we can continue:

**What did the targeting system do?**

Fear becomes the loudest thing in the room.

The machine becomes background noise.

That is precisely backwards.

## 23. 🌍 We Are Living In Post-European-Empire Times

European empire does not explain everything.

Israel/Palestine is not simply a nineteenth-century European colonial war replayed with new costumes. Ireland is not presently a British colony. The United States is not merely Europe with bigger roads. Jewish diaspora history cannot be reduced to whiteness. Black American or Black British history cannot be reduced to European imperialism.

But we are all living among debris produced across centuries of European imperial power:

**borders, partitions, diasporas, racial classifications, citizenship regimes, security categories, militaries, imperial languages, property systems, capital concentrations, migration routes, settler societies, national mythologies, ideas about civilisation and danger.**

Different people inherited radically different positions inside those systems.

The formal structures can end, transform or retreat while their classifications, institutions, incentives and memories survive.

There does not need to be one man in an office assigning everybody their conditional racial status.

The machinery can persist through inherited categories, concentrated power, institutional habits, security reflexes and ordinary people making individually understandable decisions about how to stay safe.

I can understand why you are frightened.

I can understand why you reached for safety.

I can still ask:

**Who benefits from this arrangement?**

**Who has been moved outside the safe category?**

**Whose exclusion has become the price of somebody else’s reassurance?**

## 24. ☘️ The Refusal

This node does not require Irish people to be heroes.

It does not require Jewish people to agree with one another.

It does not require Kneecap to be right about everything.

It does not require Macklemore to be right about everything.

It does not require Robert Kraft to be a villain.

It does not require Keir Starmer to be a villain.

It requires something smaller and harder.

> **I can’t carve out the different pieces of my heart so that consumption is easier.**

And:

> **PEOPLE ARE ALLOWED TO BE SCARED.**
>
> **BUT OUR FEAR IS BEING INSTRUMENTALISED FOR GAIN.**

Understanding fear does not mean handing fear the steering wheel.

Historical trauma does not create collective culpability in somebody else.

Solidarity does not require equivalence.

Security does not require dissociation.

And an argument about everybody else’s identity does not answer the empirical questions sitting underneath the rubble.

Preserve the humans.

Separate the claims.

Keep the links.

Map the power.

Do not invent coordination where the evidence only establishes relationships or incentives.

Do not turn a network into a conspiracy.

Do not turn a frightened population into an enemy.

Do not turn Palestinians into the football in everybody else’s argument.

And when the information environment starts screaming loudly enough that the original object disappears:

# **SHOW ME THE FUCKING MACHINE.**
