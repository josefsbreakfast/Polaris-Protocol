## 🛰️ Orientation

This folder establishes the conceptual core of **The Seditious Traitors Fuckery Unit**.

STFU is not the formal name of a government office.

It is an analytical label for the developing connections among:

- public enemy designation;
- investigative and prosecutorial action;
- administrative punishment;
- immigration and national-security powers;
- financial and contractual pressure;
- professional isolation;
- supporter and media harassment.

The central proposition is simple:

> **STFU is not a department. It is a routing protocol.**

A target is identified.

A vulnerability is located.

Pressure is routed through the institution, legal power, commercial dependency or public audience most capable of imposing cost.

The immediate result may be:

- investigation;
- dismissal;
- detention;
- funding loss;
- contract pressure;
- loss of representation;
- public harassment.

The intended political output is wider:

- silence;
- withdrawal;
- isolation;
- anticipatory obedience.

## 🕸️ Secret Police As A Workflow

[`🕸️_secret_police_as_a_workflow.md`](./🕸️_secret_police_as_a_workflow.md)

This node defines political policing by function rather than institutional appearance.

Its central formulation is:

> **Institutionally fragmented. Operationally cumulative. Politically coherent.**

The workflow moves through:

1. political designation;
2. amplification;
3. routing;
4. investigation or compliance scrutiny;
5. pressure on associates;
6. punishment or prolonged exposure;
7. deterrence of the wider audience.

Each participating institution retains an ordinary description:

- FBI investigation;
- DOJ review;
- visa decision;
- grant condition;
- contract assessment;
- employer risk decision;
- private lawsuit.

The target receives the combined event.

The node also separates different coordination modes:

- direct presidential order;
- formal interagency referral;
- acknowledged political tasking;
- informal signalling;
- anticipatory obedience;
- entrepreneurial repression;
- private opportunism;
- independent but system-compatible action.

This distinction is essential.

Political coherence does not require one centrally directed conspiracy.

It can emerge through shared incentives, learned expectations and legible political preferences.

## 🏛️ Ordinary Letterheads, Extraordinary Repression

[`🏛️_ordinary_letterheads_extraordinary_repression.md`](./🏛️_ordinary_letterheads_extraordinary_repression.md)

This node examines the mismatch between familiar administrative form and extraordinary political purpose or cumulative effect.

Political pressure may arrive through:

- executive orders;
- memoranda;
- subpoenas;
- clearance reviews;
- visa notices;
- grant conditions;
- contract assessments;
- tax correspondence;
- licensing action;
- employment decisions.

The node separates four questions:

- **lawful authority**;
- **lawful procedure**;
- **legitimate purpose**;
- **politically discriminatory use**.

An institution may possess a power in general.

That does not establish why the power was activated, why one target was selected or whether the remedy was proportionate.

The executive orders against named law firms serve as unusually legible examples because political grievances and administrative consequences appeared within the same formal instruments.

The node also treats procedure as a double surface:

- a possible vehicle for political repression;
- a possible route to records, review, resistance and judicial relief.

> **Lack of metropolitan recognition is not lack of institutional experience.**

## ⚖️ Evidential Discipline

The core framework does not establish that:

- every allegation against a target is false;
- every target is innocent;
- every investigation is fabricated;
- every participating institution is centrally coordinated;
- every private decision results from government pressure;
- the United States is identical to a completed historical dictatorship.

The central questions are:

- Who selected the target?
- What conduct or allegation supplied the stated basis?
- Did the person or the offence come first?
- Who initiated the formal action?
- Was ordinary procedure followed?
- How were comparable allies treated?
- Did pressure spread to associates?
- What evidence connects the separate stages?
- What behaviour changed before final adjudication?

The framework must distinguish:

- political initiation;
- evidential merits;
- procedural regularity;
- proportionality;
- direct coordination;
- alignment;
- cumulative effect.

Without those distinctions, the analysis becomes rhetorical.

With them, the apparatus becomes mappable.
