# 💼 Casework  
**First created:** 2026-07-14 | **Last updated:** 2026-07-14  
*The evidentiary casework layer for recording named targets, distinct actions, legal routes, institutional responses, resistance, outcomes and residual harm across the STFU apparatus.*

---

## 🛰️ Orientation

This folder contains the specialist casework for the **Seditious Traitors Fuckery Unit**.

Its purpose is to convert scattered incidents into a maintained evidentiary record that can be:

- compared;
- updated;
- challenged;
- corrected;
- linked to wider apparatus patterns;
- used in reporting without collapsing separate legal matters.

The folder’s central question is:

> **What happened to whom, through which institution and legal route, with what evidence of political connection, what legal outcome, what contrary evidence and what practical consequence?**

The core analytical movement is:

> **register the action → separate the legal matter → classify the route → identify initiation → compare treatment → track resistance → record outcome → preserve residual harm**

---

## 💼 The Core Recordkeeping Rule

The folder uses one central rule:

> **One row per distinct action or proceeding, not one row per person.**

A single target may therefore have several linked entries involving:

- investigation;
- prosecution;
- clearance action;
- personnel action;
- visa action;
- detention;
- grant pressure;
- contract review;
- private litigation;
- public naming;
- supporter harassment;
- associative punishment.

This prevents:

- different legal theories being merged;
- different institutions being collapsed;
- different evidential levels being blurred;
- one dismissal from appearing to resolve unrelated actions.

The target can be summarised at person or institution level.

The underlying action records remain separate.

## 🧭 How The Folder Works Together

The casework workflow is:

### Register

Create one action-level record in the master register.

### Specialise

Place the action inside the relevant specialist case file.

### Sequence

Add each dated event to the target journey.

### Compare

Test ordinary treatment and meaningful comparators.

### Challenge

Record contrary evidence and institutional resistance.

### Update

Maintain status history without overwriting prior stages.

### Aggregate

Link several actions through a common target identifier.

### Report

Feed the evidence into Media Tools and wider apparatus analysis.

## 🧱 One Action, Several Views

The same event may appear in several places.

Example:

- the register contains the action-level row;
- the specialist node contains route-specific detail;
- the timeline contains the dated sequence;
- the Media Tools story template uses the verified facts;
- the apparatus map connects the event to repeated methods or personnel.

This is not duplication where each layer performs a different function.

The record should remain internally consistent.

## 🪞 Comparator Discipline

Every case should ask:

- What similar conduct by allies exists?
- How are comparable cases ordinarily treated?
- What material difference explains different treatment?
- Was the same authority used?
- Was the same evidential threshold present?
- Was the consequence comparable?

A meaningful comparator should involve:

- similar conduct;
- similar legal power;
- similar institutional level;
- similar status;
- similar consequence.

Weak comparators damage the entire analysis.

---

## 🛡️ Contrary Evidence Is Mandatory

Every action-level record should contain:

- exculpatory facts;
- legitimate official explanation;
- strong predication;
- ordinary process;
- consistent comparator treatment;
- institutional resistance;
- successful safeguards;
- judicial correction;
- evidence of independent private action;
- facts weakening political connection.

A blank contrary-evidence field should trigger review.

The apparatus framework should be capable of becoming:

- narrower;
- weaker;
- differently classified;
- removed.

## 🧪 Public-Inclusion Threshold

A case should not enter the public register merely because:

- a political figure named someone;
- an allegation exists;
- the case resembles another;
- an anonymous report claims an investigation.

Suggested minimum threshold:

- one documented formal action;
- one reliable source identifying the target and route;
- clear separation of allegation and finding;
- evidence level assigned;
- legal status current;
- contrary evidence field completed;
- privacy and safety review completed.

A stronger apparatus classification requires more:

- documented routing;
- repeated method;
- meaningful comparator;
- observable practical consequence;
- associative or behavioural output.

## 🚫 Common Casework Failures

Avoid:

- one row per person;
- merging separate legal matters;
- treating public naming as formal action;
- treating investigation as guilt;
- treating dismissal as proof of retaliation;
- treating legal victory as full restoration;
- omitting contrary evidence;
- overwriting status history;
- drawing causal arrows from chronology alone;
- publishing sensitive associate data;
- collapsing symbolic and material consequences;
- assuming every internal institutional change was coerced;
- treating all enforcement as politically abusive.

---

## 🧪 Quality-Control Questions

Before publishing or updating a case, ask:

1. Is this one distinct action?
2. Is the target correctly identified?
3. Is the responsible institution specific?
4. Are political designation and legal initiation separated?
5. Is the route correctly classified?
6. Is predicating evidence recorded accurately?
7. Is the evidence level proportionate?
8. Is the coordination label supported?
9. Is the comparator meaningful?
10. Is contrary evidence visible?
11. Is the legal status current?
12. Is residual harm distinguished from legal outcome?
13. Are sealed or disputed materials marked?
14. Does the record expose unnecessary private information?
15. Would narrowing or removing the case improve accuracy?

## ⚠️ Evidential Boundaries

This folder does not claim that:

- every named target was innocent;
- every investigation was retaliatory;
- every public designation caused formal action;
- every recurring method was centrally coordinated;
- every private response was government-directed;
- every legal defeat proves political motive;
- every practical effect was intended;
- every university, firm or agency response was coerced;
- all enforcement involving political figures belongs inside STFU.

It makes a narrower methodological claim:

> **The apparatus becomes testable when every distinct action is recorded with its target, initiator, route, evidence level, coordination status, comparator treatment, contrary evidence, legal outcome, resistance, practical consequence and residual harm—and when several actions involving one target remain linked without being collapsed.**
