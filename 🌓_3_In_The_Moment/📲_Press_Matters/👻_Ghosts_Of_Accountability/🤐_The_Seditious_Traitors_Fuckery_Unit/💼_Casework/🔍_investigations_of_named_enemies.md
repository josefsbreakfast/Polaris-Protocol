# 🔍 Investigations Of Named Enemies  
**First created:** 2026-07-14 | **Last updated:** 2026-07-14  
*A principal case file for criminal, intelligence and administrative investigations involving publicly named political enemies, with strict separation between public demand, formal predication, investigative route, process anomaly and legal outcome.*

---

## 🛰️ Orientation

This node is the principal case file for investigations involving publicly named political enemies.

Its purpose is to compare cases involving:

- former FBI officials;
- former intelligence officials;
- prosecutors;
- political opponents;
- witnesses;
- election officials;
- critics within the administration;
- investigators associated with prior inquiries;
- officials accused of disloyalty or weaponisation.

The central question is:

> **When a public enemy is named, what offence is alleged, what predicating evidence exists, who requests action, which office handles it, and how closely does the process resemble ordinary investigative practice?**

The core named examples include:

- James Comey;
- John Brennan;
- John Bolton;
- Jack Smith;
- investigators associated with Russia inquiries;
- prosecutors involved in January 6 cases;
- election officials accused publicly by Trump.

This node should remain a living action-level case file.

---

## 🧭 The Core Analytical Sequence

For each case, record:

1. first public naming;
2. alleged offence;
3. predicating evidence;
4. known initiator;
5. formal investigative route;
6. personnel involved;
7. process anomalies;
8. public and political pressure;
9. legal outcome;
10. reputational, financial and professional consequence.

The core chain is:

> **public designation → alleged offence → investigative opening → procedural route → amplification → legal outcome → residual harm**

## 🧠 Person First Or Offence First

Use one of:

- `PERSON_FIRST`;
- `OFFENCE_FIRST`;
- `SIMULTANEOUS`;
- `UNCLEAR`;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Record the chronology supporting the classification.

### Person First

The named target appears before a specific offence or predicate.

### Offence First

A specific offence or evidential matter appears before the person is selected.

### Simultaneous

The target and alleged offence emerge together.

### Unclear

The public record does not establish sequence.

This field is diagnostic.

It is not proof of retaliation.

## 🔀 Investigative Route

Every case should distinguish:

### Active Criminal Investigation

A formal criminal matter examining possible offences.

### Preliminary Review

An early assessment before full investigation.

### Intelligence Assessment

An intelligence process examining security or foreign-influence concerns.

### Inspector-General Inquiry

An internal oversight investigation.

### Congressional Referral

A referral or request from a legislative body.

### Public Demand Without Known Formal Action

Political pressure where no formal investigative step is established.

These routes must not be collapsed.

## 🛡️ Important Cautions

Do not:

- treat investigation as guilt;
- treat public demand as formal opening;
- treat congressional referral as agency acceptance;
- treat preliminary review as criminal investigation;
- treat intelligence assessment as prosecution;
- infer retaliation solely from political salience;
- assume no public predicate means no predicate exists;
- merge separate cases involving one target;
- treat dismissal as proof of retaliatory origin;
- treat retaliatory origin as proof the conduct was lawful;
- publish sealed or grand-jury material improperly;
- reproduce defamatory allegations unnecessarily.

---

## 🧪 What Would Weaken The Retaliation Analysis?

The analysis should be narrowed where evidence shows:

- strong predication before public naming;
- ordinary investigative routing;
- career-official initiation;
- consistent comparator treatment;
- stable legal theory;
- no unusual senior involvement;
- judicial validation;
- substantial independent evidence;
- routine reopening based on new facts;
- no practical chilling or associative effect.

A credible case file must actively seek these facts.
