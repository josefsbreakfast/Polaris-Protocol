# 📋 Target And Method Register  
**First created:** 2026-07-14 | **Last updated:** 2026-07-14  
*The master case register for preserving distinct actions, comparing methods and preventing political pressure from disappearing into isolated scandal cycles.*

---

## 🛰️ Orientation

The **Target And Method Register** is the central evidentiary ledger for the entire STFU apparatus.

Its purpose is to:

- prevent incidents from being lost as isolated scandals;
- compare target classes;
- compare methods;
- identify repeated personnel, language and institutions;
- track legal and practical outcomes;
- preserve contrary evidence and institutional resistance;
- show where one target faced several distinct actions.

The central recordkeeping rule is:

> **One row per distinct action or proceeding, not one row per person.**

A single person may therefore appear in several entries where they face:

- a criminal investigation;
- a clearance action;
- an employment decision;
- a contract review;
- a visa action;
- a private lawsuit;
- supporter harassment.

Do not collapse separate legal matters into one allegation.

---

## 🧭 What The Register Is For

The register should answer:

- Who was targeted?
- What made them politically salient?
- Who first designated them?
- What allegation or enemy category was used?
- Which institution acted?
- What route carried the pressure?
- What evidence connects the action to political direction or signalling?
- What happened legally?
- What happened practically?
- Who else was affected?
- What evidence weakens the apparatus interpretation?

The register is not a list of enemies.

It is a structured record of actions.

---

## 🧱 Unit Of Analysis

The unit of analysis is:

> **one target × one distinct action × one responsible institution**

Examples:

- one executive order affecting one law firm;
- one visa revocation affecting one student;
- one clearance review affecting one former official;
- one grant freeze affecting one university;
- one investigation opened by one office;
- one private lawsuit filed by one actor.

Where several institutions jointly execute one action, record:

- the lead institution;
- the supporting institutions;
- the coordination level.

Where several actions arise from the same public designation, create separate entries and link them through a common target identifier.

---

## 🪪 Core Entry Fields

Each entry should record:

- case ID;
- target name;
- target type;
- political relevance;
- selector or first public designator;
- allegation or enemy category;
- responsible institution;
- pressure route;
- associated targets;
- evidence level;
- current status;
- practical consequence.

These are the minimum fields.

---

## 🆔 Case ID

Use a stable identifier.

Suggested format:

```text
STFU-[YEAR]-[TARGETCODE]-[ACTIONNUMBER]
```

Example:

```text
STFU-2026-LAWFIRM-003
```

The identifier should not change if:

- the action is enjoined;
- the case closes;
- the target changes representation;
- new evidence emerges.

---

## 🎯 Target Name

Record the full public name of:

- person;
- firm;
- university;
- nonprofit;
- contractor;
- media organisation;
- other institution.

Where the identity is not public, use:

- anonymised target code;
- reason for anonymity;
- disclosure status.

Do not insert private identities solely to complete the register.

## 🧠 Political Relevance

Record why the target became politically salient.

Possible descriptions include:

- investigated Trump;
- represented a political opponent;
- criticised administration policy;
- organised protest;
- administered an election;
- worked in a prior administration;
- challenged an executive order;
- supported a disfavoured institution;
- belonged to a publicly named target category.

Political salience is not proof of retaliation.

It explains why the case belongs in the register.

---

## 📣 Selector Or First Public Designator

Record:

- person;
- office;
- agency;
- movement organisation;
- media actor;
- congressional committee;
- unknown.

Include:

- first known date;
- exact or paraphrased designation;
- source.

Distinguish:

- public designator;
- formal initiator;
- responsible institution.

They may be different.

---

## 🗣️ Allegation Or Enemy Category

Record the principal allegation or category used.

Examples include:

- traitor;
- deep-state official;
- election liar;
- weaponisation actor;
- foreign influence;
- antisemitism failure;
- discriminatory employer;
- extremist network;
- material support;
- disloyal officer;
- lawfare actor.

Where a specific legal allegation exists, record it separately from the political category.

---

## 🏛️ Responsible Institution

Record the institution that took the action.

Examples include:

- White House;
- Department of Justice;
- FBI;
- State Department;
- Department of Homeland Security;
- Department of Education;
- Department of Defense;
- employing agency;
- university;
- law firm;
- private litigant;
- contractor;
- client;
- supporter network.

Avoid using “the government” where a narrower actor is known.

- `PERSONNEL_ACTION`;

One entry may have one primary route and several secondary route tags.

Do not merge several distinct proceedings into one entry merely because they affect the same target.

---

## 🪤 Associated Targets

Record whether the action affected:

- lawyers;
- clients;
- employers;
- funders;
- colleagues;
- contractors;
- relatives;
- partner institutions.

For each associated target, record:

- relationship;
- consequence;
- source;
- evidence level.

Associative impact should not be presumed.

---

## 🧾 Evidence-Level Fields

Use the strongest supported evidence level.

Suggested fields include:

- `DIRECT_WRITTEN_ORDER`;
- `FORMAL_REFERRAL`;
- `ACKNOWLEDGED_SENIOR_INVOLVEMENT`;
- `DOCUMENTARY_INFERENCE`;
- `PATTERN_EVIDENCE`;
- `TEMPORAL_ASSOCIATION`;
- `UNVERIFIED_ALLEGATION`.

These fields should align with the Media Tools vocabulary.

---

## 📅 Initiation Fields

Each entry should record:

- date of first public designation;
- date formal action began;
- known initiator;
- predicating evidence if public;
- whether an offence or a person came first.

These fields help distinguish ordinary enforcement from person-first routing.

Use one of:

- `OFFENCE_FIRST`;
- `PERSON_FIRST`;
- `SIMULTANEOUS`;
- `UNCLEAR`;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

This field should be based on chronology, not intuition.

---

## 🔗 Cumulative-Pressure Fields

Add fields for:

- number of institutions involved;
- employment effect;
- financial effect;
- legal cost;
- security effect;
- effect on associates;
- observed chilling effect.

Possible chilling effects include:

- reduced speech;
- declined representation;
- donor withdrawal;
- resignation;
- protest reduction;
- policy concession;
- self-censorship;
- no effect identified.

This field requires evidence.

---

## ⚖️ Legal-Status Fields

Use one primary legal-status value:

- `ACTIVE`;
- `CLOSED`;
- `DISMISSED`;
- `ENJOINED`;
- `SETTLED`;
- `CONVICTED`;
- `PARDONED`;
- `UNRESOLVED`.

Do not overwrite earlier statuses.

Maintain a dated status history.

Example:

```text
2026-02-03 — ACTIVE
2026-03-18 — ENJOINED
2026-05-22 — DISMISSED
2026-06-11 — APPEAL FILED
```

This preserves the case journey.

---

## 👑 Apparatus-Mode Fields

Use one or more of:

- `CROWN_PROTECTION`;
- `MOVEMENT_PROTECTION`;
- `REGIME_PROTECTION`;
- `MIXED`.

These fields describe the apparent institutional function.

They do not prove motive.

### Crown Protection

Protection of the central leader’s authority, exposure, reputation or personal power.

### Movement Protection

Protection of the wider ideological movement, its networks, narratives or mobilisation.

### Regime Protection

Protection of governing control, administrative obedience, institutional loyalty or implementation capacity.

### Mixed

More than one function appears relevant.

Add a short explanation.

---

## 🔗 Coordination Fields

Use one primary coordination label:

- `DIRECT_COORDINATION_DOCUMENTED`;
- `FORMAL_INTERAGENCY_CONTACT`;
- `SHARED_RHETORIC`;
- `INDEPENDENT_ALIGNMENT`;
- `UNKNOWN`.

### Direct Coordination Documented

Records show joint planning, communication, agreed action, command or coordinated implementation.

### Formal Interagency Contact

Records show referral, notification, information sharing or official request.

### Shared Rhetoric

Separate actors repeat the same allegation, target category, framing or demanded outcome.

### Independent Alignment

Actors appear to act separately in the same political environment.

### Unknown

The relationship between actions cannot be established.

## 🪞 Comparator Fields

Each entry should include:

- similar conduct by allies;
- ordinary treatment of comparable cases;
- explanation for different treatment.

A meaningful comparator should involve:

- similar conduct;
- similar legal power;
- similar institutional level;
- similar evidential threshold;
- similar consequence.

The comparator field must include evidence that weakens the selectivity claim.

---

## 🛡️ Contrary Evidence And Institutional Resistance

Every entry should include:

- exculpatory facts;
- institutional resistance;
- contrary evidence;
- successful safeguards;
- judicial correction;
- neutral explanations.

This is mandatory.

Record where:

- career officials resisted;
- agencies refused;
- inspectors general intervened;
- courts blocked action;
- private institutions refused compliance;
- Congress demanded records.

Resistance is part of the apparatus map.

## 🔐 Sealed, Classified Or Disputed Material

## 📎 Public-Inclusion Threshold

A case should not be placed in the public register merely because:

- the target is politically controversial;
- an allegation exists;
- a public figure demanded action;
- the case resembles another.

Suggested minimum threshold:

- one documented formal action;
- one reliable source establishing the target and route;
- clear distinction between allegation and finding;
- evidence level assigned;
- contrary evidence field completed;
- legal status current;
- privacy and safety review completed.

For stronger apparatus classification, require:

- multiple indicators;
- meaningful comparator analysis;
- documented routing or repeated method;
- observable practical consequence.

---

## 🧮 One Row Per Action Or One Row Per Target?

The recommended answer is:

> **One row per action, linked by target ID.**

Reasons:

- one person may face several independent proceedings;
- statuses differ;
- legal theories differ;
- institutions differ;
- evidence levels differ;
- some actions may be retaliatory while others are ordinary.

A target-level summary can aggregate the action rows.

## 🗂️ Standardisation

Fields most suitable for standardisation include:

- case ID;
- target type;
- route;
- evidence level;
- initiation dates;
- legal status;
- coordination;
- apparatus mode;
- number of institutions;
- practical effect tags.

Fields less suitable for strict standardisation include:

- political relevance;
- predicating evidence;
- comparator explanation;
- contrary evidence;
- residual harm.

Use controlled tags plus narrative notes.

---

## 🧪 Quality-Control Questions

Before adding or updating an entry, ask:

1. Is this one distinct action?
2. Is the responsible institution correctly identified?
3. Are public designation and formal initiation separated?
4. Is the evidence level accurate?
5. Is chronology sourced?
6. Is the legal status current?
7. Have associated targets been verified?
8. Is the comparator meaningful?
9. Is contrary evidence included?
10. Is residual harm distinguished from legal outcome?
11. Is sealed or disputed material marked?
12. Would a narrower entry be more accurate?

---

## 🚫 Common Register Failures

Avoid:

- one row per person;
- merging several legal matters;
- using allegations as findings;
- treating public rhetoric as formal action;
- treating timing as coordination;
- leaving contrary evidence blank;
- overwriting old statuses;
- treating dismissal as complete restoration;
- recording private associates without necessity;
- using unverified material as a public field;
- counting media attention as an institution;
- assigning motive as fact.

---

## ⚠️ Evidential Boundaries

This register does not claim that:

- every entry proves retaliation;
- every target is innocent;
- every recurring method was centrally coordinated;
- every public designation caused later action;
- every private institution acted under government direction;
- every legal defeat confirms abuse;
- every practical consequence was intended.

It makes a narrower methodological claim:

> **The apparatus becomes testable when every distinct action is recorded with its target, selector, initiator, route, evidence level, coordination status, comparator treatment, legal outcome, practical consequence, contrary evidence and residual harm.**
