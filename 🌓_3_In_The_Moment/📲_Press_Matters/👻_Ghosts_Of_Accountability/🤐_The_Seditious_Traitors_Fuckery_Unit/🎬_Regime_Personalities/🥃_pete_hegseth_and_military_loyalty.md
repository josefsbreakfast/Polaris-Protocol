**First created:** 2026-07-12 | **Last updated:** 2026-07-12  
*The purge becomes military doctrine when loyalty is recoded as readiness.*

## 🛰️ Orientation

Pete Hegseth’s role sits in the military and defence branch of regime enforcement.

His portfolio concerns:

- personnel loyalty;
- command culture;
- internal discipline;
- ideological reshaping;
- removal of perceived institutional resistance;
- military legitimacy;
- use of defence institutions in domestic emergencies.

The central concern is not only whether the military is deployed domestically.

It is whether the armed forces are reorganised so that:

- professional independence appears disloyal;
- legal caution appears weak;
- ideological conformity appears operational;
- loyalty to Trump appears synonymous with civilian control.

> **Military politicisation begins before domestic deployment. It begins when command, promotion, access and professional legitimacy are reorganised around political loyalty.**

## ⚖️ Four Categories To Keep Separate

### Lawful Civilian Control

Political leaders set policy and appoint military leadership.

### Ordinary Command Personnel Decisions

Removals or reassignments based on performance, strategy or trust.

### Partisan Loyalty Enforcement

Career consequences substantially tied to personal or political allegiance.

### Politicisation Of The Armed Forces

Military institutions increasingly organised around movement ideology and domestic political purpose.

One case may implicate more than one category.

The evidence determines the strongest claim.
