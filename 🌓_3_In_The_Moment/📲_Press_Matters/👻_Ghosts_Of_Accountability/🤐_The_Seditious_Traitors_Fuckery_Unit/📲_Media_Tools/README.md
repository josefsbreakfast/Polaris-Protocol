# 📲 Media Tools  
**First created:** 2026-07-14 | **Last updated:** 2026-07-14  
*Reporting the apparatus requires more than collecting outrages. It requires tracing how political hostility becomes practical pressure, at the evidence level the record actually supports.*

---

## 🛰️ Orientation

This folder contains the reporting tools for the **Seditious Traitors Fuckery Unit**.

Its purpose is to turn a distributed and often ambiguous coercive system into journalism that is:

- structurally legible;
- evidentially disciplined;
- resistant to easy rebuttal;
- attentive to cumulative harm;
- precise about uncertainty;
- useful under deadline.

The folder’s central question is:

> **How does political hostility become practical pressure, through which actor and vulnerability, with what evidence of connection, and with what effect on the target and everyone watching?**

The analytical movement is:

> **screen the incident → map the network → identify retaliatory routing → reconstruct cumulative pressure → calibrate language → close easy rebuttals → force procedural answers → write the story**

---

## 📲 The Core Reporting Rule

Do not merely report:

> **What outrageous thing happened?**

Report:

- who selected the target;
- which vulnerability was identified;
- who activated it;
- what route carried the pressure;
- whether associates were affected;
- what evidence connects the actions;
- what changed afterward.

The folder replaces personality-only scandal reporting with mechanism reporting.

## 📰 The STFU Reporting Test

[📰 The STFU Reporting Test](./📰_the_stfu_reporting_test.md)

This is the rapid intake gate.

Its core question is:

> **Does this incident convert political hostility into practical pressure through state, commercial or supporter-enabled mechanisms?**

The five-part test asks whether:

1. a person, institution or network was publicly designated;
2. a practical vulnerability was identified;
3. an official, agency or aligned private actor activated it;
4. pressure spread to associates;
5. a wider deterrent effect followed.

It also tests:

- routing;
- selectivity;
- associative punishment;
- behavioural output;
- connection to known people, doctrines and methods.

The proposed classifications are:

- `MONITOR`;
- `POSSIBLE_ROUTING_EVENT`;
- `STRONG_STFU_RELEVANCE`;
- `APPARATUS_CASE`.

The checklist is an intake aid.

It is not proof of motive.

## 🧭 How To Identify Retaliatory Routing

[🧭 How To Identify Retaliatory Routing](./🧭_how_to_identify_retaliatory_routing.md)

This node tests whether ordinary enforcement was politically redirected.

Its diagnostic sequence is:

> **initiation → predication → route choice → selectivity → rhetoric → personnel → multiplication → output**

Warning signs include:

- target named before offence;
- repeated demands to investigate;
- weak or shifting legal theory;
- unusual senior involvement;
- ordinary channels bypassed;
- multiple institutions acting in sequence;
- public celebration before adjudication;
- pressure spreading to associates;
- silence or isolation as practical output.

The node explicitly allows that:

> **Genuine misconduct can coexist with retaliatory initiation, selective severity or politically amplified punishment.**

Political prominence naturally increases scrutiny.

Retaliation requires more than publicity.

## 🔗 How To Report Cumulative Pressure

[🔗 How To Report Cumulative Pressure](./🔗_how_to_report_cumulative_pressure.md)

This node explains how to report a single field of pressure without inventing one central command.

Its central claim is:

> **Fragmented institutions can create one field of pressure even where each actor claims independence.**

It requires reporters to preserve each action separately:

- agency investigation;
- employer decision;
- private litigation;
- client withdrawal;
- supporter threats;
- immigration or clearance action.

Every connection is classified as:

- directly coordinated;
- formally referred;
- publicly signalled;
- temporally linked;
- independently aligned;
- connection unknown.

The report then reconstructs the cumulative burden:

- legal fees;
- lost income;
- security costs;
- time;
- reputational damage;
- family impact;
- reduced speech or participation.

Cumulative effect is a fact.

It is not by itself proof of central planning.

## 🧷 Language For Different Evidence Levels

[🧷 Language For Different Evidence Levels](./🧷_language_for_different_evidence_levels.md)

This node provides the controlled vocabulary.

Its central rule is:

> **Use the strongest language the evidence supports—and no stronger.**

The evidence ladder distinguishes:

1. direct evidence;
2. strong documentary inference;
3. pattern evidence;
4. temporal association;
5. unverified claim;
6. unknown connection.

It separates terms such as:

- ordered;
- directed;
- formally referred;
- appears to have prompted;
- fits a broader pattern;
- followed;
- alleged;
- the available record does not establish.

It also distinguishes:

- target;
- subject;
- suspect;
- defendant;
- convicted person;

and:

- review;
- inquiry;
- investigation;
- referral;
- prosecution;
- administrative action.

Precision should not become false neutrality.

Qualifiers should narrow the claim accurately, not bleach observable coercion out of the story.

## 🚫 Easy Rebuttals And False Balance

[🚫 Easy Rebuttals And False Balance](./🚫_easy_rebuttals_and_false_balance.md)

This is the defensive editing layer.

It replaces brittle claims such as:

- Trump personally ordered every action;
- all cases are fabricated;
- this is literally the Stasi;
- courts no longer matter;
- formally separate institutions cannot form an apparatus;
- private companies were directly ordered;
- prior administrations never investigated opponents.

With stronger formulations that distinguish:

- orders;
- referrals;
- signalling;
- anticipatory obedience;
- independent alignment;
- genuine misconduct;
- judicial resistance;
- institutional difference;
- government-created private risk.

Meaningful comparison requires:

- the same power;
- similar conduct;
- a similar institutional level;
- a similar evidential threshold;
- a similar consequence.

Avoiding false balance does not mean excluding contrary evidence.

It means refusing to pretend unlike things are equivalent.

## 🎙️ Questions For Press Briefings

[🎙️ Questions For Press Briefings](./🎙️_questions_for_press_briefings.md)

This node converts the analytical framework into procedural questions.

Its central rule is:

> **Ask the question that requires an official to name the actor, date, authority, record or safeguard.**

The question bank covers:

- initiation;
- authority;
- personnel;
- coordination;
- selectivity;
- scope;
- safeguards;
- intended output;
- transparency.

The strongest questions include:

- Who requested this review?
- On what date was it initiated?
- What predicating evidence existed?
- What written authority governs the action?
- Were political appointees involved in case selection?
- Were referrals made?
- What information was shared?
- How many comparable cases exist?
- What happens if no violation is found?
- Will inaccurate records be corrected or destroyed?

One precise procedural question often produces more evidence than a broad accusation.

## 📝 The STFU Story Template

[📝 The STFU Story Template](./📝_the_stfu_story_template.md)

This node turns the reporting method into a repeatable story structure.

The core sequence is:

1. concrete consequence;
2. target;
3. designation;
4. route;
5. process;
6. cumulative pressure;
7. wider apparatus;
8. government and institutional response;
9. legal and factual limits;
10. output;
11. residual consequence.

The story begins with what happened to the target.

It does not begin with an abstract theory of authoritarianism.

It then proves:

- who designated;
- which vulnerability mattered;
- who carried the pressure;
- what authority was used;
- what was unusual;
- what else followed;
- what remains altered.

The target must remain a person rather than becoming merely a node in the system map.

## 🧭 The Folder’s Analytical Movement

### Screen

Does the incident meet the threshold for apparatus reporting?

### Map

Which people, institutions, target classes, methods and outcomes connect it to earlier cases?

### Diagnose

Did the process begin with conduct or a politically selected person?

### Reconstruct

How did formally separate actions combine around the target?

### Calibrate

What does the evidence justify saying about each connection?

### Stress-Test

Which claims are vulnerable to easy rebuttal or false equivalence?

### Question

Which precise procedural unknowns should officials be forced to answer?

### Write

How should the final story keep the person, mechanism, limits and output visible?

## 🕸️ The Core Reporting Model

The folder uses a common chain:

> **designation → vulnerability → activation → routing → associative spread → behavioural output**

### Designation

The target is named, accused or classified.

### Vulnerability

A practical dependency becomes visible.

### Activation

An actor uses that vulnerability.

### Routing

Pressure travels through a legal, administrative, commercial or supporter-enabled mechanism.

### Associative Spread

The cost reaches lawyers, employers, clients, funders, colleagues or family.

### Behavioural Output

Someone stops speaking, representing, employing, funding, protesting or associating.

This chain should be shown through evidence.

It should never be presumed.

## 🗺️ Pattern, Doctrine And Coordination

### Recurring Pattern

Similar target selection, routing or output appears across cases.

### Shared Doctrine

Actors use the same language, target categories or theories.

### Documented Coordination

Records show instruction, referral, communication or joint action.

These categories may coexist.

They are not interchangeable.

## ⚖️ Formal Legality Is Not The End Of Analysis

A mechanism may be formally lawful.

Reporting should still examine:

- initiation;
- selection;
- severity;
- procedural regularity;
- comparator treatment;
- associative spread;
- practical output.

The legal basis explains how the power operated.

It does not alone establish why it was used against this target.

---

## 🏛️ Institutional Separation Must Remain Visible

The apparatus may be distributed across:

- political leadership;
- agencies;
- employers;
- law firms;
- universities;
- funders;
- contractors;
- private litigants;
- supporter networks.

Formal separation matters.

It prevents unsupported claims of one command structure.

The report should nevertheless describe how separate actions may:

- reinforce;
- compound;
- transmit risk;
- produce one cumulative field of pressure.

## ⚡ Anticipatory Compliance

Not every consequence requires an order.

Institutions may act because they anticipate:

- contract risk;
- regulatory exposure;
- client concern;
- reputational attack;
- political scrutiny;
- supporter campaigns.

The evidence should establish:

- what risk was perceived;
- what decision followed;
- whether government contact occurred;
- whether the institution acted independently.

Anticipatory obedience is a mechanism.

It is not a synonym for direct coordination.

---

## 🪤 Associative Punishment

The apparatus becomes more structurally significant when pressure reaches:

- lawyers;
- employers;
- clients;
- funders;
- colleagues;
- relatives;
- institutions offering support.

Associative punishment increases the cost of:

- representation;
- employment;
- funding;
- proximity;
- solidarity.

Its likely output is isolation.

## 🤫 Behavioural Output

The final reporting question is:

> **What became harder, riskier or quieter after the incident?**

Possible outputs include:

- silence;
- withdrawal;
- resignation;
- settlement;
- declined representation;
- client loss;
- donor retreat;
- reduced protest;
- institutional concession;
- anticipatory compliance.

The output must be evidenced through:

- documents;
- interviews;
- observable decisions;
- reliable testimony.

---

## ⚖️ Legal Victory And Residual Harm

The folder does not treat legal defeat as automatic resolution.

A blocked, dismissed or withdrawn action may still leave:

- legal debt;
- lost clients;
- damaged career;
- increased security risk;
- family disruption;
- copied institutional behaviour;
- reduced willingness to participate.

Stories should report both:

- formal legal outcome;
- residual practical effect.

---

## 🧠 The Target Remains A Person

The framework must not consume the person it describes.

Reporting should avoid:

- reproducing harassment;
- publishing unnecessary private information;
- exposing security measures;
- flattening the target into a symbol;
- requiring moral perfection;
- turning trauma into spectacle.

The target can be complicated, flawed or accused of genuine misconduct and still be entitled to accurate reporting on process, selectivity and disproportionate pressure.

---

## 🛡️ Harm-Minimising Reporting

Before publication, ask:

- Does this detail create new risk?
- Is the private information necessary?
- Does repeating the allegation add evidential value?
- Have associates consented to identification?
- Does the story expose family or security routines?
- Is the target’s vulnerability being reproduced?
- Can the mechanism be explained with less harmful detail?

The reporting itself should not become another enforcement surface.

## 🛠️ Rapid Workflow

Under deadline:

1. run the STFU reporting test;
2. identify the target and vulnerability;
3. name the routing actor and authority;
4. construct the designation-to-consequence chronology;
5. tag the evidence level;
6. check selectivity and comparators;
7. record associate impact;
8. request initiation and referral records;
9. ask one precise briefing question;
10. state what remains unknown.

For a full investigation:

1. build the target register;
2. map all pressure events;
3. obtain process records;
4. compare ordinary procedure;
5. identify repeated methods and personnel;
6. quantify cumulative burden;
7. include contrary evidence;
8. conduct a rebuttal-resistance edit;
9. complete the claim audit;
10. end with residual consequence.

## 🧪 Disconfirmation Discipline

Every story should actively seek evidence that:

- initiation was ordinary;
- predication preceded political naming;
- comparator treatment was consistent;
- career safeguards operated;
- private actors had unrelated reasons;
- no meaningful cumulative pressure occurred;
- legal relief fully restored the target;
- chronology was mistaken;
- the method did not repeat.

A framework that cannot be narrowed is not a reporting method.

It is a predetermined conclusion.

---

## ⚠️ Evidential Boundaries

This folder does not claim that:

- every political controversy belongs inside STFU;
- every target is innocent;
- every aligned action was coordinated;
- every private actor was directed by government;
- every public statement prompted official action;
- every unusual process was retaliatory;
- every legal defeat proves political motive;
- cumulative harm proves central planning;
- structural reporting can replace proving each case.

It makes a narrower methodological claim:

> **Political-hostility reporting becomes structurally accurate when it traces designation, vulnerability, routing, associative spread and behavioural output across institutions while preserving the evidential difference between orders, referrals, signalling, inference, pattern, alignment, chronology and unknown connection.**
