* The newsroom business-case note.
* Argues that AI search eats shallow, commodity coverage.
* Says the way around lower click-through is not more content, but better journalism.
* Covers:
    * timelines
    * document-led reporting
    * visual maps
    * satire
    * OSINT
    * investigations
    * local journalism
    * civic guides
* Explains why lazy op-eds are no longer enough.
* Key lines:
    * In the AI-search era, journalism cannot survive by being more summariseable.
    * The way around AI summaries is not more content. It is more journalism.
