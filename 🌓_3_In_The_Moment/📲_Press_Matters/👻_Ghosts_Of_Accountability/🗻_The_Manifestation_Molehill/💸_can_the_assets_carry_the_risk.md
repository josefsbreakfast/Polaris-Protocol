💸_can_the_assets_carry_the_risk.md

* The legal-financial risk note.
* Asks whether elite assets can still support the risk being taken.
* Not “is X insolvent?”
* Rather:
    * are assets liquid?
    * are they pledged?
    * are they encumbered?
    * are there civil judgments?
    * are there survivor claims?
    * are there sanctions/AML risks?
    * are there private-credit/pension/bank exposures?
* Includes Epstein contingent liability and Trump/Carroll-style civil judgment posture as examples of liability becoming collectible.
* Key line:
    * The balance sheet may exist. The risk capacity may not.
