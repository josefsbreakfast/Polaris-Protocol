The point is not causation.

🧿 Why This Looks Brittle

Several things are happening at once:

* a standards investigation is already live
* donor-provenance questions expand
* crypto lobbying questions enter the frame
* suspicious-activity / financial-intelligence language raises the stakes
* a resignation/by-election gambit attempts to convert scrutiny into mandate
* the media ecosystem initially defends the political asset
* the broadcaster employing the political asset is cutting staff
* rival-right attention appears to be moving through US platform circuits
* AI and crypto confidence remain background weather systems
* Epstein/civil-liability questions create a wider contingent-liability frame
* war and sanctions pressure keep digital money and state capacity in the strategic foreground
* satirical candidates and public ridicule puncture the seriousness of the mandate play

The useful conclusion is not:

Everything caused everything.

The useful conclusion is:

Several risk clocks are striking at once, and that makes the confidence architecture more fragile.
