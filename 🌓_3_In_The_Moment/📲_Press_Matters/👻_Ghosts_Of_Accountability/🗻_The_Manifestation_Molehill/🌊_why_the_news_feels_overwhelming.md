🌊_why_the_news_feels_overwhelming.md

* Explains why the news feels impossible to follow.
* Core point: people are not stupid; too many systems are knocking into each other.
* Separates structural overload from possible deliberate flooding-the-zone tactics.
* Introduces “track the clocks”:
    * court clock
    * regulator clock
    * parliament clock
    * market clock
    * war clock
    * election clock
    * donor clock
    * platform clock
* Helps journalists/readers slow down and sort signal from noise.
* Key line:
    * Do not chase every spark. Track the clocks.
