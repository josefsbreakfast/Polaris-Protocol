🎙️_how_to_cover_and_interview.md

* Practical journalist/editor guide.
* Covers interviewing spectacle politicians without becoming their stage.
* Avoids clinical “narcissist” language; focuses on behaviour and media mechanics.
* Core method:
    * do not argue with the persona
    * ask narrow documentary questions
    * make evasion visible
* Includes questions on:
    * who paid
    * who benefited
    * what was declared
    * what documents exist
    * what process applies
    * who carries liability
* Also covers headline discipline and quote framing.
* Key line:
    * Trust me bro is not a source.
