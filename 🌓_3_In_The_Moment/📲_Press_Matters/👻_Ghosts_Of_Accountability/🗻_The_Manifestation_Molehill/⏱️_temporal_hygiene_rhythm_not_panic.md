⏱️_temporal_hygiene_rhythm_not_panic.md

* The newsroom tempo note.
* Explains how algorithms distort the public’s sense of time.
* Argues that journalism should restore rhythm rather than intensify panic.
* Distinguishes:
    * monitor continuously
    * publish rhythmically
    * investigate patiently
* Introduces the “observation gap”: a short pause can reveal who is trying to flood the zone and why.
* Encourages scheduled updates, timelines, thresholds, and fewer panic-pieces.
* Key lines:
    * Do not let algorithms set the tempo of public understanding.
    * Speed reports the spark. Rhythm reveals the arson pattern.
