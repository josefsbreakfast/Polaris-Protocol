## 🧱 Compression Is Not Causation

The useful conclusion is not:

> Everything caused everything.

The useful conclusion is:

> Several risk clocks are striking at once, and that makes the confidence architecture more fragile.

Compression matters because fragile systems do not fail only when one giant thing happens.

They can fail when several medium-sized things become mutually legible.

A donor wobble becomes a media wobble.

A media wobble becomes a platform wobble.

A platform wobble becomes a market signal.

A market signal becomes a political signal.

A political signal becomes a legal signal.

A legal signal becomes a public-confidence problem.

The molehill does not need to explode.

It only needs people to notice it is not a mountain.

## 🧰 How Journalists Should Use This

Use the timeline to ask:

- Which clock is moving?
- What has materially changed?
- What is merely reaction?
- What is being displaced?
- Who benefits from the public feeling overwhelmed?
- What documents would clarify the issue?

- What would still matter if the main character stopped talking?

A timeline is not archive furniture.
