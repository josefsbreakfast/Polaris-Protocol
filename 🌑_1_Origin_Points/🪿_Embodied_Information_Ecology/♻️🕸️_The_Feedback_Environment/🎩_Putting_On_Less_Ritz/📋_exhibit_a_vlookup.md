# 📋 Exhibit A: VLOOKUP  
**First created:** 2026-09-16 | **Last updated:** 2026-09-20  
*Before Computer God, there was an office worker asking Excel to please look to the fucking left.*  

---

## 🛰️ Orientation

Before requesting permission to reorganise civilisation around **HUMAN SUPERINTELLIGENCE**, the technology industry is invited to reflect upon how long ordinary office workers spent explaining:  

**“I have a value here. Find the same value over there. Give me the corresponding value from that column.”**  

This was not an obscure requirement.

This was not frontier mathematics.

This was **office work**.

We eventually received XLOOKUP.

Thank you.

It is very good.

**What took you so fucking long?**

---

## 👈 The Computer Had To Be Taught Left

Classic VLOOKUP had a particularly magnificent constraint.

The thing you wanted to find had to be in the leftmost column of the lookup table, and the value you wanted returned had to be somewhere to its right.

Which produced interactions of approximately this intellectual calibre:

> **User:** The answer is literally in the cell to the left.
>
> **Excel:** Impossible journey.
>
> **User:** Can you just—
>
> **Excel:** Restructure your data.

The function could travel **RIGHTWARDS ONLY**, like a tiny bureaucrat who had been expressly forbidden from turning around.

So office workers learned workarounds.

They rearranged tables.

They built INDEX/MATCH formulas.

They memorised column numbers.

They discovered that inserting a column could make yesterday’s perfectly sensible formula return today’s entirely different answer.

They learned the significance of `$` through suffering.

They occasionally discovered that approximate matching had entered the chat.

All because the underlying human request—

**find this thing and return the corresponding thing**

—had been implemented according to the computer’s preferred table geometry rather than the user’s actual task.

Before superintelligence, lads:

**the computer had to be taught LEFT.**

---

## 📊 Sandra Did Not Request AGI

The larger productivity lesson is not that VLOOKUP was useless.

VLOOKUP was enormously useful.

That is precisely the point.

A slightly awkward tool, used constantly by enormous numbers of people, can impose a spectacular cumulative burden without any individual inconvenience looking important enough to qualify as technological transformation.

Multiply:

**small friction × millions of workers × repeated use × decades**

and suddenly the boring interface problem was a productivity problem all along.

Sandra did not request artificial general intelligence.

Sandra requested a lookup that could look left.

Please establish that you understand the distinction before proceeding to Computer God.

---

## ✨ And Then XLOOKUP Arrived

Eventually the conversation became approximately:

> **Excel:** What are you looking for?
>
> **User:** This.
>
> **Excel:** Where should I look?
>
> **User:** Here.
>
> **Excel:** What should I return?
>
> **User:** That.
>
> **Excel:** Either side?
>
> **User:** **YES, YOU BEAUTIFUL BASTARD.**

That is a good productivity tool.

The computer accommodates the task.

The worker does not have to reorganise the information merely because the software has opinions about which direction a lookup ought to travel.

This is the standard against which the current AI proposition should occasionally be measured.

Not:

**How close are we to artificial superintelligence?**

But:

**Did Sandra get her afternoon back?**

---

## 📎 Before Computer God

America, respectfully:

Do not tell us you have made **COMPUTER GOD** when some of us are old enough to remember spending a non-trivial portion of our working lives negotiating with a spreadsheet because the answer was on the wrong fucking side.

Do not give us disembodied Clippy.

Do not require a conversational relationship with the lookup function.

Do not build a data centre so that a probabilistic paperclip can ask Sandra what she means by “corresponding value”.

Just make the routine task work.

Reliably.

Audibly.

Interruptibly.

With provenance where it matters.

And preferably **in both fucking directions**.
