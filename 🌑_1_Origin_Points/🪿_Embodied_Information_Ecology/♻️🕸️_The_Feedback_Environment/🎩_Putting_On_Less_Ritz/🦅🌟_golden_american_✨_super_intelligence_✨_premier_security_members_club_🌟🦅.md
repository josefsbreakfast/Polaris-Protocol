Welcome to the:

# 🦅🌟 GOLDEN AMERICAN ✨ SUPER INTELLIGENCE ✨ PREMIER SECURITY MEMBERS CLUB 🌟🦅

Membership carries responsibilities.

The basic architecture is deliberately simple:

1. qualifying companies pay a predictable **Premier Membership Contribution**, calculated against a carefully defined measure of economic profit;
2. every company begins from the same applicable statutory formula rather than negotiating its own bespoke arrangement;
3. companies can earn reductions against that contribution through independently verified socially beneficial performance;
4. performance is represented publicly through a **ten-star accreditation system**;
5. the ten stars cannot all be farmed from one convenient area of corporate behaviour;
6. annual priorities respond to measured changes in where technological harms are actually landing;
7. companies maintain public online portfolios with quarterly evidence updates;
8. the accreditation therefore becomes both a fiscal mechanism and a public information mechanism;
9. once a company has achieved the full ten stars, further rewards require **prosocial reinvestment of capital**;
10. advanced rewards become increasingly prestigious and increasingly economically useless.

The result is not:

> Pay more because you are successful.

It is:

> **Premier membership has premier responsibilities.**

And, eventually:

> **You have behaved yourselves magnificently. You may have a cape.**

## 1. 🦅 The Premier Membership Contribution

Begin with a substantial but predictable baseline contribution.

The precise tax/accounting base needs separate technical design.

The intention is something approximately like a defined percentage of qualifying pre-distribution economic profit, calculated across the relevant consolidated corporate group.

The important principles are:

- predictable;
- difficult to game;
- applicable according to published rules;
- resistant to profit shifting and artificial restructuring;
- not individually negotiated according to who has the best lobbyists;
- sufficiently substantial that earned reductions actually matter;
- never reducible to zero merely through accreditation.

This is the **membership price before earned reductions**.

Nobody is being punished for becoming successful.

Congratulations.

Your invoice is enclosed.

## 3. ⭐ Stars Are Not Interchangeable Good-Boy Points

Ten stars does **not** mean:

> accumulate ten units of goodness however convenient.

The framework borrows conceptually from something much closer to the Duke of Edinburgh Award.

Achievement requires breadth.

Companies cannot put all their resources into one easy or reputationally convenient domain while behaving appallingly elsewhere.

Possible standing domains include:

- accessibility and disability inclusion;
- labour and workplace freedom;
- civil rights and non-discrimination;
- consumer protection;
- privacy and informational autonomy;
- child protection;
- environmental and infrastructure responsibility;
- security and resilience;
- anti-corruption;
- whistleblower protection;
- public accountability;
- independent evaluation;
- supply-chain responsibility;
- creator and intellectual-property protections;
- incident reporting and institutional learning.

The final architecture does not necessarily require every domain to produce exactly one star.

The point is structural:

> **rounded institutional competence beats strategic virtue farming.**

No:

> We planted seventeen million trees, therefore please do not inspect our employment practices.

## 4. 🎯 The Annual American Excellence Priorities

The categories can remain reasonably stable.

**The focus changes.**

Every assessment cycle should identify where the evidence indicates that technological activity is currently producing the most significant or concentrated harms.

This might identify, for example:

- particularly affected populations;
- particularly severe harm mechanisms;
- rapidly emerging technological risks;
- unusually large disparities;
- neglected problems;
- systemic failures appearing across several companies.

Rather than permanently hard-coding every priority, the system can establish an annual **American Excellence Priority Set**.

The priority-identification process should be metrics-driven and transparent.

Possible evidence streams include:

- regulator complaints;
- litigation and judgments;
- accessibility failures;
- employment outcomes;
- incident reporting;
- independent audits;
- consumer complaints;
- security events;
- environmental impacts;
- public-sector deployment outcomes;
- civil-rights disparities;
- research evidence;
- whistleblower information;
- affected-community evidence.

AI can assist with analysing the fucking enormous evidence base.

AI does not independently decide which human beings matter.

Computational systems can identify patterns, concentrations, anomalies and candidate priorities.

Accountable institutions determine the final priorities through transparent processes capable of challenge and review.

If the technology industry believes modern information environments move too quickly for traditional regulatory analysis:

**excellent.**

> **What are you doing with your surplus institutional capacity?**

- accessibility infrastructure;
- affected communities;
- worker transition and training;
- public-interest technical infrastructure;
- independent research;
- open safety infrastructure;
- environmental remediation;
- digital public goods;
- security tools available beyond the company itself;
- neglected annual-priority problems;
- shared standards;

# CORPORATE PHILANTHROPY BULLSHIT SIMULATOR

- additionality;
