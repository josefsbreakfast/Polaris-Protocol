# ➰ The Psychonetic Loop

**First created:** 2026-10-01 \| **Last updated:** 2026-10-01\
*The metaphorical opium of hyperreal capitalist realism: how successful
adaptation can make a system feel survivable, navigable, reasonable, or
even liberating from inside the position it rewards.*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🛰️ Orientation

The **psychonetic loop** is a cybernetic model for a particular problem
of agency.

A person acts inside a system. The system responds. Some responses hurt;
some reward; some teach. The person adapts. Successful adaptations
become evidence about how the world works. That evidence alters later
behaviour. Later behaviour produces further system responses.

Round we go.

The interesting bit is not simply that people can be persuaded by
capitalism, institutions, fame, status, money, ideology, professional
culture, or the people around them. That would be much less exciting and
would require significantly fewer suspicious humanities-department
words.

The interesting bit is that **successful agency inside a system can
itself become part of the mechanism by which the system reproduces its
legitimacy in the person's internal model**.

A person may correctly learn:

> I can survive this.\
> I can negotiate this.\
> I can win here.\
> I can make this machinery work for me.

Those observations can all be true.

The psychonetic problem begins when repeated success feeds back into the
model:

> Therefore this system is navigable.\
> Therefore agency works.\
> Therefore people can protect themselves if they learn the rules.\
> Therefore the system is not fundamentally coercive; it merely requires
> competence, leverage, resilience, ownership, good advisers, or better
> choices.

The loop does not require stupidity.

It may work **because the person has learned something real**.

The distortion enters when a locally successful adaptation becomes
evidence about the ecology beyond the conditions that made that success
possible.

That is the little opium.

## 1. ➰ The Basic Loop

At its simplest:

``` text
SYSTEM CONDITIONS
      ↓
PERSON ACTS
      ↓
SYSTEM RESPONDS
      ↓
REWARD / PUNISHMENT / CONSTRAINT / FEEDBACK
      ↓
PERSON UPDATES THEIR MODEL
      ↓
LATER ACTION CHANGES
      ↓
SYSTEM RESPONDS AGAIN
      ↺
```

A **psychonetic loop** is therefore not merely a belief.

It is a **belief-action-feedback system**.

The important question is:

> **What returns, and what does the return change?**

In this model, what returns is evidence generated by interaction with
the system.

What it changes is the person's later model of:

-   what is possible;
-   what is dangerous;
-   what is normal;
-   what counts as freedom;
-   what counts as competence;
-   which strategies work;
-   which harms are avoidable;
-   which harms are simply the price of participation;
-   and how much agency other people ought to be able to exercise.

The updated model then changes later action.

That later action generates new evidence.

The loop closes.

## 2. 💊 The Metaphorical Opium

The phrase **"the metaphorical opium of hyperreal capitalist realism"**
is deliberately ridiculous.

Unfortunately, it is also useful.

The "opium" is not necessarily money.

It is not necessarily greed.

It is not necessarily luxury, fame, consumption, ideology, or conscious
belief that capitalism is morally good.

The opium can be **whatever reward makes the system experientially
coherent from inside a person's particular position**.

For one person it might be:

-   ownership;
-   mastery;
-   autonomy;
-   professional competence;
-   security;
-   status;
-   normality;
-   privacy;
-   meritocratic recognition;
-   belonging;
-   being seen as reasonable;
-   being allowed to keep making the work;
-   the ability to protect one's family;
-   the ability to say *no*;
-   or simply the repeated experience that **when I pull this lever,
    something actually happens**.

That last one matters.

Agency is pleasurable.

More than pleasurable: agency can be regulating. It can make an
otherwise hostile environment intelligible.

If the system repeatedly rewards a person's interventions, then **the
experience of efficacy becomes evidence in favour of the world-model
that produced the intervention**.

The person does not need to believe:

> Capitalism is wonderful.

They may only need to believe:

> I know how to handle this.

That can be enough.

## 3. 🪞 Hyperreal Capitalist Realism

The psychonetic loop becomes particularly interesting under **hyperreal
capitalist realism** because the person is not merely navigating
markets.

They are navigating representations of markets, representations of
agency, representations of authenticity, representations of rebellion,
representations of ownership, representations of intimacy, and
representations of themselves.

A successful artist may therefore simultaneously be:

-   a human being;
-   a worker;
-   an employer;
-   an intellectual-property holder;
-   a brand;
-   a commercial partner;
-   a symbolic object;
-   a media narrative;
-   a parasocial relationship;
-   a contractual counterparty;
-   and a representation of what successful resistance to the industry
    supposedly looks like.

The hyperreal layer matters because **resistance itself can become proof
that the system permits resistance**.

A person can genuinely fight an exploitative arrangement.

They can genuinely win.

They can genuinely acquire more control.

They can genuinely help change industry expectations.

And the victory can still feed a larger story:

> See? The machinery works if you become powerful enough to negotiate
> with it.

The rebellion is real.

So is the possibility that the rebellion becomes incorporated into the
system's account of itself.

## 4. 🧠 Successful Adaptation Is Not False Consciousness

The psychonetic loop should not become a clever-sounding way of saying:

> Everyone who disagrees with me has been brainwashed.

Absolutely not.

The model is useful precisely because it permits the person's
observations to be **locally accurate**.

Imagine:

``` text
PERSON A
has money + leverage + expertise + advisers + reputation
        ↓
negotiates successfully
        ↓
learns:
"negotiation protects my agency"
```

That learning may be completely correct.

The problem appears if it silently becomes:

``` text
"negotiation protects agency"
        ↓
"people can negotiate"
        ↓
"the system therefore provides meaningful agency"
```

The missing variable is **position**.

The system encountered by someone with extraordinary leverage is not
experientially identical to the system encountered by someone who is
replaceable, indebted, frightened, legally constrained, dependent on a
gatekeeper, under surveillance, or unable to absorb the cost of saying
no.

The psychonetic loop therefore asks:

> **Which part of the person's world-model is being accurately trained
> by their own experience, and which part is being over-generalised from
> the conditions of their position?**

That is much more useful than simply shouting *false consciousness* at
everybody until the seminar ends.

## 5. 🎛️ Agency Can Become The Drug

One particularly important psychonetic reward is
**agency-through-mastery**.

The loop looks like this:

``` text
SYSTEM HARMS PERSON
        ↓
PERSON LEARNS THE SYSTEM
        ↓
PERSON RESISTS SUCCESSFULLY
        ↓
PERSON GAINS POWER / OWNERSHIP / LEVERAGE
        ↓
SYSTEM BECOMES MORE NAVIGABLE
        ↓
NAVIGABILITY IS EXPERIENCED AS FREEDOM
        ↓
MASTERY BECOMES EVIDENCE THAT
THE SYSTEM CAN BE MASTERED
        ↓
FUTURE PROBLEMS ARE MORE LIKELY TO BE READ
THROUGH THE LANGUAGE OF STRATEGY,
COMPETENCE, LEVERAGE AND CONTROL
        ↺
```

This is not hypocrisy.

It does not require somebody to forget that they were harmed.

In fact, the original harm may become central to the loop:

> I know this system is dangerous because it hurt me.\
> I also know how to survive it because I beat it.

Both statements can coexist.

That is precisely why the resulting worldview may be unusually durable.

## 6. 🎤 Taylor Swift As A Candidate Configuration

Taylor Swift is useful here **as a candidate configuration, not as a
diagnosis**.

The interesting analytical fact is the combination of:

-   extraordinary commercial success;
-   highly developed artist-side infrastructure;
-   a public history of conflict over ownership and control;
-   later acquisition of substantially greater bargaining power;
-   and a public narrative in which authorship, ownership, strategic
    competence and autonomy matter enormously.

That makes **agency-through-mastery** a useful hypothesis for examining
the information environment around her.

The hypothesis is not:

> Taylor Swift believes capitalism is good.

Nor:

> Taylor Swift personally authors every institutional response
> associated with Taylor Swift.

Nor:

> Her advisers secretly control her.

It is:

> **If a person repeatedly experiences increased ownership, strategic
> competence and leverage as successful solutions to prior exploitation,
> those successes may reinforce a model in which sufficient agency
> appears to make the system navigable.**

That model may then shape how later problems are understood.

It may also coexist with lawyers, management, publicity, commercial
partners and other advisers whose professional task is to convert messy
human situations into manageable categories of risk.

The result can occasionally feel strange from outside:

``` text
HUMAN QUESTION
Someone was hurt. Why not simply acknowledge it?

INSTITUTIONAL QUESTION
What response produces the least additional exposure?

PSYCHONETIC QUESTION
What previous experiences have taught the decision-maker
what "responsible action" inside this system looks like?
```

Those are three different questions.

Do not collapse them.

## 7. 🎸 Ed Sheeran Does Not Have To Have Taylor's Loop

Two artists can occupy overlapping markets without occupying the same
psychonetic position.

Ed Sheeran and Taylor Swift may share:

-   listeners;
-   collaborators;
-   industry infrastructure;
-   commercial partners;
-   publicity norms;
-   legal norms;
-   and the general ecology of enormous global celebrity.

That does not mean the same reward keeps the system coherent for both of
them.

A Sheeran-shaped loop might involve some mixture of:

-   craft;
-   professionalism;
-   privacy;
-   loyalty;
-   normality;
-   being a decent bloke;
-   the ability to continue making music;
-   meritocratic self-understanding;
-   or something else entirely.

Those are **questions for evidence**, not conclusions supplied by the
model.

The point of the psychonetic loop is not to create one grand explanation
for famous people behaving weirdly.

It is to ask:

> **What does this particular system reliably give this particular
> person that teaches them how to understand the system next time?**

Different person.

Different reward.

Different loop.

## 8. 🟨 Britney Spears Is The Epistemic Warning Label

Britney Spears belongs in this model for a different reason.

She is not evidence that every celebrity who behaves unexpectedly is
secretly controlled.

She is evidence that **public visibility does not reveal the private
distribution of agency**.

That distinction matters.

A public figure may appear:

-   wealthy;
-   successful;
-   cheerful;
-   productive;
-   communicative;
-   professionally active;
-   surrounded by staff;
-   and apparently capable of making ordinary choices.

None of those observations, by themselves, tell an outside observer
exactly where decision rights sit.

The lesson is therefore not:

> Weird celebrity behaviour = coercive control.

The lesson is:

> **Do not infer autonomy from normality.**

And equally:

> **Do not infer coercion from ambiguity.**

This becomes especially important where people try to communicate from
inside a monitored or coercive environment.

## 9. 📡 The Signalling Problem

Suppose an outside group believes a person may be under coercive
control.

They invent a signal:

``` text
IF YOU NEED HELP
WEAR YELLOW
```

The person later wears yellow.

That observation alone cannot establish:

``` text
THE PERSON DELIBERATELY SENT THE SIGNAL.
```

But there is a second problem.

If the signal **was** deliberate and the coercive system can observe the
public response, successful communication may expose the channel.

``` text
PERSON
  ↓
AMBIGUOUS SIGNAL
  ↓
OUTSIDERS RECOGNISE SIGNAL
  ↓
OUTSIDERS RESPOND PUBLICLY
  ↓
CONTROLLING SYSTEM OBSERVES RESPONSE
  ↓
SYSTEM LEARNS THE SIGNAL
  ↓
FUTURE COMMUNICATION CONDITIONS CHANGE
```

At that point, the signal can be:

-   prohibited;
-   monitored;
-   imitated;
-   contradicted;
-   flooded with noise;
-   or used to identify who is listening.

This is **not the general psychonetic loop itself**.

It is a neighbouring surveillance/feedback mechanism.

Its importance here is methodological: it prevents the analyst from
assuming that observable behaviour provides transparent access to
agency.

Britney therefore functions as the little sign taped to the machine:

> **YOU DO NOT ACTUALLY KNOW WHO HAD WHICH DECISION RIGHT FROM THE
> OUTPUT ALONE.**

Useful sign.

Keep sign.

## 10. 🏢 The Organisation Is Not The Person

Celebrity makes this particularly easy to forget because one human face
can name an entire commercial system.

"Taylor Swift did X" may refer to:

-   Taylor Swift personally deciding something;
-   Taylor Swift approving a recommendation;
-   Taylor Swift delegating a category of decisions;
-   management acting within established authority;
-   publicity implementing a communications strategy;
-   lawyers advising against a response;
-   a commercial partner carrying out contractual functions;
-   a platform or promoter making an operational decision;
-   or several of these interacting.

The face remains singular.

The decision system is not.

This does **not** remove responsibility from the person whose
organisation it is.

Delegation is itself part of governance.

But analytical responsibility requires distinguishing:

``` text
FORMAL DECISION AUTHORITY
        ≠
INFORMATIONAL INFLUENCE
        ≠
PROFESSIONAL ADVICE
        ≠
DELEGATED OPERATION
        ≠
CONTRACTUAL CONSTRAINT
        ≠
COERCIVE CONTROL
```

If we cannot distinguish them from available evidence, we mark the
uncertainty.

We do not fill in the arrow because the story would be tidier.

## 11. 🧮 The Psychonetic Questions

When a person's behaviour seems oddly inconsistent with their apparent
values, the useful questions are not immediately:

> Who got to them?

or:

> Were they lying all along?

Try:

### What is the reward?

What does this system reliably give them?

Money is allowed as an answer.

It is simply not the only answer.

### What is the successful adaptation?

What did they learn to do that materially improved their position?

### What returned?

What response told them that the adaptation worked?

### What changed?

Did the return alter:

-   their behaviour;
-   their expectations;
-   their tolerance for risk;
-   their model of other people's options;
-   their understanding of responsibility;
-   or their sense of what counts as freedom?

### What became invisible?

Which conditions of their own success are easy to stop noticing once
those conditions become normal?

### What is being generalised?

Does a strategy that works from their position become a theory of how
the system works for everybody?

### Who preprocesses reality?

Who decides what information reaches the person and in what form?

This may include:

-   friends;
-   family;
-   staff;
-   management;
-   lawyers;
-   publicists;
-   security;
-   executives;
-   political advisers;
-   platforms;
-   audiences;
-   metrics;
-   or professional peers.

### Where are the decision rights?

Who can:

-   decide;
-   veto;
-   recommend;
-   delay;
-   implement;
-   refuse;
-   escalate;
-   or simply make an option seem professionally unserious?

### What evidence would falsify the loop?

If the answer is "nothing", we have stopped analysing and started
writing mythology.

Put the whiteboard marker down.

## 12. ⚠️ Failure Modes

### The Evil Billionaire Shortcut

Wealth can dramatically change a person's relationship to constraint.

It does not tell us their internal mechanism.

``` text
RICH
≠
SAME PSYCHONETIC LOOP
```

### The Poor Little Celebrity Shortcut

Professional influence is not automatically coercion.

Advice is not control.

Delegation is not captivity.

A powerful person can receive extensive advice and still own the final
decision.

### The Britney Shortcut

Britney Spears demonstrates that extreme hidden constraints can exist
behind highly visible celebrity performance.

She does not establish hidden coercion in somebody else's case.

``` text
POSSIBLE
≠
PRESENT
```

### The PR Did It Shortcut

"PR" is not a tiny wizard living behind a curtain.

Publicity is one input into an organisational decision system.

Sometimes its advice wins.

Sometimes legal wins.

Sometimes management wins.

Sometimes the artist wins.

Sometimes everybody agrees.

Sometimes the supposedly safe decision is simply a bad decision.

### The Capitalism Explains Everything Shortcut

A theory that explains every possible outcome explains nothing
particularly well.

The psychonetic loop requires an actual return pathway:

``` text
ACTION / STATE
→ SYSTEM RESPONSE
→ MODEL UPDATE
→ CHANGED LATER ACTION
```

If we cannot identify what returns and what changes, we do not have the
loop.

We merely have vibes wearing spectacles.

## 13. ♻️ Why This Lives In Cybernetics

The central mechanism is feedback.

Remove celebrity and it still works.

Remove capitalism and versions of it still work.

Remove surveillance and it still works.

Remove a structured information environment and the general mechanism
can still be described.

Remove the return pathway and the concept collapses.

Therefore:

``` text
PRIMARY:
♻️ CYBERNETICS
```

The general form is:

``` text
PERSON ACTS
→ SYSTEM RETURNS CONSEQUENCES
→ PERSON UPDATES MODEL
→ UPDATED MODEL CHANGES LATER ACTION
→ NEW ACTION GENERATES NEW CONSEQUENCES
↺
```

Other Polaris domains become necessary in particular applications.

For example:

``` text
WHEN INFORMATIONAL CONDITIONS THEMSELVES
ARE CHANGED BY THE LOOP
→ ♻️🕸️ The Feedback Environment

WHEN OBSERVATION PRODUCES CONSEQUENTIAL RESPONSE
→ ♻️🧿 Observation Becomes Intervention

WHEN THE PERSON'S EMBODIED STATE PARTICIPATES
IN THE RETURN PATHWAY
→ 🫀♻️ The Body In The Loop
```

But the psychonetic loop itself does not need all of them.

No smuggling 🐣 into the room because we learned several nouns.

## 14. 🧪 Working Definition

> **A psychonetic loop is a recursive belief-action-feedback process in
> which a person's interactions with a system generate consequences that
> update their model of that system, altering later behaviour and
> thereby generating further evidence that may reinforce, refine, or
> destabilise the model.**

Under hyperreal capitalist realism, the particularly interesting form
is:

> **A psychonetic loop in which successful adaptation produces enough
> agency, security, mastery, status, belonging, or legibility that the
> system becomes experientially coherent from inside the rewarded
> position --- allowing local success to function as evidence for the
> wider navigability or legitimacy of the system.**

Or, for the humanities department after midnight:

> **The metaphorical opium of hyperreal capitalist realism.**

There.

Much better.
