## 🧿 Analysis / Content  

### 1. Coercion Is a Skillset  
Perpetrators excel in:
- narrative control,  
- environmental manipulation,  
- grooming,  
- impression management,  
- reading vulnerability,  
- exploiting system gaps,  
- controlling information flow.

These are skills — not accidents.

### 2. System Harm Enables Perpetrator Harm  
Where systems have:
- untraceable flagging routes,  
- anonymous escalation,  
- lack of oversight,  
- fear-based workplace cultures,  
- gender bias,  
- no correction mechanisms,

perpetrators thrive effortlessly.

### 3. Why Victim-Blaming Is Structural  
Victim-blaming:
- protects perpetrators,  
- hides institutional failures,  
- relieves systems of responsibility,  
- exaggerates individual choice over structural constraint.

### 4. Shifting the Lens  
Assess:
- “How was this allowed?”  
- “What system gaps were exploited?”  
- “Who benefitted from silence?”  
- “What institutional conditions enabled this?”

We fix harm by studying perpetrators — not victims.
