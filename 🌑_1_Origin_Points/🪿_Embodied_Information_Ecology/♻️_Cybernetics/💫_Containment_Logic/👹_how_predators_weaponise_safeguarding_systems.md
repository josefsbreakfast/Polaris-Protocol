## 🧿 Analysis / Content  

### 1. **Safeguarding Is Built on Asymmetry**  
Safeguarding frameworks assume:
- the *reporter* is credible,  
- the *subject* is vulnerable or risky,  
- ambiguity must be escalated,  
- acting is safer than not acting.

High-status abusers understand this intuitively.

They exploit the structure, not the people inside it.

---

### 2. **Vagueness Is the Perfect Weapon**  
Predators rarely make explicit accusations.

They use phrases like:
- “I had a concern.”  
- “Something seemed off.”  
- “There was unusual contact.”  
- “I might be wrong but…”  

No details.  
No evidence.  
Just a *tone*.

And safeguarding systems **treat the tone itself as evidence**.
