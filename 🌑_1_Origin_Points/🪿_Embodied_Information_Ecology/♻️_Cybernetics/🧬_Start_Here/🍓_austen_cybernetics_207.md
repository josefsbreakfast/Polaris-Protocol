## 🛰️ Orientation

We have spent six nodes observing information systems inside
*Persuasion*.

We have watched:

-   history persist;
-   models alter behaviour;
-   behaviour become evidence;
-   proxies fail;
-   perturbation reveal latent capability;
-   metadata produce trust;
-   strategic actors exploit signals;
-   accurate models create power.

There is now one obvious problem.

Somebody had to notice all of this.

Austen is modelling:

-   Anne;
-   Wentworth;
-   Sir Walter;
-   Lady Russell;
-   Louisa;
-   the Crofts;
-   Mr Elliot;
-   marriage;
-   inheritance;
-   money;
-   rank;
-   reputation;
-   gender;
-   maintenance;
-   professional competence;
-   social signalling;
-   information flow.

But Austen is not hovering above Georgian Britain in a cybernetic
observation satellite.

She is writing from inside the society she describes.

And she can still look at the machinery and say, approximately:

# MY BROTHER IN CHRIST, THIS FUCKING GEAR IS ON BACKWARDS.

## 👁️ There is no view from nowhere

Introductory diagrams often place the observer outside:

``` text
OBSERVER
   ↓
SYSTEM
```

Human systems are rarely that tidy.

The observer may be:

-   affected by the system;
-   classified by it;
-   constrained by it;
-   rewarded by it;
-   dependent upon it;
-   participating in it.

The observer is one of the things happening.

## ♻️ Second-order cybernetics

Second-order cybernetics asks us to inspect the observer as part of the
observational system.

Questions include:

-   Who is observing?
-   From where?
-   With what categories?
-   With what interests?
-   Under what constraints?
-   How does observation affect the system?
-   How does membership in the system affect observation?

Austen was not secretly a twentieth-century cyberneticist.

This is a retrospective teaching lens.

But *Persuasion* is extraordinarily interested in people trying to
understand systems while living inside them.

## 🏛️ Embedded critique

A weak model of criticism imagines:

``` text
PERSON ESCAPES SYSTEM
        ↓
PERSON BECOMES OBJECTIVE
        ↓
PERSON CRITICISES SYSTEM
```

Most human knowledge does not work like that.

People often understand systems because they have:

-   lived within them;
-   learned their rules;
-   depended upon them;
-   experienced contradictions;
-   watched them operate repeatedly.

Embeddedness can create blind spots.

It can also create knowledge.

The useful question is:

> **What does this position allow the observer to see, and what might it
> prevent them from seeing?**

## 🍓 Kellynch is not an abstract system

Sir Walter has title to Kellynch.

Anne has a model of Kellynch.

The Crofts inhabit Kellynch.

Mr Elliot anticipates inheriting Kellynch.

For Anne:

-   home;
-   memory;
-   landscape;
-   continuity;
-   relationship.

For Sir Walter:

-   ancestry;
-   rank;
-   prestige.

For Mr Elliot:

-   inheritance;
-   position;
-   future asset.

For the Crofts:

-   an actual place to inhabit.

Same physical system.

Different information environments.

Meaning emerges through relationships among observer, body, history,
place, power and need.

## 🧹 And somebody has to do the fucking dusting

A country house does not remain:

``` text
BEAUTIFUL ANCESTRAL ESTATE
```

through metadata.

It requires maintenance.

So does:

-   a household;
-   a marriage;
-   a family;
-   a social network;
-   an institution.

Maintenance work is often:

-   repetitive;
-   distributed;
-   low-status;
-   feminised;
-   poorly recorded;
-   taken for granted precisely when performed successfully.

The successful output is:

> nothing went wrong.

Which makes the labour producing that output extremely easy to
disappear.

# COMPENSATE ANNE FOR HER LABOUR.

Eighteenth-/early-nineteenth-century literature:

> Best we can do is Captain Wentworth.

Anne:

# I WILL TAKE HIM.

## 🧰 Maintenance is information work

Dusting is funny because it is concrete.

The larger category includes:

-   remembering;
-   scheduling;
-   noticing;
-   anticipating;
-   checking;
-   coordinating;
-   smoothing conflict;
-   maintaining relationships;
-   preparing;
-   following up.

The competent maintainer knows:

``` text
what needs doing
+
when it needs doing
+
who needs to know
+
what has already happened
+
what happens if nobody does it
```

If the labour succeeds, the surrounding system may perceive:

``` text
NOTHING HAPPENED
```

Correct.

**Because somebody fucking handled it.**

## 🫥 Successful maintenance can become invisible

A serious observability problem:

``` text
maintenance succeeds
        ↓
failure does not occur
        ↓
observer sees no failure
        ↓
observer infers little maintenance was required
```

Oops.

Anne repeatedly occupies this kind of position.

Her competence can be simultaneously:

-   essential;
-   visible to careful observers;
-   undervalued by the prestige hierarchy.

Austen makes the reader notice what the surrounding system is bad at
pricing.

## 🔖 The system says rank

Sir Walter cares intensely about title, lineage, beauty and precedence.

Austen can place:

``` text
FORMAL STATUS
```

beside:

``` text
ACTUAL JUDGEMENT
ACTUAL COMPETENCE
ACTUAL USEFULNESS
ACTUAL CHARACTER
```

and let the mismatch sit there.

Sir Walter can demonstrate the problem himself.

## ⚖️ Immanent critique

**Immanent critique** tests a system partly against its own stated
values, purposes or standards.

The hierarchy says rank signifies value.

Okay.

Sir Walter.

Next question.

The marriage market treats established status as evidence of
suitability.

Okay.

Mr Elliot.

Interesting.

Young Wentworth is treated as an imprudent prospect because his future
is insecure.

Okay.

Let's run the longitudinal follow-up.

Oh.

Women are expected to possess judgement, prudence, constancy and proper
feeling.

Okay.

Why was Anne's judgement given so little authority over one of the most
consequential intimate decisions of her life?

You do not always need to attack the apparatus from Mars.

# LET THE APPARATUS EMBARRASS ITSELF.

## ⚓ And then the Navy shows up

The naval characters complicate hereditary valuation.

This is not:

> Navy = meritocracy = good.

The Navy is an institution of war and empire, and professional
advancement does not abolish hierarchy.

But the novel places inherited prestige beside another route to status:

-   professional competence;
-   service;
-   experience;
-   earned fortune;
-   demonstrated capability.

Wentworth returns materially transformed.

The Crofts can rent Kellynch.

Sir Walter owns the ancestral estate but cannot comfortably sustain his
existing lifestyle there.

# HOW'S EVERYONE FEELING ABOUT THE NAVY NOW.

Competing valuation systems have become visible.

## 💰 Money is not fake either

Austen is not saying:

> love is pure and material conditions do not matter.

Money matters.

Housing matters.

Inheritance matters.

Security matters.

Employment matters.

Young Wentworth's insecurity was a real consideration.

The interesting question is:

> **Which material risks are being assessed, by whom, using which
> variables, with what confidence, and whose future bears the
> consequences of error?**

## 🏛️ The drawing room is infrastructure

We should refuse the old distinction between:

``` text
SERIOUS SYSTEMS
```

and:

``` text
LADIES HAVING FEELINGS IN DRAWING ROOMS
```

The drawing room is where people negotiate:

-   marriage;
-   property;
-   inheritance;
-   kinship;
-   reputation;
-   dependency;
-   domestic labour;
-   sexual relationships;
-   housing;
-   economic security;
-   social alliances.

My brother in Christ:

# THE DRAWING ROOM IS WHERE THE PROPERTY SETTLEMENT IS HAPPENING.

Courtship fiction can function as systems analysis because the
apparently personal environment is where multiple institutional
structures converge.

## 🧹 Who gets the dusting duties?

Marriage does not merely allocate:

``` text
ROMANTIC PARTNER
```

It reorganises household management, care, social administration, kin
obligations and domestic labour.

So when a system identifies Anne as capable, discreet and responsible,
we must ask:

> **Who benefits from Anne continuing to perform those
> characteristics?**

Is this a marriage proposal or are you assigning her the fucking dusting
duties?

The polished output:

``` text
RESPECTABLE HOUSEHOLD
```

may depend on enormous quantities of maintenance labour that disappear
from the prestige model.

## 🛰️ What the apparatus can see

The social apparatus is extremely good at observing:

-   title;
-   family;
-   fortune;
-   marriageability;
-   reputation;
-   beauty;
-   precedence.

It is less consistently good at observing or rewarding:

-   maintenance;
-   adaptive competence;
-   quiet judgement;
-   longitudinal constancy;
-   care;
-   problem prevention.

Observability is partly produced by the categories the system has
decided to record.

Anne does not suddenly become valuable when Wentworth notices her
competence.

She was valuable before his model could represent it.

## 📚 The novel changes the sensor

The surrounding social system may under-observe certain variables.

The novel can direct attention toward:

-   what Anne notices;
-   what she remembers;
-   what she suppresses;
-   what she does;
-   what other characters fail to notice;
-   discrepancies between formal status and actual usefulness.

The literary form changes the observation environment.

The book makes quiet variables observable.

## 🪞 The observer is still situated

Austen's insight does not make Austen omniscient.

An embedded observer still has:

-   historical location;
-   cultural assumptions;
-   social position;
-   available categories;
-   blind spots.

So we can ask:

-   What could Austen see particularly well?
-   What assumptions remain embedded?
-   Which structures become visible?
-   Which remain background?
-   What can later observers notice that Austen did not?

The observer can observe the apparatus.

Later observers can observe the observer.

Welcome to recursion.

## 🔁 Observation all the way down

``` text
Anne observes Wentworth.
Wentworth observes Anne.
Anne models Wentworth observing Anne.
Mr Elliot observes Anne.
Anne observes Mr Elliot.
Mrs Smith supplies observations of Mr Elliot.
Austen observes the social field.
The reader observes Austen's representation.
Polaris observes the reader observing Austen observing the Elliots.

Cybernetics:
please stop adding observers.
```

No. 😌

There is no magical final observer whose model becomes reality itself.

Good analysis does not eliminate perspective.

It makes perspective available for inspection.

## 🧬 Reflexivity

Human social systems encounter representations of themselves.

People encounter stories about:

-   marriage;
-   class;
-   masculinity;
-   femininity;
-   respectable behaviour;

and those representations can enter the information environment through
which later people model themselves.

This does not mean one novel straightforwardly causes a particular
social outcome.

It means cultural representation is one of the ways societies observe
and reproduce themselves.

Sometimes the representation is extremely rude.

## 🔥 Punk from inside the drawing room

There is a model of transgression that requires standing outside,
rejecting everything and burning the institution down.

Austen offers another possibility.

Know the rules.

Know why they exist.

Know the people who believe in them.

Know what useful functions they perform.

Then place Sir Walter Elliot in the middle of the page and let him talk.

That is punk as fuck.

> I know why this machine exists.
>
> I know what I receive from this machine.
>
> I know the people operating this machine.
>
> I know which bits are useful.
>
> **AND I CAN STILL SEE THAT THIS FUCKING GEAR IS ON BACKWARDS.**

## 🛠️ Critique does not require total rejection

A system can contain useful functions and still contain:

-   bad incentives;
-   misweighted variables;
-   hidden labour;
-   perverse feedback;
-   obsolete assumptions.

The choice is not:

``` text
EVERYTHING IS FINE
```

or:

``` text
DESTROY THE ENTIRE MACHINE
```

We can ask:

``` text
What is this for?
What works?
Who benefits?
Who maintains it?
Who bears the cost?
What information is missing?
Which feedback loops are broken?
Which gear is on backwards?
```

## 📏 Let the apparatus embarrass itself

A practical method:

1.  identify what the system claims to value;
2.  inspect what it actually measures or rewards;
3.  identify invisible maintenance;
4.  identify proxies;
5.  look for contradictions;
6.  inspect the observer;
7.  let the apparatus embarrass itself.

Often surprisingly efficient.

## 🕸️ Back to Embodied Information Ecology

The *Persuasion* sequence has shown that information is not merely
transmitted.

It is:

-   interpreted;
-   embodied;
-   remembered;
-   suppressed;
-   performed;
-   manipulated;
-   spatially situated;
-   historically accumulated;
-   acted upon.

Anne does not possess:

``` text
KELLYNCH_DATA.csv
```

She knows Kellynch through living.

She knows Wentworth through history.

She knows her family through accumulated interaction.

Her body, memory, attachments and environment are part of the
information architecture.

## 🌉 Two literacies, one join

This little module is deliberately a meeting point.

Someone arriving from systems work may already understand:

-   feedback;
-   proxies;
-   observability;
-   perturbation;
-   objective functions.

Someone arriving from Austen may already understand exactly why
Wentworth's model is wrong, why Anne's restraint is misleading, why Sir
Walter's value system is absurd, and why Mr Elliot is dangerous.

Neither literacy needs to be demoted into an illustration of the other.

The useful move is interoperability.

> **Two knowledge traditions have been describing some of the same
> fucking problems. Put them at the same table.**

## ⚓🌙 High Priestess / mermaid diagram

Anne and Wentworth also give us a final image for the interface itself.

Anne holds contextual, embodied, relational and longitudinal
information.

Wentworth is highly responsive to explicit operational information.

The answer is not:

> MAKE HIGH PRIESTESS USE SPREADSHEET.

Nor:

> THROW SPREADSHEET INTO SEA; VIBES SHALL GOVERN.

The answer is:

# BUILD THE FUCKING INTERFACE.

At Lyme, Anne becomes legible in Wentworth's operational language.

During the Harville conversation, Wentworth finally listens closely
enough to receive Anne's language of memory and constancy.

Then he writes the letter.

Mermaid diagram updated:

``` text
Anne → still loves me
me → catastrophically still loves Anne
Louisa → married to buddy
Mr Elliot → fuck off
action → WRITE LETTER IMMEDIATELY
```

**SYSTEMS INTEROPERABLE.**
