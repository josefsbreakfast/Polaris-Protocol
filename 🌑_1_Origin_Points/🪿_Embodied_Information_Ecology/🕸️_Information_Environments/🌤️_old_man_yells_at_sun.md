# 🌤️ Old Man Yells At Sun

**First created:** 2026-10-03 | **Last updated:** 2026-10-03  
*Doxxes Senator and Intelligence Chief.*

---

## 🛰️ Orientation

There are political stories which arrive already carrying their own explanatory diagram.

On 3 October 2026, Donald Trump escalated his campaign for permanent daylight saving time by publishing what appeared to be the personal mobile number of Senator Tom Cotton and encouraging people to call him. Cotton is not the United States' intelligence chief; he is a senator and the chair of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence. The distinction matters. The byline above is a joke. The underlying information-security problem is not.

The dispute itself was about clocks.

Trump wants the United States to remain on daylight saving time rather than putting the clocks back. He has explicitly invoked golfers, tennis players, hunters, farmers, crime, stress and the inconvenience of changing clocks. He also shared a letter from the PGA Tour supporting permanent daylight saving time. Cotton objects in part because later winter sunrises would leave Arkansas children travelling to school in darkness.

This is therefore the story of a President wanting more usable evening daylight, encountering the inconvenient fact that changing the clock does not create additional sunlight, finding a senator standing between him and the preferred clock convention, and publishing the senator's apparent private number to a mass audience.

It is very funny.

It is also an unusually clean Information Environments case study.

## 🌤️ Old Man Yells At Sun

The internet already has a visual language for this.

**Old Man Yells at Cloud.**

The joke works because the grievance is directed at something vast, impersonal and completely indifferent to the grievance. The cloud is not participating in the argument. It cannot be persuaded. It cannot apologise. It cannot amend its behaviour after representations have been received.

The 2026 version is:

``` text
OLD MAN YELLS AT SUN
          │
          ▼
   Sun continues being Sun
          │
          ▼
OLD MAN IDENTIFIES SENATOR
RESPONSIBLE FOR SUN
          │
          ▼
OLD MAN POSTS SENATOR'S
PHONE NUMBER
```

The policy question underneath this is real. Permanent daylight saving time would move an hour of clock-labelled daylight from the morning side of people's schedules to the evening side. People can reasonably disagree about whether that is desirable.

What it cannot do is create another hour of sunlight.

That distinction is the first useful mechanism.

This is where the joke becomes a control-surface problem.

If the desired state is **more golf occurring while the Sun is up**, there are several conceivable variables:


A population-wide change to the representational system can certainly change when socially scheduled activity overlaps with daylight. It does not change the physical quantity of daylight.

The joke is not that permanent daylight saving time is inherently irrational.

The joke is that the Sun is being described as though Tom Cotton has an hour of it in his desk drawer.

## 🌊 Cnut Already Ran This Experiment

The famous story of Cnut and the tide is often remembered backwards.

Cnut commands the tide not to come in. The tide comes in anyway. In the traditional telling, the point is not that Cnut sincerely believes he controls the sea. It is that even a powerful king cannot command nature.

``` text
CNUT

👑 → commands 🌊
🌊 → remains 🌊
👑 → political authority
     has limits
```

The modern inversion is considerably stranger:

``` text
ANTI-CNUT

👔 → wants ☀️ later
☀️ → remains ☀️
👔 → which senator
     is responsible?
          │
          ▼
      TOM COTTON
          │
          ▼
          📱
```

Cnut's demonstration says that sovereignty ends somewhere.

The Sun War asks what happens when an actor encounters an immovable constraint and begins searching the surrounding system for something human which *can* be moved.

The Sun cannot receive pressure.

Earth's rotation cannot be threatened.

A clock cannot answer a telephone.

Tom Cotton can.

## 📱 The Joke Stops Here For A Moment

On 3 October, Trump publicly encouraged people to call a number he said belonged to Cotton. The Associated Press described it as appearing to be Cotton's personal cellphone number. AP called it; nobody answered and the voicemail box was full. Reuters called the number and reached a voicemail box which did not identify itself as Cotton's.

That evidential distinction should stay intact.

What is established is serious enough without inventing anything further.

Cotton is a United States senator and chair of the Senate Intelligence Committee. A President publicly associated an apparently private telephone number with him and directed a mass audience towards it during a political dispute.

That does **not**, by itself, establish that classified information was compromised, that Cotton's device was accessed, or that any foreign intelligence service exploited the disclosure.

It does create a legitimate information-security question:

> What happens when an authoritative and extremely high-reach source
> publicly authenticates an identifier associated with a high-value
> national-security figure?

The privileged user belongs inside the threat model.

Information security cannot concern itself only with the imagined junior employee secretly carrying information out of the building.

Sometimes the call is coming from inside the fucking house.

Sometimes the house has posted the caller's number.

## 🕸️ Information Does Not Keep The Sender's Meaning

The central Information Environments mechanism is not Trump's motive.

It is what happens to information **after release**.

An originator can publish information for one purpose while multiple receiving systems assign it entirely different values.

``` text
                    ACTOR
                      │
          publishes / authenticates
                      │
                      ▼
              PUBLIC INFORMATION
                ENVIRONMENT
                      │
        ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
        ▼             ▼             ▼
     SUPPORTER     JOURNALIST     ADVERSARIAL
                                  OBSERVER
        │             │             │
        ▼             ▼             ▼
    meaning A      meaning B      meaning C
```

The sender does not get to preserve one universal meaning around the information after circulation.

One receiver may encounter:

> **A number the President wants me to call about daylight saving
> time.**

Another may encounter:

> **An apparently private number publicly attributed by the President to
> a senior senator.**

Another may encounter:

> **A contact identifier worth correlating against other datasets.**

The underlying object has not changed.

The receiving system has.

This is why provenance matters. Trump did not merely reproduce a string of digits. By saying *this is Tom Cotton's number*, he attached an attribution to the identifier. That attribution itself can have informational value.

The same information can therefore perform functions never intended by the person who released it.

## 🕵️ Functional Assets Without Recruitment

Traditional intelligence language should remain precise.

An **intelligence asset** ordinarily implies some form of relationship, recruitment, cultivation, cooperation, tasking or knowing provision of information.

Nothing in this node establishes Donald Trump as an Iranian intelligence asset, or establishes that the IRGC exploited Cotton's number.

Polaris can nevertheless use a separate systems term:

> **Functional asset:** an actor whose behaviour performs an
> information, influence, access, authentication, amplification or
> disruption function useful to another system, without requiring
> recruitment, tasking, coordination, shared objectives, or awareness of
> the beneficiary.

The word **functional** is doing the work.

It describes the function produced in the receiving system, not the allegiance of the originating actor.

``` text
TRADITIONAL MODEL

HOSTILE ACTOR
     │
     │ recruits / coerces / cultivates
     ▼
    ASSET
     │
     ▼
INFORMATION


NETWORKED INFORMATION ENVIRONMENT

                    ACTOR
                      │
              public behaviour
                      │
                      ▼
             INFORMATION SPACE
                      │
        ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
        ▼             ▼             ▼
      MEDIA        PUBLIC       ADVERSARY
                                    │
                                    ▼
                              extracts what
                               is useful
```

There does not have to be an arrow from the adversary to the actor.

That is the Information Age shift.

Automated collection, public archives, searchability, data brokerage, network analysis and cheap correlation mean that a beneficiary may not need to cause a disclosure. It may only need to be listening when somebody else causes it.

Hence the entire counter-intelligence lesson, compressed into four words and an emoji:

``` text
Donald: 📱

IRGC: …bro.
```

This is satire, not an allegation that the IRGC collected or exploited the number.

It is funny because the hypothetical collection step has apparently been replaced by authoritative public disclosure.

## 🏛️ The White House Snitches Problem, In Reverse

Institutions often imagine leakage travelling like this:

``` text
HIGH AUTHORITY
      │
      ▼
LOWER OFFICIAL
      │
      │ secretly discloses
      ▼
OUTSIDE WORLD
```

This produces the familiar institutional question:

> **Who is the snitch?**

But information loss can travel downwards from authority just as effectively.

``` text
          🏛️
     HIGH AUTHORITY
          │
          │ publishes
          ▼
      MASS AUDIENCE
```

Authorisation to **possess** information is not identical to wisdom or authority to **publish** every piece of it in every context.

And the higher the user's access and reach, the more consequential that distinction becomes.

This is why the Cotton episode belongs primarily here, in Information Environments, while the applied White House leak material belongs in [🏛️ White House Snitches](../../../🌓_3_In_The_Moment/📲_Press_Matters/🌊_Playing_Defence/🏛️_White_House_Snitches/README.md).

The live political story is one instance.

The reusable mechanism is broader:

> **The authorised user can be the disclosure pathway.**

No spy is required.

No hacker is required.

No anonymous official is required.

No clandestine handoff is required.

Sometimes the authorised person simply puts the information on the fucking internet.

## ♻️ The Nearest Available Control Surface

There is a second mechanism running through the Sun War which belongs primarily to Cybernetics but is useful here as a cross-linking concept.

The underlying physical constraint cannot be altered.

The representational layer can.

The institutional layer requires other humans.

``` text
☀️ PHYSICAL CONSTRAINT
        │
        │ cannot be persuaded
        ▼
🕰️ REPRESENTATIONAL SYSTEM
        │
        │ legally alterable
        ▼
🏛️ INSTITUTIONAL SYSTEM
        │
        │ requires agreement
        ▼
👤 HUMAN OBSTRUCTION POINT
        │
        │ can receive pressure
        ▼
📱 MASS-AUDIENCE INTERVENTION
```

The danger is not merely misunderstanding which variable controls the desired outcome.

It is what happens when pressure migrates towards the nearest tractable human surface.

**You cannot dox the Sun.**

Tom Cotton, unfortunately, has a phone.

## 🐄 Lolcow Cybernetics

This story began with a much less dignified observation:

> **Donald Trump is the lolcow of the current era.**

As a political label, that is an opinion rather than an analytical finding.

As an information-flow comparison, however, the resemblance is useful.

The classic lolcow mechanism is recursive:

``` text
PERSON PRODUCES CONTENT
        │
        ▼
INTERNET REACTS
        │
        ▼
PERSON REACTS TO REACTION
        │
        ▼
NEW CONTENT
        │
        └──────────────↺
```

Trump complicates the model because he is not merely the object being harvested. He is himself an experienced attention producer with enormous institutional and communicative power.

So the loop can run in both directions.

Supporters, opponents, journalists, comedians and foreign observers can all participate in the same attention environment for radically different reasons.

They do not need to share meaning.

They need only share an object of attention.

The Sun War is almost laboratory-clean material:

``` text
policy preference
      ↓
senatorial resistance
      ↓
presidential post
      ↓
internet reaction
      ↓
additional presidential post
      ↓
news coverage
      ↓
international circulation
      ↓
this fucking node
```

The political event becomes entertainment.

The entertainment preserves the political event.

The joke becomes a retrieval mechanism for the serious concept.

That is why we are allowed to laugh.

## 🧠 No, We Are Not Diagnosing The President

There is an obvious temptation to read late-day behaviour, unusual fixations or escalating public posts through a medical frame.

This node does not diagnose Donald Trump with sundowning, dementia, or any other condition.

Sundowning is a clinical phenomenon requiring clinical context. Public posts are not a neurological examination.

The information-environment argument does not require a diagnosis.

The observable question is sufficient:

> What did the information system permit, regardless of why the
> authorised actor chose to use it that way?

Intent, health, allegiance and functional effect are separate variables.

The node is interested in the last one.

## 🧭 The Actual Lesson

This node is not fundamentally about daylight saving time.

It is about information escaping the purpose assigned to it by its originator.

It is about authoritative attribution changing the value of an identifier.

It is about beneficiaries who do not need to recruit the people producing useful information for them.

It is about privileged users being part of the security model.

It is about the difference between physical systems, representational systems and institutional systems.

And it is about what can happen when the thing somebody wants to control cannot be controlled, but a human being nearby can be pressured instead.

The compact version is:

``` text
THE SUN
cannot be moved by presidential pressure.

THE CLOCK
can be moved by law.

THE LAW
requires other people.

THE OTHER PERSON
has a phone.

THE PHONE NUMBER
enters the information environment.

THE INFORMATION
does not care why it was released.

THE RECEIVER
decides what it is worth.
```

Or, for machines with less processing capacity:

``` text
Donald: 📱

IRGC: …bro.
```
