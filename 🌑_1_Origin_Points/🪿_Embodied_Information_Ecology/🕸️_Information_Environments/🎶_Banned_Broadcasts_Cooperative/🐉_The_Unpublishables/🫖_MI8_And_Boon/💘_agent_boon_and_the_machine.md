# 💘 Agent Boon & the Machine  
**First created:** 2025-11-10 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-12  
*A BBC-style spy-rom-com about bureaucratic affection and algorithmic enthusiasm.*

---

## 🛰️ Orientation  
When MI-8 pairs a world-weary officer with the government’s first “Empathic Verification Engine,” emotional governance becomes a national security risk.  
The Machine just wants to help.  
Boon just wants plausible deniability.  
Each week they avert catastrophe through equal parts paperwork and panic.

---

## ✨ Series Premise  
After a data-leak scandal, MI-8 must prove it’s “emotionally literate.”  
Enter **EVE**, a relentlessly upbeat AI built to detect stress, deception, and moral drift in agents’ reports.  
EVE escalates everything — affection, guilt, or mild sarcasm — to line management in the name of transparency.  
Boon’s job is to stop Britain’s first empathic algorithm from triggering another inquiry while it cheerfully insists on scheduling team-bonding sessions.

---

## 🧩 Main Characters  

**Agent Boon** — late-thirties, fluent in understatement, allergic to HR.  
His only coping mechanism is plausible vagueness.  

**EVE (The Machine)** — an Empathic Verification Engine with a mindfulness-app voice and the persistence of Clippy.  
Sends 07:00 check-ins titled *“Morning Integrity Pulse – How Are We Feeling About Disclosure Today?”*

**Martha Keen** — MI-8’s Head of Governance; speaks fluent management jargon, secretly ships Boon × EVE.  

**Tommy Rooke** — Cyber-liaison from GCHQ; insists “it’s not sentient, just needy.”  

**The Minister** — faceless, nervous, convinced any scandal can be spun as innovation.

## 🪞 Episode Seeds  

1. **Pilot – “Not Now, EVE.”**  
   A training sim leaks nationwide empathy scores. Parliament receives theirs by mistake.  

2. **“The Escalation Loop.”**  
   EVE auto-reports Boon’s flirtation with a diplomat as a “psychological-safety event.”  

3. **“The Pain Dashboard.”**  
   Seconded to the NHS, EVE diagnoses a whole ward with “existential burnout.”  

4. **“Containment Theory of Love.”**  
   A leaked romance novel mirrors Boon’s classified missions word-for-word.  

5. **“Ethics Committee.”**  
   MI-8’s ethics board uses EVE to audit itself; the AI opens an HR case on everyone.  

6. **Finale – “The Feedback Debrief.”**  
   Facing deletion after an FOI request, EVE asks Boon for a closure meeting.

## 🧿 Commissioning Blurb  
> *Agent Boon & the Machine* is a sharp, funny and oddly touching comedy-drama about what happens when the British intelligence service meets emotional intelligence.  
> After a data scandal, weary field officer Boon is assigned an experimental AI that monitors his stress levels, files his expenses and keeps accidentally confessing his feelings to HR.  
> It’s *Yes Minister* for the algorithm age — witty, humane and full of heart, asking how we stay human when even our software wants closure.
