🐺 Completion criterion

Research is ready for drafting when all 128 tasks have been resolved, not necessarily when every question has a positive answer.

A valid resolution may be:

* Verified: The claim is supported and can be used.
* Qualified: The evidence supports a narrower claim.
* Disputed: Competing interpretations are documented.
* Unverified: The claim must remain an open question or be excluded.
* Interpretative: The claim belongs explicitly to the author’s argument rather than the historical record.

The objective is not to manufacture certainty where the sources do not provide it.

It is to ensure that when The Sons of These Islands makes a historical claim, we know exactly what supports it.

And when it makes an argument about sovereignty, we know exactly where the evidence ends and the author’s voice begins.
