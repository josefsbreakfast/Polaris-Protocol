”🥔 I’d suggest working through the cluster in causal order, rather than alphabetical order or the order the nodes were written.

That way, each polished node establishes the evidence and mechanisms needed by the next. We also avoid repeatedly correcting the same historical assumptions.

🧭 Recommended refinement order

🌱 Phase 1 — Establish the conditions

These nodes build the historical environment before we examine the catastrophe itself.

1. 🌱 Before The Blight
2. 📐 English Land Controls
3. 🥔 Phytophthora infestans
4. 🌾 There Was Always Food In Ireland

Objective: Establish what existed, who controlled it, who depended on it, and what the biological disturbance actually changed.

🏛️ Phase 2 — Reconstruct the governing machinery

5. 🇬🇧 What Britain Knew
6. 🏛️ What Britain Did
7. 🔥 Eviction And Clearance
8. 🕯️ The Lawful Catastrophe

Objective: Reconstruct the relationship between information, institutional decisions, property rights, relief mechanisms and their consequences.

I’d pay particular attention to chronology here. The difference between what an institution knew initially and what it continued doing after receiving evidence of harm is central to the cluster.

🫀 Phase 3 — Follow the consequences into bodies

9. 🪦 How And Why People Died
10. 🚢 Where Everybody Went
11. 🌿 The Demographic Scar

Objective: Establish mortality mechanisms, displacement, migration and the longer demographic consequences.

This is where the cluster’s position inside The Body In The Loop becomes especially important.

🩸 Phase 4 — Classification and historical interpretation

12. 🌫️ What Do We Call It?
13. 🩸 Why Call It Genocide?
14. 🗺️ Translation Is Not Equivalence

Objective: Evaluate terminology and historical classification against the mechanisms established in the preceding nodes.

I would deliberately leave the genocide node until this point. Not because the question is secondary, but because it deserves the strongest possible evidentiary foundation.

☘️ Phase 5 — Historical afterlives

15. ☘️ Land And Independence
16. ☘️ Remembering An Gorta Mór
17. 🫀 Bodies In Bronze

Objective: Follow changes in land, political organisation, historical memory and public commemoration without making later developments inevitable.

🪶 Phase 6 — Solidarity and contemporary translation

18. 🪶 Tied Twice To Land Back
19. ☪️ Sadaqah From The Sultan
20. 🍉 What Oct 09 Meant

Objective: Examine how information about suffering travels between communities, how people respond, and how historical knowledge can inform present-day questions without collapsing distinct histories into equivalence.

The Sultan node already has particularly useful distinctions between documented assistance, disputed maritime traditions and the politics of archival evidence.  ⁠￼

🥔 Phase 7 — Final integration

21. 🥔 An Gorta Mór — README

I’d leave the README until last.

It’s already a substantial intellectual map, but polishing it before the individual nodes would risk making the navigation reflect an earlier version of the cluster.

Once the nodes are refined, we can update the README to reflect what the research actually establishes, including remaining uncertainties. Its existing methodological safeguards are worth preserving.  

⸻

🪿 How I’d approach each node

For each, I’d use the same four-stage process:

1. Editorial audit: What works, what repeats, what’s missing, and what needs clarification?
2. Historical and evidentiary audit: Which claims are established, contested, insufficiently sourced or potentially inaccurate?
3. Systems audit: Are the causal mechanisms and relationships with neighbouring nodes adequately developed?
4. Polish: Improve structure, navigation, sourcing and readability without sacrificing your voice or the node’s substance.

We should also maintain a cluster-wide issue register so that discoveries in later nodes can be carried back into earlier ones without losing track.

⭐ One important recommendation

Don’t try to make every node publication-perfect on its first pass.

I’d complete the structural and evidentiary audits across each phase, then polish the nodes together. That allows us to catch contradictions between neighbouring accounts before committing to final wording.

And I would keep the README’s existing warning that this is active research. The cluster explicitly documents uncertainties and previous LLM failures; those are part of its provenance, not embarrassing leftovers to tidy away.  

My suggested starting point: 🌱 Before The Blight.

Because before we can explain why the catastrophe killed people, we need to understand the system in which those people were already trying to live. 🥔”
