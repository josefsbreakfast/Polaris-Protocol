# 💉 Full Flu Shot™️
**First created:** 2026-09-10 | **Last updated:** 2026-09-10  
*Why stop at catching print when the surrounding data economy has already spent fifteen years trying to make everybody else legible?*

---

## 🛰️ Orientation

The base **Mannobjectifier™️** has a serious limitation.

It can look.

It cannot know.

A camera may estimate a visible bodily characteristic, but it cannot tell the user:

- whether he has a wife;
- whether he has three girlfriends who all believe they are the only girlfriend;
- whether his dating profile is six years out of date;
- whether his crypto wallet represents generational wealth or twelve pounds and an NFT of a disappointed monkey;
- whether he habitually appears within a suspicious radius of his ex;
- whether his public personality and private behaviour have ever met;
- or whether several entirely innocent data points become considerably less charming when placed beside one another.

For that, the Mannobjectifier™️ requires integration.

Welcome to:

> **Mannobjectifier+™️ Full Flu Shot™️**

The fictional promise is simple:

> **Know him before you know him.**

The analytical problem is worse.

---

## 💉 What Is A Full Flu Shot™️?

A **Full Flu Shot™️** is the imaginary end-state of integrating Mannobjectifier™️ with the wider surveillance-capitalist information environment.

The original product asks:

```text
what can this camera infer?
```

The integrated product asks:

```text
what can every available system infer
when their observations are joined together?
```

The distinction matters.

A single dataset may be mundane.

A location history may show places.

A purchase history may show transactions.

A social graph may show relationships.

A platform history may show activity.

A data broker may hold demographic or commercial attributes.

An identity-resolution system may decide that several records describe the same person.

The **Full Flu Shot™️** is the point at which those fragments are imagined as one consumer-facing dossier.

Not because this would be a good idea.

Because the joke stops being funny remarkably quickly once the architecture becomes recognisable.

## 📱 The Consumer Experience

The fictional interface should behave like an aggressively reassuring safety product.

For example:

```text

IDENTITY MATCH
Confidence: HIGH

Relationship overlap:        POSSIBLE
Recent profile activity:     DETECTED
Location inconsistency:      REVIEW
Financial bravado index:     ELEVATED
Crypto solidity:             UNCONFIRMED
Ex-partner proximity:        PLEASE DON'T
Public/private mismatch:     FURTHER DATA REQUIRED

Overall recommendation:

ASK TWO MORE QUESTIONS
BEFORE GOING BACK TO HIS FLAT
```

The joke is that the interface looks useful.

The danger is that interfaces which look useful encourage users to forget how much uncertainty, context loss and invasive data collection may sit underneath them.

A warning badge is not the same thing as knowledge.

A dashboard is not the same thing as truth.

And **HIGH CONFIDENCE** remains capable of being confidently wrong.

## 🕸️ Integration Is The Product

The most important Full Flu Shot™️ insight is that no single sensor needs to know very much.

The power comes from joining observations.

Consider:

```text
Dataset A knows where he goes.

Dataset B knows what he buys.

Dataset C knows who he follows.

Dataset D knows which devices appear together.

Dataset E knows which accounts resemble one another.

Dataset F knows what somebody else inferred about him last year.
```

Individually, each may be incomplete.

Together, they can produce a much richer representation.

That representation may then become input into:

- recommendations;
- risk scores;
- advertising;
- fraud systems;
- identity systems;
- moderation;
- insurance;
- employment screening;
- relationship surveillance;
- policing;
- or another dataset sold onward to somebody else.

The Mannobjectifier+™️ does not need omniscience.

It needs **interoperability**.

That is much more plausible.

## 🔗 Identity Resolution: Congratulations, We Think That's Him

A joined surveillance environment depends on deciding which records belong to the same person.

This is an enormously consequential step disguised as housekeeping.

The Full Flu Shot™️ therefore includes the fictional feature:

> **Congratulations, We Think That's Him™️**

Possible matching signals might include identifiers, devices, accounts, locations, contact details or other relational information.

The important analytical distinction is:

```text
records can be linked
≠
records were linked correctly
```

Once a mistaken link enters a profile, later systems may treat the combined record as established fact.

A false connection can therefore become more persuasive after aggregation rather than less.

More data can amplify an error.

## 💔 Romantic Overlap Detection

The original product cannot warn of mistresses.

This has been identified as a critical market deficiency.

**Romantic Overlap Detection™️** would promise to infer whether apparently separate relationships overlap in time.

The obvious commercial pitch is irresistible:

> **Why ask him when metadata exists?**

The obvious social consequence is appalling.

Relationship inference can move rapidly from:

```text
consumer safety feature
```

to:

```text
intimate-partner surveillance
```

depending on:

- who is doing the observing;
- whether the observed person consented;
- what information is available;
- how persistent the observation is;
- whether location or communications data are involved;
- and what the observer intends to do with the result.

The same technical capability can sit inside very different power relationships.

That is precisely why capability alone does not settle whether a use is legitimate.

## 📍 The Ex-Girlfriend Problem

The Mannobjectifier+™️ deliberately reverses a familiar surveillance scenario.

Imagine a woman being offered:

- location histories;
- device associations;
- account discovery;
- relationship mapping;
- behavioural prediction;
- identity resolution;
- and automated alerts

about a man she used to date.

Presented as empowerment, the package can initially sound protective.

Now reverse it.

A man purchases the same capability to understand where his ex-girlfriend goes, who she sees and whether her behaviour has changed.

The ethical structure becomes much harder to ignore.

This is the point of the Full Flu Shot™️.

The answer to asymmetric access to intrusive technology is not:

> **democratise the infrastructure until everyone can surveil everyone.**

Sometimes the better answer is:

> **perhaps nobody needed this feature.**

## 🪞 Safety Language Can Hide Surveillance

Surveillance products rarely need to market themselves using the word *surveillance*.

They can be sold as:

- safety;
- convenience;
- personalisation;
- fraud prevention;
- verification;
- parental reassurance;
- relationship transparency;
- optimisation;
- discovery;
- trust;
- or peace of mind.

Each framing may describe a genuine use.

It may also make the underlying observational capability feel less intrusive.

The Full Flu Shot™️ deliberately abuses the language of preventative health:

> **One quick shot. Know the risk.**

That branding is awful.

It is supposed to be.

The joke demonstrates how a familiar protective frame can make invasive information practices sound responsible before anyone has examined what the system actually observes.

## 🧠 Inference Is Not Biography

Even a vast integrated dataset does not contain a human being.

It contains records.

Those records may be:

- accurate;
- inaccurate;
- current;
- stale;
- duplicated;
- misattributed;
- context-poor;
- generated automatically;
- inferred probabilistically;
- copied from another source;
- or derived from an earlier mistake.

The Full Flu Shot™️ therefore requires another mandatory warning:

> **⚠️ A dossier is a representation of a person, not the person.**

This distinction becomes more important as the dossier becomes more impressive.

A sparse record looks incomplete.

A beautiful dashboard can conceal incompleteness behind design.

## 🦠 Data Contagion

The Flu Shot™️ metaphor becomes especially unpleasant when an inference begins travelling between systems.

Imagine:

```text
System A produces an inference
        ↓
System B receives the inference as data
        ↓
System C receives B's enriched profile
        ↓
System D independently appears to confirm it
```

Four systems may now contain substantially the same claim.

That does not necessarily mean four independent observations occurred.

The apparent consensus may have one ancestor.

Provenance matters.

Without it, repetition can masquerade as corroboration.

The Mannobjectifier+™️ calls this feature:

> **Community Immunity™️**

Legal has asked us to stop.

## 📊 The Dashboard Problem

Dashboards create a peculiar kind of authority.

A messy reality becomes:

```text
GREEN
AMBER
RED
```

or:

```text
12%
64%
93%
```

The interface removes friction.

It also removes the visible labour of interpretation.

A Full Flu Shot™️ might therefore convert uncertain social evidence into:

```text
MISTRESS RISK: 82%
```

But what exactly does 82% mean?

Which population produced the model?

What counts as a mistress?

How recent is the underlying data?

How many signals were independent?

How many came from the same source?

What happens when the system is wrong?

Who gets to challenge the classification?

And why, exactly, has somebody built a mistress classifier?

The cleanest interface may sit on top of the messiest epistemology.

## 🧿 From Observability To Intervention

The Full Flu Shot™️ becomes most consequential when observation changes what happens next.

A user receives a warning.

The warning alters behaviour.

The subject is avoided, confronted, reported, blocked, investigated or classified.

That response creates new information.

The new information may itself enter later systems.

The sequence becomes:

```text
observation
    ↓
inference
    ↓
classification
    ↓
intervention
    ↓
changed behaviour
    ↓
new observation
```

At that point, the system is no longer merely describing a person.

It is participating in the environment through which that person's future behaviour becomes visible.

This is where a silly pair of dick-measuring glasses wanders directly into cybernetics.

We apologise to everyone.

## 🛑 What The Product Should Not Teach

The Mannobjectifier™️ is satire.

It should not become a manual for locating, monitoring, identifying or profiling real people.

The useful questions are architectural:

- what becomes observable;
- how observations become linked;
- how inference differs from fact;
- how power changes the meaning of observation;
- how uncertainty disappears inside interfaces;
- how errors propagate;
- how safety rhetoric can legitimise intrusive capabilities;
- and how observation can become intervention.

The target is the infrastructure.

Not somebody's ex.
