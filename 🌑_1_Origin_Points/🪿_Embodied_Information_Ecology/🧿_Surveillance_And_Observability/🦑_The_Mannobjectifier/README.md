# 🦑 The Mannobjectifier™️

**First created:** 2026-09-10 \| **Last updated:** 2026-09-10
*A satirical consumer-surveillance product for the girls, the gays and

## 🛰️ Orientation

**The Mannobjectifier™️** is a satirical product concept about bodies,
observability, inference, objectification and the cheerful
industrialisation of intrusive technologies once the direction of the
gaze is reversed.

The product proposition is simple.

Take a pair of chunky glasses.

Bolt on a smart camera.

Add an AI interface.

Point the resulting apparatus at adult men.

And use computer vision to:

> **catch some print.**

In its original internet usage, **catching print** meant estimating the
length of a man's flaccid penis from what could be observed through his
clothing.

For a brief and culturally instructive moment, women publicly discussed
visually assessing men's intimate anatomy.

A vocal subset of the male internet lost its damn mind.

The Mannobjectifier™️ asks the obvious innovation-policy question:

> **what if, instead of learning from that discomfort, we scaled it?**

## 👓 The Product

The Mannobjectifier™️ is imagined as deliberately conspicuous smart
eyewear: chunky frames with a visible camera attachment and an
AI-assisted heads-up display.

Its fictional technical stack combines:

``` text
camera input
      ↓
body / garment detection
      ↓
visible contour estimation
      ↓
geometric inference
      ↓
confidence interval
      ↓
CATCH SOME PRINT™️
```

The joke depends on refusing to pretend that inference is the same thing
as knowledge.

A system may be able to detect a visible contour.

It may estimate a measurement.

It may assign a confidence score.

It may still be hilariously underqualified to tell the user what they
actually want to know.

This makes the Mannobjectifier™️ an unusually efficient teaching device
for several recurring problems in surveillance systems:

-   what is visible is not necessarily what is understood;
-   a proxy is not the thing it claims to represent;
-   measurement error does not disappear because software produced a
    decimal place;
-   confidence in classification is not confidence in meaning;
-   intimate inference can feel very different depending on who is being
    observed;
-   and adding more data does not necessarily answer the original
    question.

## 📏 One Dimension May Limit Reasonable Expectations Of Functionality

Every Mannobjectifier™️ result should carry the mandatory product
warning:

> **⚠️ One dimension may limit reasonable expectations of
> functionality.**

This is simultaneously:

-   a consumer disclaimer;
-   a statistical warning;
-   an epistemological problem;
-   and a review.

The device estimates what it estimates.

Users are strongly discouraged from constructing an entire theory of the
man from a single measurement.

This remains true even where the measurement is unusually encouraging.

## 📉 Known Product Limitations

The Mannobjectifier™️ development team believes strongly in
transparency.

### 💰 Crypto remains outside the field of view

Sadly, we cannot currently tell whether his crypto account also has a
big dick, which is arguably a more solid dick.

Financial integrations remain under consideration.

### 💉 Mistress detection is not yet supported

The current model cannot warn users of mistresses.

However, with eventual integration into existing surveillance
capitalism, **Mannobjectifier+™️** may be able to provide a full **Flu
Shot™️** warning covering every inferable aspect of his life history.

This would include the kind of relationship, behavioural, location,
social and commercial data that increasingly become available when
ordinary consumer systems are joined together.

In other words:

> just like for men using the same technologies against *their*
> ex-girlfriends.

The satire is not that such an infrastructure would be desirable.

The satire is that much of the infrastructure already exists, and its
intrusiveness can become newly legible when the imagined beneficiary
changes.

### 📐 Length is not function

The Mannobjectifier™️ reminds all users that:

> **one dimension may limit reasonable expectations of functionality.**

We cannot stress this enough.

## 💉 The Flu Shot™️

**Flu Shot™️** is the deliberately grotesque endpoint of the product
roadmap.

The primitive Mannobjectifier™️ observes a body.

The mature Mannobjectifier+™️ would participate in an information
environment.

Once connected to:

-   location histories;
-   commercial datasets;
-   social graphs;
-   relationship inference;
-   platform activity;
-   behavioural profiling;
-   identity resolution;
-   and other surveillance-capitalist infrastructure,

the product would no longer merely ask:

``` text
what can I see?
```

It could begin asking:

``` text
what can the surrounding data ecosystem tell me about him?
```

That escalation is the point.

The joke begins with a penis.

It ends with **observability as infrastructure**.

## 🧿 Why It Lives Here

The Mannobjectifier™️ belongs inside **🧿 Surveillance And
Observability** because it is a deliberately stupid demonstration of a
serious distinction.

Observation does not have to begin with a secret state programme or an
invisible data broker.

It can begin with:

``` text
a body
+
a line of sight
+
a measurement
+
an inference
```

Technology can increase the resolution, persistence, scalability and
apparent authority of that inference.

Networked data can then expand the field of observation beyond the body
itself.

The resulting asymmetry matters.

Who gets to look?

Who gets measured?

Who gets classified?

Who gets to combine observations?

Who gets to infer?

Who gets to act on the inference?

And at what point does the person being observed discover that the
system exists?

The Mannobjectifier™️ does not resolve those questions.

It puts enormous glasses on them.

## 🪞 The Reversal

The core satirical mechanism is not:

> men should be objectified because women have been objectified.

It is:

> **some forms of observation become remarkably easy to recognise as
> invasive once the accustomed direction of observation is reversed.**

The joke therefore works by taking familiar technological rhetoric and
applying it to an intimate male body:

-   frictionless;
-   AI-powered;
-   personalised;
-   data-driven;
-   confidence-scored;
-   interoperable;
-   scalable;
-   and coming soon to a platform ecosystem near you.

The discomfort is analytically useful.

The product is not.
