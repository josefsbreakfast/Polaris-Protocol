More concretely, the first question is what explanatory work is being done?

The four domains

🫀 Embodiment

Route here when the note can substantially be understood by examining the embodied human organism itself.

Includes perception, cognition, attention, affect, stress, arousal, nervous-system responses, memory, fatigue, pain, sensory processing, bodily regulation and embodied/situated cognition.

Test: Could I write this note without needing an information system, feedback mechanism or observer to explain the central phenomenon?

If yes → 🫀_Embodiment/

⸻

♻️ Cybernetics

Route here when the central object is feedback, regulation or adaptation.

Includes feedback loops, control, homeostasis, reinforcement, system response, recursive processes, adaptation, learning, signalling and the relationship between output and subsequent input.

The critical distinction is:

something changes
≠ cybernetics
something changes
and that change feeds back into what happens next
= potentially cybernetics

If the note is principally explaining that mechanism → ♻️_Cybernetics/

⸻

🕸️ Information Environments

Route here when the central object is the informational environment itself.

Includes communication environments, media ecology, information availability, discoverability, information abundance/scarcity, networks, platforms, transmission, attention environments, epistemic conditions and how information is organised or encountered.

The key distinction:

information affecting a body
→ probably 🫀🕸️
structure/properties of the information environment
→ 🕸️

⸻

🧿 Surveillance And Observability

Route here when the central object is observation, visibility, legibility or monitoring.

Includes surveillance, observability, data collection, monitoring, legibility, visibility, watchers and watched, asymmetrical visibility, classification based upon observation, and awareness of possible observation.

Importantly:

data exists
≠ surveillance
information can be observed/collected
in relation to an actor or system
→ observability
observation is actually organised or exercised
→ surveillance

So this folder can discuss the conceptual machinery without assuming every observable system is actively surveilled.

⸻

The six interfaces

The test here is stronger:

If removing either domain destroys the phenomenon the note is trying to explain, it belongs at the interface.

🫀♻️ The Body In The Loop

Embodiment + feedback

The body’s state or behaviour becomes part of a feedback/regulatory process.

stimulus
→ bodily response
→ response affects subsequent stimulus/intervention
→ new bodily response

Conditioning, behavioural reinforcement, adaptive physiological regulation and human-in-the-loop feedback are obvious candidates.

⸻

🫀🕸️ Information Is Experienced

Embodiment + information environment

How informational conditions become perception, cognition, emotion or bodily experience.

information environment
        ↓
attention / perception
        ↓
embodied experience

Information overload, doomscrolling, attentional capture, affective information environments, sensory load, etc.

The note isn’t merely about information or bodies. It’s about information encountering a body.

⸻

🫀🧿 The Observed Body

Embodiment + surveillance

What actual or anticipated observation does to embodied humans—and what embodied behaviour makes observable.

Includes chilling effects, hypervigilance, self-presentation, behavioural modification under observation, bodily legibility and physiological consequences of surveillance.

The useful test:

Would the bodily phenomenon materially change if the possibility of observation disappeared?

If yes, this interface is probably right.

⸻

♻️🕸️ The Feedback Environment

Cybernetics + information environments

Information environments that respond to behaviour and thereby alter subsequent information exposure.

information
→ behaviour
→ system response
→ changed information
→ behaviour...

Recommendation systems are the obvious example, but the category is much broader than algorithms.

⸻

♻️🧿 Observation Becomes Intervention

Cybernetics + surveillance

Observation becomes input into a regulatory or adaptive process.

observe
→ classify
→ respond
→ observe response
→ update
→ respond again

This distinction is important because surveillance alone doesn’t require feedback.

A CCTV camera recording continuously may primarily be 🧿.

A system where observed behaviour changes how the subject is subsequently treated is ♻️🧿.

⸻

🕸️🧿 The Observed Information Space

Information environment + surveillance

Observation changes the conditions under which information is produced, distributed, encountered or understood.

Includes monitored communication spaces, audience awareness, surveillance of information networks, visibility of communicative behaviour and information asymmetries created through observation.

Again:

someone watches a person
→ 🧿
being watched changes the person
→ 🫀🧿
someone watches an information environment
→ 🕸️🧿
observation triggers adaptive intervention
→ ♻️🧿

That gives us some genuinely useful boundaries.

🐣 The Whole Ecology

This should have the highest admission threshold.

A note does not go here simply because it mentions three or four domains.

It belongs here when the phenomenon cannot adequately be explained without their interaction.

Especially:

🕸️ information environment
        ↓
🫀 embodied perception
        ↓
behaviour
        ↓
🧿 observation
        ↓
♻️ classification / response
        ↓
changed 🕸️ information environment
        ↓
changed 🫀 experience
        ↓
...

🐣 therefore contains emergence, multi-domain recursion and whole-system behaviour.

Its test should be:

Is the thing I am explaining an emergent property of the ecology rather than a property of one component or one interface?

If yes → 🐣_The_Whole_Ecology/.

The anti-chaos rules

I’d put these directly into the eventual routing note:

MENTION ≠ MEMBERSHIP
Route by explanatory object,
not by every concept appearing in the note.
ONE DOMAIN
→ domain folder
TWO DOMAINS WHOSE RELATIONSHIP IS THE OBJECT
→ interface folder
THREE OR FOUR DOMAINS
→ does NOT automatically mean Whole Ecology
EMERGENT / RECURSIVE MULTI-DOMAIN PHENOMENON
→ 🐣 Whole Ecology
WHEN TWO LOCATIONS SEEM EQUALLY PLAUSIBLE:
ask what would be lost if one domain were removed.
If nothing important is lost:
route to the simpler category.
If the argument collapses:
route to the interface.
PREFER THE LOWEST SUFFICIENT LEVEL.
Do not promote a note to an interface
when one domain explains it.
Do not promote a note to Whole Ecology
when one interface explains it.

That last principle is probably the one that will keep this usable six months from now:

Route to the lowest level of ecological complexity sufficient to explain the phenomenon.

That gives us an actual classification system rather than eleven attractive buckets.
