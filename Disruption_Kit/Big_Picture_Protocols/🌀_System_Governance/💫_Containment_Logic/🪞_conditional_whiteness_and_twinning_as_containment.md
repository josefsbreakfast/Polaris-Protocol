**First created:** 2025-11-07 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-14  
*Diagnostic reflection on how institutions manage belonging through racial coding and oppositional pairing.*  

---

## 🧭 Orientation
Belonging in elite institutions often depends on **conditional whiteness** — access mediated by the performance of acceptability.  
When individuals reject this performance, they are re-racialised and paired with other marginalised groups, diluting solidarity and visibility.  
This node maps that mechanism as a containment practice.

---

## 🧩 Key Features
- **Performative Acceptance:** Whiteness as temporary pass.  
- **Re-Racialisation Trigger:** Dissent recoded as foreignness.  
- **Twinning Mechanism:** Opposition paired with other marginalities.  
- **Continuum of Philo/Anti Bias:** Flattery flips to hostility.  
- **Containment through Inclusion:** Diversity optics replace safety.
