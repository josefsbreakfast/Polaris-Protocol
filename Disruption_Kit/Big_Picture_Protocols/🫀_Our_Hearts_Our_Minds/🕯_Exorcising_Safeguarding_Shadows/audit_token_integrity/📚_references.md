## ⚖️ Legal Foundations  

| Framework | Citation | Relevance |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** | Article 5 (1)(d) — *Accuracy* | Requires controllers to ensure personal data are accurate and kept up to date. |
| **Data Protection Act 2018** | Part 2 s.171 & s.172 | Governs enforcement and remedial powers for inaccuracy and rights to rectification. |
| **Counter-Terrorism and Security Act 2015 s.26** | *Prevent Duty* | Allows sharing for safeguarding and crime prevention — important when boundaries between datasets blur. |
| **Domestic Abuse Act 2021** | — | Provides statutory footing for VAWG-related safeguarding data; relevant to victim/offender role separation. |
