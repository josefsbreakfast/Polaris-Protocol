## 🧿 Analysis / Content  

### 1. Definition  
A respectability pipeline:

- looks respectable  
- feels intellectual  
- trades on institutional status  
- offers belonging and meaning  

The ideological payload is delayed.

### 2. Why These Pipelines Exist  
Modern extremists know overt symbols repel the very people whose legitimacy they need. So they re‑skin the project as:

- philosophical debate  
- national reflection  
- moral clarity conversations  

The radicalism is introduced gradually.

### 3. Who Is Targeted  
Key targets:

- MPs, aides, councillors  
- academics and students  
- clergy and Christian professionals  
- think‑tank staff  
- mid‑career professionals  
- young men drifting rightwards online  

They are invited for their **status and influence**, not their vulnerability.

### 4. Pipeline Stages  

1. **Flattering Invitation** – “We need voices like yours.”  
2. **Prestige Environment** – nice venue, serious programme.  
3. **Softening Content** – talk of decline, duty, meaning.  
4. **Mask‑Slip** – talk turns to existential threat, purifying the nation.  
5. **Capture or Exit** – attendees either rationalise it or walk out.

### 5. Post‑2020 Supercharging
