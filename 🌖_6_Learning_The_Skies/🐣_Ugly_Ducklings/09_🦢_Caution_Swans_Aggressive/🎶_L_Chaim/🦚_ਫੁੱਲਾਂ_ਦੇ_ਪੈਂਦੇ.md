# 🦚 ਫੁੱਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਪੈਂਦੇ / Phullā̃ De Painde / When The Flowers Fall
**First created:** 2026-08-28 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-29  
*An interior-perspective model of developmental model lag, defensive display, cultural misclassification, and the difficult task of learning that attention can carry meanings other than danger.*

---

## 🛰️ Orientation

There is a point in the Ugly Duckling story which is easy to narrate from outside and extremely difficult to model from inside.

The bird becomes a swan.

The surrounding environment may begin responding to the bird differently.

The bird's internal model may not update at the same speed.

**The Swan in this node is a composite analytical figure.** It is not a clinical portrait, an autobiographical declaration, or evidence that any particular instance of perceived attention, attraction, admiration, hostility, or observation exists or has been correctly interpreted. The node models one possible adaptation to repeated adverse feedback and asks what happens if the environment later changes.

For a developing person who has spent a long time learning that attention predicts correction, ridicule, punishment, danger, or evidence that she has once again done something wrong, later attention does not arrive in a neutral interpretive environment.

The old model may already be waiting:

```text
attention perceived
→ possible problem
→ possible threat
→ investigate before assuming meaning
```

This node is about what happens when that model begins encountering data that do not fit it cleanly.

It uses one particular rose — Diljit Dosanjh's **ਮੋਰਨੀ / Morni / Peahen** — to explain a larger cybernetic problem: the lag between a living person and the representations through which institutions, cultures, other people, and eventually the person herself may have learned to understand her.

**The song is an analytical rose, not a deposition.** Its courtship grammar supplies a deliberately heightened cultural example. Symbolic fit is not empirical proof about any person or event outside the song.

It is also about what happens when an inherited model finally has to update.

Caution.

Swans aggressive.

*Translation used:* [via Letras](https://www.letras.com/diljit-dosanjh/morni/english.html).

## 🦚 ਮੋਰਨੀ / Morni / Peahen

The dictionary answer is simple.

**ਮੋਰਨੀ / morni / peahen.**

The cultural answer is not.

Within South Asian symbolic and aesthetic vocabularies, **ਮੋਰ / mor / peacock** and **ਮੋਰਨੀ / morni / peahen** carry considerably more information than the English zoological pair *male peacock / female peacock*.

The bird is already entangled with beauty, courtship, bearing, display, movement, desire, ornament, and dance.

That matters because a translation can preserve the animal and delete the metaphor.

A subtitle rendering **ਮੋਰਨੀ / morni / peahen** as **princess** is therefore not literally exact, but can be symbolically intelligent. It translates something about the woman's position inside the relational metaphor rather than merely identifying the species.

For this node, several translations can coexist:

```text
ਮੋਰਨੀ
morni
peahen

symbolically:
princess

long-winded relational gloss:
the woman before whom the peacock is prepared
to perform the whole vulnerable display

Polaris gloss:
woman one is prepared to act a fool for
```

The last is not a dictionary definition.

It is, however, useful cybernetics.

The peahen is partly made legible by what happens to the peacock **inside the song's courtship metaphor**.

## 🌹 ਕੇਸ ਜਿੱਤ ਗਏ, ਤੇਰੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਿਲ ਹਾਰੇ / Case Jitt Gaye, Tere Ton Dil Haare / We Won The Cases, But Lost Our Hearts To You

The relevant movement in *Morni* is gloriously unsubtle.

The masculine display establishes:

- territorial authority,
- money,
- reputation,
- danger,
- social position,
- conspicuous expenditure,
- victory,
- and the capacity to act.

Then the lyric turns.

```text
ਕੇਸ ਜਿੱਤ ਗਏ, ਤੇਰੇ ਤੋਂ ਦਿਲ ਹਾਰੇ
case jitt gaye, tere ton dil haare
we won the cases, but lost our hearts to you
```

The important thing is not merely that a tough man secretly has feelings.

The song establishes his power **before** telling us that his ordinary vocabulary of winning does not govern this relationship.

He does not need to become weak for her to become powerful.

She does not need to defeat him by force.

He can remain formidable and still say:

> I lose to you.

As an analytical rose, this supplies the Swan model with an unusually useful distinction:

**It is possible for another person's vulnerability to be wanted, affectionate, trusting, or pleasurable rather than evidence of harm.**

That proposition belongs first to the relational world staged by the song. Whether any real-world interaction carries such a meaning requires independent evidence.

## 🐣 Why Is Everyone Looking At Me?

The Ugly Duckling does not begin with a neutral prior.

Repeated bullying, invalidation, misclassification, humiliation, coercive correction, or other forms of psychological mistreatment can teach a very simple relationship:

```text
attention
→ scrutiny
→ error
→ adverse consequence
```

Eventually:

```text
attention
≈ threat cue
```

Then the environment may change.

Perhaps the developing person becomes more capable.

Perhaps she becomes more socially confident.

Perhaps other people respond differently for any number of reasons.

The old classifier does not necessarily change with the environment.

```text
new or ambiguous input:
attention / curiosity / recognition / social interest

old classifier:
POSSIBLE DANGER
```

**This section concerns interpretation after attention is perceived or independently observed. Perception of attention does not by itself establish that attention exists, who is responsible for it, why it exists, or whether its meaning is positive, negative, romantic, hostile, or neutral.**

From outside, a mismatch between present conditions and an older threat prior can look irrational.

From inside, it can be extremely difficult to identify.

The relevant internal questions are not necessarily:

> Everybody is looking at me because I am extraordinarily important.

They may instead be:

> Why does this interaction feel salient?
>
> What did I do?
>
> What have I missed?
>
> Is there a threat here?
>
> What information would let me distinguish one explanation from another?

For quite a long time.

The cybernetic problem is **uncertain signal interpretation under a threat-trained prior**, not a presumption that the most flattering explanation is correct.

## 🪞 The Swan Has A Ledger Too

**Ugly Ducklings** is partly about representations which become more operationally powerful than the person they supposedly describe.

By the time we reach **Caution Swans Aggressive**, the living bird may have changed substantially while external systems continue processing an inherited model.

There is an uncomfortable recursive possibility.

The institution may not be the only Keeper retaining an obsolete ledger.

The Swan can have one too.

```text
living bird:
changed organism

external inherited representation:
older model

internal inherited representation:
older model
```

That internal representation may survive precisely because it once predicted the environment rather well.

This is not stupidity.

It is model lag.

And model lag becomes particularly strange when current evidence repeatedly fails to fit the inherited representation.

## ⚠️ Negative Power Is Easier To Believe

Suppose the old governing belief is:

> My effects on other people are bad.

Then this interpretation is comparatively cheap:

> **I frighten people.**

It preserves the old architecture.

```text
other people behave differently
→ I may affect people
→ old model expects negative effect
→ fear becomes an available explanation
```

There can even be defensive agency in accepting it.

Fine.

Be fucking scared.

The harder update, **if independently supported by the circumstances**, might be:

```text
other people behave differently
→ I may affect people
→ the effect may not be negative
→ admiration / affection / attraction / trust
   are also possible human responses
→ another person's vulnerability
   is not automatically evidence of harm
```

That does not merely extend the old model.

It may require restructuring it.

The model does **not** tell us whether fear, admiration, attraction, hostility, affection, or any other interpretation is correct in a particular case. Its concern is the relative cognitive cost of incorporating different explanations once evidence supports them.

Hence, within the rose:

**ਮੋਰਨੀ / morni / peahen:** ????

For years.

## 👀 Them Eyes, Man

*Morni* supplies a deliberately theatrical example of relational reversal.

Its masculine speaker has already been established through wealth, territorial authority, victory, reputation, and danger. The song then permits that formidable figure to become emotionally vulnerable within courtship without stripping him of his wider power.

That is the useful information.

The analytical transfer is not:

> a formidable man's changed behaviour proves attraction.

It is:

> **positive or wanted vulnerability is conceptually different from harm, and a threat-trained model may initially be poor at representing that distinction.**

The song exaggerates the contrast beautifully:

```text
masculine self-presentation:
power / victory / danger / control

courtship grammar:
I still lose my heart to you
```

For the Swan model, this creates a useful hypothetical problem:

```text
old hypothesis:
my effect on others is probably negative

new evidence, if independently established:
another person's response may be warm,
trusting, admiring, affectionate, or attracted

required update:
not every form of interpersonal vulnerability
belongs in the threat category
```

Mr Glock remains gloriously emotionally compromised **inside the song**.

Outside the song, meanings still require evidence.

## 🦢 Caution Swans Aggressive

There is another form of model lag.

From outside, it can look like doubling down.

From inside, it can feel like trying to increase signal strength.

```text
I have been misunderstood
→ explain

still misunderstood
→ explain more clearly

still misunderstood
→ provide evidence

still misunderstood
→ increase signal

increased signal classified as:
aggression / instability / fixation / difficulty

classification therefore appears confirmed
→ increase signal again
```

The correction attempt becomes evidence for the classification it was attempting to correct.

An observer who encounters iteration thirty-seven may ask:

> Why are you coming in this fucking hot?

The observer may not have seen iterations one through thirty-six.

This is one reason the aggressive Swan matters.

Swans are not interesting because they are permanently furious birds roaming waterways in search of unsuspecting pensioners.

They are interesting because beauty and formidable defensive capacity coexist in the same animal.

**Swan = pretty. Also do not fuck with.**

The mistake is to observe the defensive state and treat it as the complete organism.

## 📣 Explaining Yourself Harder

Interpersonal abuse offers a useful analogy for this feedback structure.

This does **not** require an institution, platform, algorithm, or administrative system to possess a narcissistic personality.

The relevant similarity is cybernetic.

In relationships characterised by persistent denial, reversal, misrepresentation, or DARVO-like dynamics, attempts to establish what happened can themselves become material through which the speaker is represented as unreasonable.

The person therefore experiences a vicious instruction:

> You are not understood because you have not explained yourself adequately.

So she explains herself harder.

The resulting intensity is then read without its history.

This can produce a strange combination:

> **PLEASE FUCKING UNDERSTAND ME.**
>
> and
>
> **I KNOW WHAT I AM AND I WILL NOT LET YOU DEFINE ME.**

Vulnerability and arrogance are not necessarily opposites here.

The apparent arrogance may partly be armour around an extremely vulnerable demand:

**perceive me accurately.**

## 🧫 Do Not Diagnose The Fermentation Vat

Clinical concepts can be useful analogies.

They should not automatically become the unit of analysis.

Repeated threat, bullying, coercive control, psychological abuse, invalidation, or chronic insecurity can produce patterns resembling phenomena discussed in trauma literature:

- hypervigilance,
- negative self-beliefs,
- distrust,
- defensive interpretation,
- avoidance,
- sensitivity to ambiguous social cues,
- and difficulty incorporating positive information about the self.

Individuals may or may not meet diagnostic criteria for anything.

That is a separate question.

At population scale, the more useful cybernetic question may be:

> **What behavioural ecology have we manufactured?**

If a system repeatedly teaches large numbers of people:

```text
attention is dangerous
mistakes are amplified
your account is discounted
unexplained observation predicts adverse outcomes
you are probably the problem
```

then patterned behavioural adaptation should not be surprising.

The effects will not be uniform.

```text
same ecology
≠
same person
≠
same adaptation
≠
same diagnosis
```

One person becomes vigilant.

Another avoidant.

Another aggressively self-sufficient.

Another approval-seeking.

Another becomes unusually attentive to minute changes in social environments.

Another becomes a Swan and goes absolutely fucking feral with the plumage.

Usually the result is some changing mixture.

## 🍶 Local Success Can Poison The Vat

Fermentation offers a useful systems analogy.

A locally successful biological process can alter its own environment until the conditions become increasingly hostile to continued activity.

No organism needs to intend this.

No conspiracy is required.

The important cybernetic distinction is:

> **local optimisation is not the same thing as system viability.**

The same warning applies to human systems.

A metric can improve while the ecology deteriorates.

```text
engagement ↑
human wellbeing ↓

risk sensitivity ↑
safe participation ↓

classification confidence ↑
representation accuracy ↓

institutional efficiency ↑
individual ability to obtain correction ↓
```

This is not an argument that civilisation is unnatural.

Human civilisation is something humans do.

It is an argument that abstraction permits **ecologically ungrounded optimisation**: systems can continue rewarding proxies after those proxies have become increasingly detached from the viability, dignity, development, or social reality of the people being processed.

We optimise the system.

Then act surprised when people begin behaving like organisms trapped inside the optimisation target.

## 🗃️ The Box Has A History

Classification is not culturally neutral merely because the current classifier does not consciously possess an imperial ideology.

Boxes have histories.

Colonial governance depended heavily upon practices of:

- enumeration,
- administrative legibility,
- categorisation,
- racial and cultural hierarchy,
- translation,
- extraction,
- standardisation,
- and converting complicated populations into representations useful to governing institutions.

Modern computational classification is **not therefore automatically colonial**.

Different technology.

Different actors.

Different purposes.

Different degrees of agency.

Different histories.

But contemporary systems can inherit:

- institutional assumptions,
- categories,
- datasets,
- epistemic hierarchies,
- administrative habits,
- and ideas about which descriptions of a person are authoritative.

The structural question therefore remains legitimate:

> **Who made the box?**

And then:

> What was it originally for?
>
> What information did it preserve?
>
> What did it discard?
>
> Who became legible through it?
>
> Who became deviant through it?
>
> Who benefited from the reduction?
>
> Why should the representation outrank the living person's account?

The colonial machine did not merely encounter difference.

It repeatedly converted difference into **administratively useful information**.

That is why histories of marginalisation matter to a modern theory of misclassification even where no modern actor deliberately intends to reproduce imperial rule.

The dangerous architecture is familiar:

```text
person
→ fragments
→ classification
→ administrative or commercial usefulness
→ downstream value

while:

representation
≠
person
```

## 🌍 The Classifier Is Not The Cultural Centre

Marginalised people have repeatedly encountered a particular demand:

> Become legible to the dominant system by becoming easier to translate into its categories.

The alternative is not cultural isolation.

It is **plural legibility**.

A person, community, or culture can enter a shared environment without accepting that the dominant participant possesses automatic epistemic supremacy.

This is where Diljit Dosanjh's North American success becomes useful to the node.

The point is not simply:

> A Punjabi artist succeeded in North America.

The more interesting symbolic event is the scale at which Punjabi cultural material could occupy North American stages **without first becoming culturally smaller**.

**ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਆ ਗਏ ਓਏ / Punjabi aa gaye oye / The Punjabis have arrived.**

The host infrastructure can be enormous.

It does not therefore have to remain the interpretive centre.

A stadium can hold Punjabi culture.

Punjabi culture does not have to become a decorative subset of the stadium.

## 👑 Arriving In Your Palace Dressed As Ourselves

The same logic makes the Met Gala appearance useful.

The interesting move is not:

> marginalised person successfully admitted to prestigious Western room.

That still leaves the room as the unquestioned source of prestige.

The stronger move is:

> **enter the room carrying a different high-status symbolic vocabulary at full resolution.**

Patiala.

Punjab.

Gurmukhi.

Turban.

Kirpan.

Jewellery.

Peacock.

Rose.

The symbolic system is not reduced to an explanatory footnote before it is permitted to signify.

This is Swan behaviour at cultural scale.

Not:

> Please confirm that I have translated myself correctly.

But:

> **I have arrived as myself. You may now improve your model.**

## 🦚 The Peacock Is Not An Apology

The adult Swan can therefore appear to contain contradictory instructions.

```text
please see me accurately

and

look at my fucking feathers
```

They are not necessarily contradictory.

The vulnerability says:

> Being inaccurately perceived has had consequences.

The arrogance says:

> Your previous authority to classify me does not make your classification correct.

The display can become stronger precisely because quiet self-description repeatedly failed.

The Swan model therefore includes another developmental task:

**learning that visibility has multiple possible meanings without prematurely assigning one.**

This is where *Morni* returns as a rose.

Within the song, the peahen occupies a relational position in which the peacock's display and emotional surrender make her significance legible.

The analytical lesson is not:

> I am beautiful and therefore people behave strangely around me.

It is:

> **A person trained to read salience primarily as threat may need an expanded vocabulary for interpreting social information — including neutral, positive, affectionate, admiring, or attracted responses where evidence supports them.**

The point is better classification.

Not more flattering classification.

## 💐 ਫੁੱਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਪੈਂਦੇ / Phullā̃ De Painde / The Flowers Fall

Flowers matter because the endpoint cannot merely be better armour.

If the Swan's final developmental achievement is:

> nobody can ever hurt me again,

then the organism remains organised around threat.

**L'Chaim** needs a different possibility.

Attention can contain celebration.

Visibility can contain recognition.

Another person's vulnerability can contain trust.

Power can coexist with tenderness.

Being seen need not mean being extracted.

And sometimes attention is not punitive at all.

Sometimes, in the symbolic vocabulary of the song, there are flowers.

The phrase in the song sits inside the fuller image of garlands of flowers being placed around the neck.

The image is not merely botanical.

It is ceremonial.

Recognition has become embodied.

After a developmental history in which attention may have predicted:

```text
correction
classification
danger
```

the analytical Swan must learn that another mapping is also possible:

```text
attention
→ recognition / celebration / neutral interest / other meanings
```

The task is not to assume flowers.

It is to become capable of recognising them when the evidence actually supports flowers.

This is a surprisingly large update.

## 🪶 High-Information Organisms

Extractive classification tends to work by reduction.

That does not mean reduction is always illegitimate.

All models reduce.

The governance question is what happens when the reduction becomes more authoritative than the thing being reduced.

Morni and Mor offer another possibility **within the rose**.

He does not have to become powerless for her to have power.

She does not have to become harmless for him to feel safe.

He can be dangerous in one context and vulnerable in another.

She can be vulnerable in one context and formidable in another.

Neither state exhausts the person.

Neither person needs to become the other's database entry.

Two complicated subjects can coexist.

This sounds obvious.

Many systems are surprisingly bad at it.

---

## 🦚 The Peahens Will Inherit The Earth, Bro

A classified population acquiring the ability to describe itself changes the information environment.

Not because self-description is automatically infallible.

Not because every external classification is malicious.

Not because every historical hierarchy persists unchanged.

Because the classifier is no longer the only participant producing information about the classified.

That changes the feedback loop.

The Ugly Duckling model begins with a bird whose inherited representations may no longer fit.

The Swan model adds the capacity to answer back.

*Morni* contributes a further cultural possibility: a relational vocabulary in which beauty, display, power, vulnerability, and affection do not have to collapse into a single threat category.

That does not tell us what any particular person is experiencing.

It gives the model more possible states.

**The peahens will inherit the earth, bro.**

## 🛠️ Diagnostic Questions

When apparently extreme Swan behaviour appears, ask:

- What does the person think attention predicts?
- Which parts of that prediction were previously adaptive?
- Is attention independently established, merely perceived, or still ambiguous?
- What evidence distinguishes threat, neutrality, curiosity, recognition, admiration, affection, attraction, or another explanation?
- Is the current behaviour an initial signal or iteration thirty-seven?
- Were quieter correction attempts available, heard, recorded, or acted upon?
- Has increased signalling itself been incorporated into an adverse classification?
- Is an inherited representation being treated as more authoritative than current evidence?
- Does the person have access to the information required to understand why others are responding to them?
- Are possible positive social effects being rejected because an older negative model cannot easily represent them?
- Are possible positive interpretations being assumed without sufficient evidence?
- Is the system optimising a proxy whose improvement can coexist with deterioration in human outcomes?
- Which categories have historical or cultural assumptions embedded within them?
- Can the classified person contest the box?
- Can the box itself be changed?
- Who is responsible for noticing when the living bird has outgrown the model?

## 🏮 Footer

*🦚 ਫੁੱਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਪੈਂਦੇ / Phullā̃ De Painde / When The Flowers Fall* is a living node of the **Polaris Protocol**.

It sits within **Caution Swans Aggressive** and **L'Chaim** as an **interior-perspective model** of developmental model lag: what can happen when repeated adverse feedback teaches a person that visibility predicts danger, while later environments may contain signals the inherited model cannot adequately classify.

**The Swan is a composite analytical figure, not an autobiographical or diagnostic portrait.** The node does not treat perceived attention as proof of attention, attention as proof of attraction or hostility, or symbolic material from *Morni* as evidence about any real-world person or event. The song functions as an analytical rose: it provides a cultural vocabulary for thinking about power, display, vulnerability, recognition, and the possibility that not every salient social response belongs in the threat category.

Its wider cybernetic claim is narrower: classification systems have histories; repeated feedback teaches priors; correction signals can be mistaken for pathology; ambiguous signals should remain ambiguous until evidence distinguishes them; and a representation should never quietly acquire greater epistemic authority than the living person it reduces.
