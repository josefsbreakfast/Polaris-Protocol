🌖_6_Learning_The_Skies/🧩_Accidental_Autistic_Diaspora_Dataset/

Core question: Can a system classify people according to behaviours relevant to commerce, politics, propaganda or security and accidentally construct a population that substantially proxies neurodivergence or another protected characteristic?

Things to include

* 🧩 The accidental category
    * The system doesn’t need AUTISTIC = TRUE.
    * It can select persistence, argumentation, anomaly detection, information-seeking, network position, political dissent, etc.
    * These behaviours have multiple possible causes.
    * The resulting cluster may nevertheless correlate strongly with neurodivergence.
    * Intentional targeting ≠ proxy targeting ≠ disparate outcome.
* 🧠 Autism without turning autism into a metaphor
    * Autism is a heterogeneous neurodevelopmental condition.
    * “Autistic” must not become shorthand for clever, pedantic, argumentative, obsessive or politically resistant.
    * Autistic people can have radically different politics, abilities and support needs.
    * Undiagnosed / suspected / trait-overlap populations need to remain distinct from diagnosed autistic populations.
* ✡️ Jewish culture and behavioural norms
    * Research existing literature around Jewish intellectual/religious practice and cognition.
    * Rabbinic/Talmudic traditions of argument, textual interrogation and competing interpretation.
    * Which behaviours are socially legible or prestigious within particular Jewish environments?
    * Huge warning against “Jews are autistic”.
    * Jewish cultures are themselves heterogeneous.
    * The Rabbi’s Brain as one source to interrogate critically rather than a foundational authority.
* 🪞 Culture changes the meaning of the observation
    * Same behaviour, different cultural interpretation.
    * perseveration somewhere may become David has Opinions somewhere else.
    * Cultural normalisation might obscure disability.
    * Conversely, outsiders might pathologise culturally ordinary behaviour.
    * Neither interpretation establishes neurotype.
* 👩 Gender
    * Historic diagnostic models and recognition of autism in women/girls.
    * Gendered interpretation of argument, directness, intensity, expertise, etc.
    * Interaction between Jewish cultural norms and gender norms.
    * Could behavioural selection accidentally produce a population disproportionately containing neurodivergent Jewish women?
* 🕸️ Diaspora as network
    * Diasporas aren’t clean geographic populations.
    * Family, religious, cultural, institutional, linguistic, professional and digital connections.
    * Consequently, anyone trying to model a diaspora may rely heavily on relational/behavioural information.
    * Network centrality ≠ political authority.
    * Dense minority networks ≠ conspiratorial power.
* 📊 Commercial population segmentation
    * Oracle and other data brokers.
    * Audience construction.
    * Behavioural data.
    * Profile enrichment.
    * Sensitive-attribute inference.
    * What happens after commercial data crosses into another system?
* 🔗 Dataset joins
    * Commercial dataset → platform information → government information → intelligence holdings.
    * Each actor may know something different.
    * Nobody needs to possess the eventual classification at the beginning.
    * The joins can create a new informational object.
* 🇮🇱 Israeli/diaspora case study
    * Keep documented facts separate from hypotheses.
    * Public diplomacy and diaspora engagement.
    * Social-media monitoring.
    * Narrative management during wartime.
    * What populations are actually identified?
    * What data providers/vendors are involved?
    * What can be established about Israeli government bodies versus IDF/intelligence/Unit 8200?
    * Do not collapse these into one actor.
* 🎯 Dissent and narrative resistance
    * Distinguish persuadability from network consequence.
    * Someone disagreeing privately is different from:
        anomaly → research → discussion → collective verification → alternative account.
    * Political bloc versus epistemic network.
    * What would an information-management system actually care about?
* ⚖️ Discrimination
    * Protected-characteristic law in relevant jurisdictions.
    * Direct versus indirect discrimination.
    * Disability discrimination.
    * Proxy variables.
    * Foreseeability versus intention.
    * What obligations arise once disproportion becomes known?
    * Don’t decide legal liability before establishing system architecture and jurisdiction.
* 🔎 Transparency and evidence
    * What would distinguish:
        * deliberate identity targeting;
        * deliberate proxy targeting;
        * foreseeable overlap;
        * overlap discovered downstream;
        * genuinely accidental selection?
    * Procurement.
    * Contracts.
    * DPIAs/privacy assessments.
    * Taxonomies.
    * schemas.
    * vendor/subprocessor lists.
    * litigation/disclosure.
    * Parliamentary/Knesset material.
    * company documentation.
* 🚨 Antisemitism guardrail
    * Never reason:
        Jewish people in network → Jewish conspiracy.
    * Actors, institutions, relationships, evidence, actions.
    * Minority networking is not unique to Jews.
    * Jewish historical marginalisation neither establishes contemporary powerlessness nor collective power.
    * Avoid both antisemitic and philo-Semitic exceptionalism.
* 🌍 Comparative cases
    * Don’t let Israel become the supposedly uniquely sinister example.
    * Advertising.
    * policing.
    * counter-extremism.
    * education.
    * recruitment.
    * fraud detection.
    * insurance/finance.
    * healthcare.
    * political campaigning.
    * other diasporas.

Central proposition

A classifier does not need to know what you are in order to discriminate against people who resemble you in the dimensions it can observe.
