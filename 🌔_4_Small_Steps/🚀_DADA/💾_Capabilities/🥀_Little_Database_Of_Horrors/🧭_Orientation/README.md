## 🛰️ Orientation

The Little Database of Horrors begins with a modest administrative promise:

> Understand the community. Identify risk. Coordinate help.

The danger begins when the system starts asking:

> Who may safely know whom?

A database becomes politically dangerous when it stops merely recording people and begins assessing what those people may become capable of together.

The relevant object is no longer only the individual.

It is:

- the relationship;
- the introduction;
- the bridge between communities;
- the route to protection;
- the coalition that may form;
- the practical capacity that trust creates.

This folder introduces the capability model, its benign appeal, its political stakes, and the evidentiary rules required to investigate it responsibly.

It does not claim that one complete hidden system has already been proven.

It asks whether existing technologies, institutions, data routes, and coercive practices could converge into systems that classify, permit, obstruct, or redirect consequential relationships.

---

## 🥀 Compact Definition

The **Little Database of Horrors** is a capability model in which:

- identity data;
- social graphs;
- behavioural scoring;
- administrative intelligence;
- platform systems;
- institutional gatekeeping;
- embassy and consular routes;
- coercive leverage;

may converge to assess and shape the relationships through which people gain:

- trust;
- protection;
- legitimacy;
- resources;
- access;
- evidence;
- collective agency.

Its danger lies in the shift from:

> seeing the network

to:

> scoring the network

to:

> pruning the network.

The dangerous step is not merely recording relationships.

It is treating relationships as objects to be permitted, obstructed, or redirected.

The wider Little Database of Horrors cluster examines:

- data sources;
- technical capabilities;
- institutional pathways;
- regime and state practices;
- signs of system strain;

It helps prevent several predictable errors:

- treating technical capability as proof of use;
- interpreting every social difficulty as deliberate interference;
- missing the political importance of relationships;
- dismissing foreseeable abuse because the full architecture is not public;
- replacing careful investigation with weak accusation;
- waiting for catastrophic proof before considering safeguards.

This folder is therefore both:

- an entry point;
- an evidentiary boundary;
- a reader-safety mechanism;
- a route into the wider capability study.

## 🌱 How The Plant Grows

The capability may develop through a progression.

### 1. Collection

Institutions gather information about:

- people;
- families;
- organisations;
- travel;
- communications;
- political activity;
- support needs.

### 2. Integration

Separate records and systems are connected.

### 3. Mapping

Social and institutional relationships become visible.

### 4. Interpretation

Connections receive political or administrative meaning.

### 5. Scoring

People and relationships are ranked by risk, influence, or utility.

### 6. Filtering

Contacts are treated as:

- safe;
- tolerable;
- useful;
- dangerous.

### 7. Shaping

The system alters:

- visibility;
- access;
- timing;
- introductions;
- opportunity;
- reputation.

### 8. Coercion

Pressure is applied through:

- law;
- finance;
- immigration;
- family;
- employment;
- reputation;
- physical force.

No single stage proves the next.

The point of the model is to identify where a seemingly ordinary administrative system begins crossing into relationship governance.

## 🪴 Feed Me Data

The *Little Shop of Horrors* metaphor describes a system that grows through failure.

The project begins small.

The model produces uncertainty.

Officials ask for more data.

The model produces errors.

Officials add more categories.

People behave unpredictably.

Officials interpret unpredictability as concealment.

The community reacts to surveillance or pressure.

The system treats that reaction as confirmation.

> Feed me data.

The plant’s appetite becomes self-validating.

But the model’s hunger is not a validation metric.

More intimate data do not necessarily make human relationships predictable.

They may simply make the system more intrusive, more confident, and more dangerous.

## 🔭 Core Evidentiary Rule

> Capability is not conduct.  
> Motive is not proof.  
> Pattern is not attribution.  
> A vivid metaphor is not evidence.

The cluster distinguishes between:

- **possible** — technically or administratively feasible;
- **plausible** — consistent with known capabilities and incentives;
- **probable** — better supported than competing explanations;
- **established** — evidenced strongly enough to state as fact.

Later case-study nodes may examine documented practices.

Each case must still be judged on its own evidence.

The capability model provides questions.

It does not supply automatic answers.

---

## 🔬 Core Investigative Rule

> Do not begin by trying to prove the whole plant. Begin with the first visible root.

Start with:

- the immediate event;
- the decision that produced it;
- the information relied upon;
- the rule applied;
- the route the information travelled;
- the threshold that was crossed;
- the evidence that would disprove the hypothesis.

Small proof can produce real protection.

One corrected record may matter.

One exposed data-sharing route may matter.

One unreviewable score may matter.

One identified gatekeeper may matter.

The wider architecture becomes credible only when the smaller mechanisms hold.

## 🧭 Rival Explanations

An apparent relationship-management pattern may also arise from:

- platform recommendation;
- homophily;
- class sorting;
- ordinary prejudice;
- community factionalism;
- professional gatekeeping;
- institutional cowardice;
- poor records;
- resource shortages;
- coincidence;
- several uncoordinated systems.

Ordinary explanations should be tested, not assumed.

Hidden coordination should be tested, not presumed.

A large pattern may reflect:

- one controller;
- several shared systems;
- copied policy;
- common incentives;
- no coordination at all.

The evidence must distinguish between them.

## 🚫 No Amateur Counter-Intelligence

This cluster is not permission to conduct amateur agent-hunting.

Do not:

- create loyalty tests;
- publicly name people from weak patterns;
- bait suspected actors;
- conduct covert confrontations;
- unlawfully access accounts or devices;
- pressure vulnerable people to participate;
- expose relatives;
- cut people off because they resemble a hypothetical profile;
- turn social difficulty into proof of state alignment.

A theory designed to explain wedges must not become a machine for creating them.

The appropriate responses are:

- evidence preservation;
- direct communication;
- professional investigation;
- legal advice;
- technical review;
- institutional safeguards;
- community protection.

## ⚠️ What This Cluster Is Not

This cluster is not:

- proof of one global database;
- a claim that every state uses the same system;
- an explanation for every failed friendship, romance, introduction, or referral;
- a list of suspected agents;
- a method for testing loyalty;
- an argument against all safeguarding;
- an argument against all data collection;
- an argument against institutional coordination;
- a substitute for case-specific evidence;
- a claim that technical systems can perfectly predict human relationships.

The model is a reason to investigate.

It is not permission to accuse.

- surveillance;

- institutional betrayal;
- opaque gatekeeping;
- repeated unexplained obstruction.

Readers are encouraged to separate:

- documented events;

- fears;
